The '''2021 Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2021 to elect members of [[Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England. This was on the same day as other local elections.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elections 2021 |url=https://www.dudley.gov.uk/council-community/elections/elections-2021/ |website=Dudley.gov.uk |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220703152938/https://www.dudley.gov.uk/council-community/elections/elections-2021/ |archive-date=3 July 2022 |url-status=dead |access-date=29 April 2021 }}</ref> One-third of the seats were up for election, with two wards (Belle Vale and Kingswinford North & Wall Heath) electing two councillors.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Thandi |first1=Gurdip |title=Who is standing for local election 2021 in Dudley? |url=https://www.birminghammail.co.uk/black-country/who-standing-local-election-2021-20356261 |publisher=Birmingham Mail |date=9 April 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241215001354/https://www.birminghammail.co.uk/black-country/who-standing-local-election-2021-20356261 |archive-date=15 December 2024 |access-date=29 April 2021 |url-status=live }}</ref>

Two seats were contested here rather than the ward's usual one: the seat due for ordinary election in 2020 was not filled that year because the COVID-19 pandemic led to the nationwide postponement of the May 2020 English local elections, so it was instead contested alongside the ward's regular 2021 seat. The 2020-cycle seat also covered the vacancy left by Jake Cooper, who had won the seat from Labour in 2018 and resigned before 2021.<ref>{{cite web |title=Local Elections Archive Project: Dudley 2021 |url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2021/59/ |website=Local Elections Archive Project |access-date=3 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Election Summary: Belle Vale, Dudley, 6 May 2021 |url=https://elections.democracyclub.org.uk/elections/local.dudley.belle-vale.2021-05-06/ |website=Democracy Club |access-date=3 August 2026}}</ref>
