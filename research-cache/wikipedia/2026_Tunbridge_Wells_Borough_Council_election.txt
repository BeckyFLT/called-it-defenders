The '''2026 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' was held on 7 May 2026, alongside the [[2026 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections across the United Kingdom]] being held on the same day, to elect 15 of 39 members of [[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]] in [[Kent]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Local election results|url=https://tunbridgewells.gov.uk/news/2026/may/local-election-results|website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council|date=2026-05-08|access-date=2 August 2026|language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Election results by party, 7 May 2026|url=https://democracy.tunbridgewells.gov.uk/mgElectionResults.aspx?ID=31&RPID=0|website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council|access-date=2 August 2026|language=en-GB}}</ref>

The council remained under [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] majority control.<ref name=Honey&Harris/><ref name=Keohan/>

Due to ongoing [[Upcoming structural changes to local government in England|local government reorganisation]], this will be the final election to Tunbridge Wells Borough Council before it is abolished and replaced by the new [[unitary authority]] for West Kent. Elections to the shadow authority are due to take place on 6 May 2027, with the new council assuming its full responsibilities from April 2028.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Decision made on the new local government structure for Kent and Medway|url=https://www.medway.gov.uk/news/article/2110/decision_made_on_the_new_local_government_structure_for_kent_and_medway|website=Medway Council|date=2026-07-16|access-date=2 August 2026|language=en-GB}}</ref>
