The '''2022 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' was held on 5 May 2022 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire District Council]] in England.<ref>{{cite web
 |url= https://www.huntingdonshire.gov.uk/elections/upcoming-elections/
 |title= Upcoming Elections
 |work= Huntingdonshire District Council
 |access-date= 28 April 2022
 }}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2022 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].

The elections saw two councillors elected unopposed,<ref>{{cite web
 |url= https://www.cambridge-news.co.uk/news/local-news/first-councillors-elected-huntingdonshire-elections-23666980
 |title= First councillors elected in Huntingdonshire elections as no challenge offered
 |work= Cambridgeshire Live
 |date= 13 April 2022
 |access-date= 28 April 2022
 }}</ref> the first uncontested elections in the district since 1988.

==Summary==

===Background===

The Conservative Party had been in control of the District Council since 1976. The Conservatives lost eight of their councillors, including the leader of the council, Ryan Fuller. As such the council after the election is under [[no overall control]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Statement on Huntingdonshire District Council election results 2022|date = 6 May 2022| work=[[Huntingdonshire District Council]]
|access-date=8 May 2022|url= https://www.huntingdonshire.gov.uk/news/statement-on-huntingdonshire-district-council-election-results-2022/}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/local-council/local-elections-2022-live-updates-huntingdonshire-8945544|work= The Hunts Post | title=Local Elections 2022: live updates from Huntingdonshire|date=6 May 2022|access-date=8 May 2022}}</ref> A coalition of the Liberal Democrats, Labour, Green and Independent councillors subsequently took control of the council, with Sarah Conboy, the leader of the council's Liberal Democrat group, being appointed leader of the council.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Brown |first1=Hannah |title=New Huntingdonshire leader discusses aims after historic power change and is 'under no illusion scale of task ahead' |url=https://www.cambridge-news.co.uk/news/cambridge-news/new-huntingdonshire-leader-discusses-aims-24003555 |access-date=24 May 2022 |work=[[Cambridge News]] |date=19 May 2022}}</ref>
