[[File:Carmarthenshire UK ward map 2004.svg|thumb|Results of the 2004 Carmarthenshire County Council election]]
The third election to [[Carmarthenshire County Council]] in [[Wales]] was held in May 2004. It was preceded by the [[1999 Carmarthenshire County Council election|1999 election]] and followed by the [[2008 Carmarthenshire County Council election|2008 election]]. As in previous elections, the Independent councillors had the largest number of seats. This resulted in a coalition between Independent and Labour Councillors for the next four years.
