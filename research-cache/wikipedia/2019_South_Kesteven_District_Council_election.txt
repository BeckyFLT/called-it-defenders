The '''2019 South Kesteven District Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[South Kesteven]] District Council. This was on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]]. The entire council (56 seats) was up for election. The incumbent [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] lost 5 seats and [[UK Independence Party|UKIP]] lost 1, while [[Independent politician|independents]] gained 5 and the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] gained 2, though the Conservatives, led by Matthew Lee, retained a comfortable majority on the council.<ref>{{cite web |title=South Kesteven District Council |url=https://democraticdashboard.com/council/south-kesteven-district-council |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170308100013/http://democraticdashboard.com/council/south-kesteven-district-council |url-status=usurped |archive-date=8 March 2017 |website=Democratic Dashboard |access-date=24 December 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=SKDC Elections: Conservatives maintain control on South Kesteven District Council although it's a close call for leader Matthew Lee |url=https://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/skdc-elections-conservatives-maintain-control-on-south-kesteven-district-council-although-its-a-close-call-for-leader-matthew-lee-9069114/ |work=Grantham Journal |access-date=4 August 2026}}</ref> Two wards (Aveland and Dole Wood) did not hold a vote, as an equal number of candidates ran as seats available.
