The '''2010 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2010 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=change>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/election2010/council/html/3779.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-23 | publisher = BBC News | date=2009-04-19}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 42
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 6<ref name=local>{{cite news| url = https://www.theguardian.com/society/table/2010/may/07/local-elections | title = Local elections 2010 | access-date = 2010-11-24 | date = 2010-05-07 | work = The Guardian | location=London}}</ref>

==Election result==
The results saw the Conservatives stay in control of the council despite losing 2 seats.<ref name=casualties>{{cite web | url = http://www.thisiskent.co.uk/news/article-2152709-detail/article.html | archive-url = https://archive.today/20130505123338/http://www.thisiskent.co.uk/news/article-2152709-detail/article.html | url-status=dead | archive-date = 2013-05-05 | title = Tory casualties at borough elections | access-date = 2010-11-24 | date = 2010-05-14 | publisher = this is Kent }}</ref> The [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] gained seats in [[Benenden]] and [[Cranbrook, Kent|Cranbrook]] [[Wards of the United Kingdom|wards]] to hold 6 seats, compared to 42 for the Conservatives.<ref name=casualties/> Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] in the election was 68.59%.<ref name=results>{{cite web | url = http://www2.tunbridgewells.gov.uk/default.aspx?page=3445 | title = Tunbridge Wells Borough Council Election Results - 6 May 2010 | access-date = 2010-11-23 | publisher = Tunbridge Wells Borough Council }}{{Dead link|date=April 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
