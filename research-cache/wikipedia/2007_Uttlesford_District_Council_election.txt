The '''2007 Uttlesford District Council election''' took place on '''3 May 2007''' to elect members of [[Uttlesford District Council]] in [[Essex]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Uttlesford-1973-2011.pdf |title=Uttlesford District Council Elections 1973-2011 |access-date=31 December 2025 |work=[[Colin Rallings]] & [[Michael Thrasher]] |publisher=The Elections Centre, [[Plymouth University]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2007 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
