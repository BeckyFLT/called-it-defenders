{{Short description|Welsh local election}}
{{Use dmy dates|date=April 2022}}
The second elections to the [[Carmarthenshire County Council]] were held in May 1999.<ref name="WM 8-5-99 res">{{cite news|title=Results|newspaper=Western Mail|date=8 May 1999}}</ref> It was preceded by the [[1995 Carmarthenshire County Council election|1995 election]] and followed by the [[2004 Carmarthenshire County Council election|2004 election]]. They resulted in a coalition between Independent councillors and [[Plaid Cymru]] for the next five years.
