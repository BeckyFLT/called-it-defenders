North Somerset unitary council is elected every four years, with fifty [[councillor]]s to be elected. Since the first election to the unitary authority in 1995, the council has either been under [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative party]] control, or no party has held a majority. The Conservatives gained a majority at the [[2007 North Somerset Council election|2007 election]] and have retained control since then. At the 2015 election, the outcome was as follows:<ref>H. Pickstock, [http://www.bristolpost.co.uk/ELECTIONS-2015-Conservatives-retain-control-North/story-26466376-detail/story.html#ixzz47ALf5Xyp Conservatives retain control of North Somerset] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150718220956/http://www.bristolpost.co.uk/ELECTIONS-2015-Conservatives-retain-control-North/story-26466376-detail/story.html |date=18 July 2015 }} at bristolpost.co.uk dated 8 May 2015</ref>
