{{Short description|1991 UK local government election}}
The '''1991 East Devon District Council election''' took place on 2 May 1991 to elect members of [[East Devon District Council]] in England. This was on the same day as other [[1991 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]]. There were minor changes to the district boundaries for this election.

==New district boundaries==
[[File:The Devon (District Boundaries) Order 1987, Change Map.gif|thumb|]]
District boundary changes took place effective April 1988. No districts were added or created. All changes involved the city of [[Exeter]], which borders [[East Devon]] to the west. The areas transferred from Exeter to East Devon had a population of about nine persons; the areas transferred from East Devon to Exeter had an estimated population of 189.<ref>{{cite web |title=The Devon (District Boundaries) Order 1987 |url=https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1576/made |website=Legislation.gov.uk |access-date=22 May 2022}}</ref>
