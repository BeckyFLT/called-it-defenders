The '''2019 West Suffolk District Council election''' was held on 2 May 2019 and was the inaugural election of the new [[West Suffolk District Council]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Local Elections 2019 in the Anglia region |url=https://www.itv.com/news/anglia/story/2019-04-30/local-elections-2019-in-the-anglia-region/ |website=ITV News |access-date=2 January 2020 |language=en}}</ref> It was held concurrently with [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections across the United Kingdom]].

The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] won 36 of the 64 seats, giving it outright control of the new council.<ref name="WSC-2019-results"/>

* Exning councillor Simon Cole, elected as a Conservative in 2019, was sitting with the West Suffolk Independents group by July 2022.<ref>{{cite web |title=Your Councillors |url=https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |publisher=[[West Suffolk District Council]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220701103447/https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |archive-date=1 July 2022 |url-status=dead |access-date=24 July 2026}}</ref>
* Kentford and Moulton councillor Roger Dicker, elected for the West Suffolk Independents in 2019, was sitting as a non-grouped independent by July 2022.<ref>{{cite web |title=Your Councillors |url=https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |publisher=[[West Suffolk District Council]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220701103447/https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |archive-date=1 July 2022 |url-status=dead |access-date=24 July 2026}}</ref>
* Brandon West councillor David Palmer, elected for the West Suffolk Independents in 2019, was sitting as a Conservative by July 2022.<ref>{{cite web |title=Your Councillors |url=https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |publisher=[[West Suffolk District Council]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220701103447/https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |archive-date=1 July 2022 |url-status=dead |access-date=24 July 2026}}</ref>
* St. Olaves councillor Max Clarke, elected as Labour in 2019, was sitting as an independent by July 2022.<ref>{{cite web |title=Your Councillors |url=https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |publisher=[[West Suffolk District Council]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220701103447/https://democracy.westsuffolk.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=WARD |archive-date=1 July 2022 |url-status=dead |access-date=24 July 2026}}</ref>
