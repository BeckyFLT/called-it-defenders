The 2021 elections to [[Amber Valley|Amber Valley Borough Council]] were held on Wednesday 5th May 2021, 16 seats in the council were up for election. The Conservative Party retook control of the council from The Labour Party after losing control in 2019. These seats were due to have been contested in 2020, but the elections were delayed by a year due to the COVID-19 pandemic along with [[2021 United Kingdom local elections|other elections]].
