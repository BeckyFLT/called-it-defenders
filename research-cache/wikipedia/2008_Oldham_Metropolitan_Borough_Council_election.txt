[[File:Oldham UK local election 2008.svg|thumb|Results of the 2008 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] Council''' were held on 1 May 2008. One third of the council was up for election. The council remained in no overall control with the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] overtaking the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] as the largest party.

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 30
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 22
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 7
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1
