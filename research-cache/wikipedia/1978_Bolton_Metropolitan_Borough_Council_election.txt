{{Short description|1978 UK local government election}}
[[File:EnglandBolton.png|thumb|200px|The [[Metropolitan Borough of Bolton]] shown within England.]]
Elections to [[Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council]] were held in May 1978. The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] retained control of the council.

23 seats were contested, with 11 being won by the Conservative Party and 12 by the Labour Party.

After the election, the composition of the council was:

*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 45
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 23
*[[Liberal Party (UK)|Liberal Party]] 1
