The '''2012 Oldham Council election''' took place on 3 May 2012 to elect members of Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council in the North West of England.<ref name="BBC">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-17299200 |title=Local elections 2012 |access-date=2012-04-30 |work=BBC News}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2012 United Kingdom local elections]].

[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] retained control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was:

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 44
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 14
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 2
