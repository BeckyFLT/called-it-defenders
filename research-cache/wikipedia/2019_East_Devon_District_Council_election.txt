The '''2019 East Devon District Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[East Devon District Council]] in England. This was on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]]. The whole council was elected on new ward boundaries that increased the number of seats from 59 to 60.
