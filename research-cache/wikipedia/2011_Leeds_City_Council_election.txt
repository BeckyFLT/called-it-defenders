The '''2011 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 5 May 2011 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England. It was held on the same day as other [[2011 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across the UK]].

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's seats were up for election. The councillors subsequently elected replaced those elected when their individual seats were previously contested in [[2007 Leeds City Council election|2007]].

After forming a [[minority government|minority administration]] following the [[2010 Leeds City Council election|2010 election]], the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] regained overall control of the council for the first time since the council had been in [[no overall control]] in [[2004 Leeds City Council election|2004]]. Labour gained seven seats, including four from the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.leedsguide.co.uk/review/news/leeds-council-election-results-2011-labour-takes-control/18530 |title = News: Leeds Council Election Results 2011: Labour Takes Control {{!}} What's on in Leeds {{!}} Eating Out in Leeds {{!}} Listings {{!}} Leeds Guide Magazine  |access-date=2011-05-12 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110725073107/http://www.leedsguide.co.uk/review/news/leeds-council-election-results-2011-labour-takes-control/18530 |archive-date=25 July 2011 }}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{cite news| url = https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/election2011/council/html/da.stm | title = Elections 2011: England council elections - Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-22 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref><ref name="YEP">{{cite news| url = http://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/news/latest-news/top-stories/leeds-local-election-2011-results-in-full-1-3358114 | title =  Leeds local election 2011: Results in full | access-date = 2013-01-22 | newspaper = [[Yorkshire Evening Post]]}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{Cite web| url = http://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2011/67/ | title = Local Elections 2011: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-22 | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref>
