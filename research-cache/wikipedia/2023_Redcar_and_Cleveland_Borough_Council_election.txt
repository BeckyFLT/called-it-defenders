The '''2023 Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect members of [[Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council]] in England. This was on the same day as [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections in England]]. All 59 seats were up for election. Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council's elections are held every four years.

==Summary==
Prior to the election the council was under [[no overall control]], being run by a coalition of the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] and the "Independent Group", comprising 13 of the [[Independent politician|independents]]. The [[leader of the council]] was independent councillor Mary Lanigan.<ref name=before>{{cite web |title=Your councillors |url=https://rcbc.moderngov.co.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?bcr=1 |website=Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council |access-date=21 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230504182250/https://rcbc.moderngov.co.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?bcr=1 |archive-date=4 May 2023}}</ref>

The council remained under no overall control after the election. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] was the largest party, and both it and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] made gains, largely at the expense of the independents and Liberal Democrats. Mary Lanigan lost her seat.<ref>{{cite news |title=Censured Redcar and Cleveland council leader loses seat |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-tees-65500840 |access-date=24 May 2023 |work=BBC News |date=5 May 2023}}</ref><ref name=Arnold>{{cite news |last1=Arnold |first1=Stuart |title=Labour now largest party on Redcar and Cleveland Council as authority's former leader ousted |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/labour-now-largest-party-redcar-26856476 |access-date=24 May 2023 |work=Teesside Live |date=5 May 2023}}</ref>

A minority Labour administration formed after the election, with Labour group leader Alec Brown being appointed leader of the council at the subsequent annual council meeting on 25 May 2023.<ref>{{cite web |title=Council minutes, 25 May 2023 |url=https://rcbc.moderngov.co.uk/ieListDocuments.aspx?CId=161&MId=261 |website=Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council |access-date=21 February 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Arnold |first1=Stuart |title=New local authority leader wants rival councillors to work together to 'bridge differences' |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/new-local-authority-leader-wants-27003087 |access-date=21 February 2024 |work=Teesside Live |date=27 May 2023}}</ref>

==Post-election==

===Affiliation changes===
Vera Rider, elected as an independent in May 2023 (having previously been elected as a Conservative in 2019 but left the party in 2021), re-joined the Conservatives in November 2023.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Arnold |first1=Stuart |title=Vera's return as independent councillor rejoins Conservative group two years after quitting |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/veras-return-independent-councillor-rejoins-28129698 |access-date=22 February 2024 |work=Teesside Live |date=18 November 2023}}</ref>

Tristan Learoyd resigned from the Liberal Democrats in July 2024,<ref>{{cite news |last1=Arnold |first1=Stuart |title=Row continues as Lib Dem councillor quits local group after previous suspension |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/row-continues-lib-dem-councillor-29665923 |access-date=3 August 2024 |work=Teesside Live |date=3 August 2024}}</ref> following his suspension by the local party. Learoyd previously represented both Labour (2011) and Green (2014), as well as being part of the Independent Group during the 2011-2015 council, and became independent for the third time.

Following his election as [[Member of Parliament (United Kingdom)|Member of Parliament]] (MP) for [[Middlesbrough South and East Cleveland (UK Parliament constituency)|Middlesbrough South and East Cleveland]] in [[2024 United Kingdom general election|2024]], Luke Myer vacated his councillor seat in the Longbeck ward.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Arnold |first1=Stuart |title=Local by-election looms after newly elected MP quits as borough councillor |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/local-election-looms-after-newly-29641494 |access-date=6 September 2024 |work=Teesside Live |date=31 July 2024}}</ref> A subsequent election on September 5 saw the Conservative Party candidate Stephen Crane win the seat.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Arnold |first1=Stuart |title=Tory success as candidate tops poll in Longbeck ward beating Labour into second |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/tory-success-candidate-tops-poll-29882934 |access-date=6 September 2024 |work=Teesside Live |date=6 September 2024}}</ref>

In January 2025, Justin Thompson was suspended by the Conservative Party following comments around the [[Gaza war|Gaza War]]<ref>{{Cite web |last=Arnold |first=Stuart |date=2025-01-31 |title=Tory councillor suspended after comparing Middle East conflict to the Holocaust |url=https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/tory-councillor-suspended-party-after-30893915 |access-date=2025-02-21 |website=Teesside Live |language=en}}</ref>
