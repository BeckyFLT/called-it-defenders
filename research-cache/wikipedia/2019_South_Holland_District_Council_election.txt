The '''2019 [[South Holland, Lincolnshire|South Holland]] [[District council (England)|District Council]] [[election]]''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of the [[South Holland District Council]] in [[Elections in England|England]]. It was held on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
