The '''1998 Oldham Council election''' took place on 7 May 1998 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[Greater Manchester]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=local>{{cite news | title = Local Elections results | newspaper = The Times| page = 46 | date = 1998-05-09 }}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 36
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 23
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1<ref name=policy>{{cite news | title = Policy and politics: Local Elections: Analysis: Council poll results | newspaper = The Guardian | page = 16 | date = 1998-05-09 }}</ref>

==Campaign==
Before the election Labour ran the council with 35 [[councillor]]s as compared to 24 for the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]].<ref name=enemy>{{cite news | first = Peter | last = Hetherington | title = Local elections: Labour faces its 'real enemy' The collapse of Conservatism in south Lancashire is distressing its old adversary | newspaper = The Guardian | page = 8 | date = 1998-05-05 }}</ref> The election was mainly fought between these 2 parties with the Liberal Democrats campaigning on local issues under a slogan of "open, local, clean and green".<ref name=enemy/> They said that Labour were arrogant and complacent, while Labour attacked the Liberal Democrats for being irresponsible on public spending and opportunistic.<ref name=enemy/>

==Election result==
Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] in the election was 31%.<ref name=mbc>{{cite web | url = http://www.oldham.gov.uk/local_govt/councillors/results.htm | title = Oldham MBC Local Election Results 6th May 1999 | access-date = 2010-04-09 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20000615034125/http://www.oldham.gov.uk/local_govt/councillors/results.htm | archive-date = 15 June 2000 | publisher = Oldham Metropolitan Borough | url-status = dead | df = dmy-all }}</ref>
