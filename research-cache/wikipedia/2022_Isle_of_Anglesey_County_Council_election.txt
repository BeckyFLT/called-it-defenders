The '''2022 Isle of Anglesey County Council election''' took place on Thursday 5 May 2022 to elect all 35 members of [[Isle of Anglesey County Council]]. It was held on the same day as all other councils in Wales, as part of the [[2022 Welsh local elections]].

The election saw the number of councillors elected to Isle of Anglesey County Council increase, from 30 to 35, as part of boundary changes to the council carried out by the [[Local Democracy and Boundary Commission for Wales]].

[[Plaid Cymru]] gained the council from [[no overall control]], leading to the first administration formed of only political parties since the council's creation in 1996.

==Background==
At the previous election in 2017, no party won an overall majority. [[Plaid Cymru]] won 14 seats and fell just two shy of winning a majority. [[Independent (politician)|Independent]] candidates won 14 seats and the three remaining seats were won by [[Welsh Labour|Labour]] and the [[Welsh Liberal Democrats|Liberal Democrats]].<ref>{{cite news |author=Gareth Wyn-Williams |title=Local election results: No party wins overall control in Anglesey Council |url= https://www.dailypost.co.uk/news/local-election-results-anglesey-no-12993982 |work=North Wales Live |date=5 May 2017 |accessdate=13 April 2025}}</ref> As a result, the council was governed by a Plaid-independent coalition.<ref name="2022elec">{{Cite news |date=6 May 2022 |title=Welsh election results 2022: Plaid Cymru takes Anglesey and Gwynedd |language=en-GB |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-wales-61306727 |access-date=13 April 2025}}</ref>

Boundary reviews were carried out by the [[Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru|Local Democracy and Boundary Commission for Wales]] in advance of the election. As a result, the number of electoral divisions was increased from 11 to 14 and the number of councillors was increased from 30 to 35.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Anglesey boundary changes confirmed by Local Democracy and Boundary Commission for Wales |language=en-GB |work=Local Democracy and Boundary Commission for Wales |url=https://www.dbcc.gov.wales/reviews/11-20/anglesey-final-recommendations |access-date=13 April 2025}}</ref>

==Aftermath==
Plaid Cymru won control of the council after winning 21 of the 35 seats. Independent candidates took 10 seats, Labour won three and the Liberal Democrats won one seat. Anglesey was one of four councils won by Plaid Cymru across Wales. It was the first time since the council's creation in 1996 that a political party had taken control of the council.<ref name="2022elec"/>
