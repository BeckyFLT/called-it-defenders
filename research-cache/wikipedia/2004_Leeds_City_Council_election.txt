The '''2004 Leeds City Council election''' took place on 10 June 2004 to elect members of [[City of Leeds]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in England.

Following a full [[boundary review]] of Leeds' [[electoral wards]] by the [[Boundary Committee for England]], all of the council's 99 seats were contested on the new [[electoral ward|ward]] boundaries. The [[1980 Leeds City Council election|previous all-out election in Leeds was in 1980]].

The election saw the previously [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour-run]] council falling into [[no overall control]]. The [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] and [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] agreed to take control of the council in a formal [[Coalition government|coalition]], the first non-Labour administration in 24 years since 1980.<ref name="BBC">{{Cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2004/locals/html/3712.stm | title = Local elections | access-date = 2013-01-11 |publisher= BBC News}}</ref><ref name="results">{{cite web| url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/files/2004/week1/DB6E9FF685E3BD9280256EC3002A6374.pdf | title = Declaration of Result of Poll |publisher= leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 2013-01-11 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20101011031953/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/files/2004/week1/DB6E9FF685E3BD9280256EC3002A6374.pdf | archive-date=2010-10-11}}</ref>
