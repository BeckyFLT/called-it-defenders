The '''2018 Oldham Council election''' took place on 3 May 2018 to elect members of [[Oldham Council]] in England.<ref>{{cite web |title = Statement of Persons Nominated 9 April 2018 |url = https://www.oldham.gov.uk/downloads/file/4888/statement_of_persons_nominated_9_april_2018 |website = oldham.gov.uk |publisher = [[Oldham Council]] |access-date = 23 April 2018 |df = dmy-all |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20180423232338/https://www.oldham.gov.uk/downloads/file/4888/statement_of_persons_nominated_9_april_2018 |archive-date = 23 April 2018 |url-status = dead }}</ref> The election took place on the same day as other [[2018 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] in England. The election saw the majority [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] increase its number of seats by two. The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] also gained two seats, while the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] lost one seat. [[UKIP]] ceased to have representation on the council following this election.<ref name="BBCnews">{{cite news|title=Trafford lost by Conservatives|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-manchester-43999868|access-date=10 May 2018|work=BBC News|date=3 May 2018}}</ref> The election left Labour with 47 seats, the Liberal Democrats 8 and the Conservatives 4 with the remaining seat being held by an [[Independent (politician)|Independent]].<ref name="SaddleworthInd">{{cite news|last1=Belmore|first1=Aimee|title=Saddleworth elects three new councillors to represent area on Oldham Council|url=https://saddind.co.uk/saddleworth-elects-three-new-councillors-to-represent-area-on-oldham-council/|access-date=10 May 2018|work=Saddleworth Independent|date=5 May 2018}}</ref>
