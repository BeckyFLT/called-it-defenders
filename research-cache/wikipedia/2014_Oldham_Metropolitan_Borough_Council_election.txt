[[File:Oldham UK local election 2014.svg|thumb|Results of the 2014 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
The '''2014 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 22 May 2014 to elect members of [[Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="BBC">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/events/vote2014/england-council-election-results |title=England council results |access-date=23 May 2014 |work=BBC News}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2014 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].

[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] retained control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was:

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 45
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 10
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 2
*[[United Kingdom Independence Party|UKIP]] 2
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1
