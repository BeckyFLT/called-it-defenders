The '''2010 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2010 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Barnsley|Barnsley]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[South Yorkshire]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="change">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/election2010/council/html/3651.stm|title=Barnsley|work=BBC News|access-date=20 June 2011|date=19 April 2009}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 37
*Barnsley Independent Group 18
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 6
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 1
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1<ref name="results">{{cite web|url=http://www.barnsley.gov.uk/2010-local-elections|title=2010 Local Elections|publisher=Barnsley Council|access-date=20 June 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110724165858/http://www.barnsley.gov.uk/2010-local-elections|archive-date=24 July 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Background==
The [[2008 Barnsley Council election|previous election in 2008]] saw Labour's majority reduced to just one seat.<ref name="herald">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/uk_politics/election_2010/england/8658312.stm|title=Local elections may herald power shift in Yorkshire|date=3 May 2010|work=BBC News|access-date=21 June 2011|first=Len|last=Tingle}}</ref> The Barnsley Independent Group formed the main opposition with 22 seats compared to 32 for Labour, with [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]], a couple of other [[Independent (politician)|independents]] and 1 [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] making up the council.<ref name=herald/>

==Election result==
The results saw Labour increase their majority from 1 to 11 seats after making 5 gains from the Barnsley Independent Group.<ref name="grows">{{cite news|url=http://www.thestar.co.uk/news/local_elections_labour_majority_grows_in_barnsley_video_1_853945|title=Local elections: Labour majority grows in Barnsley|date=8 May 2010|work=[[Sheffield Star]]|access-date=21 June 2011|archive-date=4 October 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121004231919/http://www.thestar.co.uk/news/local_elections_labour_majority_grows_in_barnsley_video_1_853945|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="sheffield">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/politics/election_2010/england/8668366.stm|title=Lib Dems lose control of Sheffield council|date=7 May 2010|work=BBC News|access-date=21 June 2011}}</ref>
