{{main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}

Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council were held on 4 May 2000.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] party gained overall control of the council from [[no overall control]].

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 31
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 15
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1
