The '''2002 Tunbridge Wells Council election''' took place on 2 May 2002 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. The whole council was up for election with boundary changes since the [[2000 Tunbridge Wells Council election|last election in 2000]].<ref name=hold>{{cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/uk_politics/vote2000/locals/131.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref> The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] Party stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=london>{{cite news | title = London elections 2002 - part 4 | work = Evening Standard|location=London | page = 67 | date = 2002-05-03 }}</ref>

==Results==
The results saw the Conservatives increase their majority, while the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] group leader lost his seat.<ref name=surprises>{{cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/england/1965693.stm | title = Surprises for Labour in London | access-date = 2010-11-21 | date = 2002-05-03 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>
