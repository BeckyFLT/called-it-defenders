'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] Metropolitan Borough Council''' were held on 4 May 2006.  One third of the Council was up for election.

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] retained overall control of the Council.

After the election, the composition of the council was:

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 31
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 27
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 1
*[[Independent (politician)]] 1
