[[File:Newcastle-under-Lyme UK local election 2002.svg|thumb|Results of the 2002 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election]]
'''Elections to Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council''' were held on 2 May 2002.  The whole council was up for election, with boundary changes since the [[2000 Newcastle-under-Lyme Council election|last election]] in 2000 that increased the number of seats by four. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party lost overall control of the council to [[no overall control]].

Three seats in Thistleberry ward were vacant until a by-election in June.
