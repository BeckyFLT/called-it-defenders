The '''2021 North East Lincolnshire Council election''' took place on 6 May 2021 to elect members of [[North East Lincolnshire Council]] in England.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.nelincs.gov.uk/your-council/elections-and-voting/election-dates-and-results/north-east-lincolnshire-council-ward-elections-6-may-2021/ |title=North East Lincolnshire Council Ward Elections – 6 May 2021 - NELC &#124; NELC |access-date=2021-05-20 |archive-date=2021-05-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210507102721/https://www.nelincs.gov.uk/your-council/elections-and-voting/election-dates-and-results/north-east-lincolnshire-council-ward-elections-6-may-2021/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2021 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]]. 14 seats were up for election, and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] retained overall control.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/topics/cpr6g3e6z15t|title=North East Lincolnshire Council|access-date=3 July 2021|work=[[BBC News]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210703221134/https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/topics/cpr6g3e6z15t/north-east-lincolnshire-council|archive-date=3 July 2021|url-status=live}}</ref>

These elections were due to take place in 2020, but were postponed due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
