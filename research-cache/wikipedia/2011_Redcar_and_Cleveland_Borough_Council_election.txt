[[File:Redcar and Cleveland UK local election 2011 map.svg|thumb|300px|right|Map of the results of the 2011 Redcar and Cleveland council election. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] in red, [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] in yellow, [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] in blue and [[Independent (politician)|independents]] in grey.]]
The '''2011 Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council election''' took place on 5 May 2011 to elect members of [[Redcar and Cleveland]] [[Unitary authorities of England|Unitary]] Council in England. The whole council was up for election and the Labour Party gained overall control of the council from [[no overall control]].<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/election2011/council/html/ee.stm|title=Redcar & Cleveland|date=10 May 2011|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=10 September 2016}}</ref>
