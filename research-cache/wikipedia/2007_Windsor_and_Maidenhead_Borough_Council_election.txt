The '''2007 Windsor and Maidenhead Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[Royal Borough of Windsor and Maidenhead|Windsor and Maidenhead]] [[Unitary authority|Unitary]] Council in [[Berkshire]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] gained overall control of the council from the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]].<ref name=gain>{{cite news| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/vote2007/councils/html/me.stm | title = Windsor & Maidenhead Royal | access-date = 2010-11-28 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

==Election results==
The results saw the Conservatives gain control of the council from the Liberal Democrats after gaining 19 seats, 18 of them from the Liberal Democrats.<ref name=celebrate>{{cite news | title = Tories celebrate 'a pretty good night' in home counties | newspaper = Evening Standard|location=London | page = 1 | date = 2007-05-04 }}</ref> This meant the Conservatives held 36 seats, compared to 16 for the Liberal Democrats.<ref name=royal>{{cite news| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/england/berkshire/6622071.stm | title = Tories win Royal Borough control | access-date = 2010-11-28 | date = 2007-05-04 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref> Among the Liberal Democrats who were defeated was the leader of the council, [[Mary Rose Gliksten]], who was defeated in [[Castle Without]] [[Wards of the United Kingdom|ward]].<ref name=royal/> Meanwhile, the [[British National Party]], who were standing 4 candidates for the council for the first time, failed to win any seats, but won more votes than the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] in the wards they were contesting.<ref name=royal/><ref name=fears>{{cite news | title = Fears fade as extreme right fails to make significant gains BNP | newspaper = [[The Herald (Glasgow)|The Herald]] | page = 8 | date = 2007-05-05 }}</ref>
