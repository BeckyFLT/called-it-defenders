'''Elections to [[Amber Valley Borough Council]]''' in [[Derbyshire]], [[England]] took place on Thursday 5 May 2016. One third of the council seats were up for election and after the election the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] continued to hold overall control of the council.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2016-05-07 |title=Amber Valley election: Conservatives retain control |language=en-GB |publisher=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/election-2016-36236272 |access-date=2023-03-21}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
* [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 23
* [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 22
