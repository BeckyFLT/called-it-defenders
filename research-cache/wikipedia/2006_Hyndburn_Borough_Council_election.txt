}}Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council were held on 4 May 2006. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] party stayed in overall control of the council. Two seats had been lost to Labour in by elections in 2005 (Rishton and Overton) but both these were won back at this election. However the Conservatives did lose one seat to Labour, Immanuel Ward reducing their majority to 19. (18+2-1=19)

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 19
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 15
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1
