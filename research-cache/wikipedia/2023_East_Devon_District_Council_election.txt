The '''2023 East Devon District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect all 60 members of [[East Devon District Council]] in [[Devon]], England. This was on the same day as other [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across England]].

The council remained under [[no overall control]], being led by an alliance of the Liberal Democrats, Greens, Labour and some of the independent councillors.

==Overview==
Prior to the election the council was under no overall control, being led by the "Democratic Alliance Group" of 23 councillors, comprising the [[East Devon Alliance]], Liberal Democrats, Greens, and two of the independent councillors. The group was led by Paul Arnott of the East Devon Alliance. They were supported by some of the other independent groups and Labour. The 16 independent councillors who did not represent a registered political party were divided into five different groupings:
*The Independents (5)
*Independent Progressive Group (4)
*Cranbrook Voice (3)
*Part of Democratic Alliance Group (2)
*Not aligned to a group (2)
There was also one vacant seat on the council.<ref>{{cite web |title=Your councillors by political grouping |url=https://democracy.eastdevon.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=GROUPING&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |website=East Devon District Council |access-date=22 June 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230208025313/https://democracy.eastdevon.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=GROUPING&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |archive-date=8 February 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Kershaw |first1=Rob |title=East Devon Conservative quits group as he 'can't defend the indefensible' |url=https://www.devonlive.com/news/devon-news/east-devon-conservative-quits-group-8184495 |access-date=22 June 2023 |work=Devon Live |date=24 February 2023}}</ref>

The local party of the East Devon Alliance did not contest the 2023 election. Of the 12 councillors it had prior to the election, four stood as Liberal Democrats, five stood as independent candidates and three did not stand for re-election. The East Devon Alliance leader Paul Arnott, who was also leader of the council, was one of those who stood as a Liberal Democrat, having already joined the party at a national level in 2022.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Kershaw |first1=Rob |title=East Devon Independents to stand as Liberal Democrats in elections |url=https://www.devonlive.com/news/devon-news/east-devon-independents-stand-liberal-8281129 |access-date=22 June 2023 |work=Devon Live |date=23 March 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Arnott |first1=Paul |title=East Devon District Council leader joins national Lib Dems ahead of by-election |url=https://www.sidmouthherald.co.uk/news/local-council/20439535.east-devon-district-council-leader-joins-national-lib-dems-ahead-by-election/ |access-date=22 June 2023 |work=Sidmouth Herald |date=7 June 2022}}</ref>

The election saw the council remain under no overall control. The Conservative group leader, Philip Skinner, lost his seat. A Democratic Alliance Group formed again after the election, this time comprising the Liberal Democrats, Greens and nine of the independent councillors. Of the other independent councillors, nine formed the "Independent Group" and one sat with the single Liberal councillor as the "Independent Councillor Group". Paul Arnott was re-appointed leader of the council after the election, this time as a Liberal Democrat.<ref>{{cite web |title=Council meeting, 24 May 2023 |url=https://democracy.eastdevon.gov.uk/ieListDocuments.aspx?CId=151&MId=1997&Ver=4 |website=East Devon District Council |access-date=22 June 2023}}</ref>

==Changes 2023–2027==

===Affiliation changes===
*Alasdair Bruce, elected as a Conservative in 2023, left the party in February 2024 to sit as an independent in the Independent Group.<ref name=Bakewell/>

*Daniel Wilson, elected for Labour in 2023, left the party in April 2024 to sit as an ungrouped independent.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Boothroyd |first1=David |title=Lib Dems losing in the Highlands |url=https://www.localcouncils.co.uk/2024/04/lib-dems-losing-in-the-highlands/ |access-date=9 May 2024 |work=Local Councils |publisher=Thorncliffe |date=12 April 2024}}</ref>

*Bethany Collins, elected for Labour in 2023, left the party in May 2024 to sit as an independent in the Democratic Alliance Group.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Boothroyd |first1=David |title=Kingstanding Still |url=https://www.localcouncils.co.uk/2024/05/kingstanding-still/ |access-date=1 July 2024 |work=Local Councils |agency=Thorncliffe |date=31 May 2024}}</ref>
*Del Haggerty, elected as a Conservative in 2023, left the party in June 2024 to sit as an independent in the Democratic Alliance Group.<ref>{{cite web |title=Councillor Del Haggerty |url=https://democracy.eastdevon.gov.uk/mgUserInfo.aspx?UID=831 |website=East Devon District Council |access-date=1 July 2024}}</ref>
