A by-thirds [[Hyndburn Borough Council]] local election, was held on Thursday 2 May 2019.<ref name="HBC-Notice">{{Cite web|url=https://www.hyndburnbc.gov.uk/download-package/notice-of-election-2019-borough/ |title=Hyndburn Borough Council - 2019 Notice of Election Borough|access-date=1 April 2019 |work=The Hyndburn Borough Council}}</ref> Approximately one third of the local council's 35 seats fell up for election on that day.<ref name="TEC-Notice">{{Cite web |url=https://www.electoralcommission.org.uk/find-information-by-subject/elections-and-referendums/upcoming-elections-and-referendums |title=Upcoming elections & referendums |access-date=1 April 2019 |work=The Electoral Commission |archive-date=5 May 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180505140550/https://www.electoralcommission.org.uk/find-information-by-subject/elections-and-referendums/upcoming-elections-and-referendums |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Background==
Before the election [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] had a majority of 26 councillors, [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] had 9 councillors.<ref name="HBC-Councillors">{{Cite web|url=https://democracy.hyndburnbc.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=PARTY&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |title=Hyndburn Borough Council - Your councillors by political party |access-date=1 April 2019 |work=The Hyndburn Borough Council}}</ref>

==Council composition==
Prior to the election the composition of the council was:

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 26
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 9

Labour defended 9-seats and Conservatives defended 3-seats.

The local Labour and Conservative Party's both ran candidates in each of the 12 wards.

No candidates stood as potential Green or Independents councillors, in any Hyndburn ward, but UKIP candidates stood across only 2 wards.<ref name="HBC-Nominated">{{Cite web|url=https://www.hyndburnbc.gov.uk/download-package/statement-of-persons-nominated-hbc-2019/ |title=Hyndburn Borough Council - 2019 Statement of Persons Nominated |access-date=1 April 2019 |work=The Hyndburn Borough Council}}</ref>

==Local Election result==
The majority grouping of councillors as the headline result of the election, was unchanged; with Labour retaining an overall 26-seat majority, but with two seats switching hands, one from Labour to Conservative, one from Conservative to Labour.

After the election, the composition of the council's 35 seats was -

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 26
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 9
