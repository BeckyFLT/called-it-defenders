The '''2021 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 6 May 2021 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in [[England]]. It was held on the same day as other [[2021 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across England and the rest of the UK]], including the inaugural [[2021 West Yorkshire mayoral election|West Yorkshire mayoral election]].

The election was originally scheduled for 7 May 2020, alongside the later cancelled [[2021 England and Wales police and crime commissioner elections|2020 West Yorkshire Police and Crime Commissioner election]] and other [[2021 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across the UK]], but was delayed for a year due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Local elections postponed for a year over coronavirus |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-51876269 |access-date=13 March 2020 |work=BBC News |date=13 March 2020}}</ref>

As per the election cycle, both one third of the council's 99 seats and an additional [[casual vacancy]] in [[Roundhay (ward)|Roundhay]] ward were contested.<ref name="2 resign"/> Following the postponement of the original election in 2020, the incumbent councillors' terms have been extended for an additional year to their initial two-year term granted in [[2018 Leeds City Council election|2018]]. All councillors elected in 2021 will serve a one-off three-year term, instead of the usual four-year term, to retain their original term end date of 2024.

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] maintained their majority control of the council despite losing two seats. The closest contest saw Labour hold [[Temple Newsam (ward)|Temple Newsam]] ward by 12 votes ahead of the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]].
