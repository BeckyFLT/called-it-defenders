'''Elections to [[Gosport Borough Council]]''' took place on the 5th May 2022 as part of the [[2022 United Kingdom local elections]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=|title=2022 Borough of Gosport Elections|url=https://www.gosport.gov.uk/elections2022|access-date=28 April 2022|website=Gosport.gov.uk}}</ref>

The [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] gained control of the council from the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Local Election Results: Find out who won seats to your council {{!}} Sky News |url=https://election.news.sky.com/england-councils-41/gosport-29878 |access-date=2022-05-06 |website=election.news.sky.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Background ==
The whole council was up for election following a boundary review.<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=|title=Gosport Borough Council|url=https://s3-eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/lgbce/Reviews/South%20East/Hampshire/Gosport/Final/Summary.pdf|access-date=28 April 2022|website=lgbce.gov.uk}}</ref> This reduced the size the council from 34 to 28.

The seats up had previously been elected in 2018 and 2021. The elections in [[2018 Gosport Borough Council election|2018]], saw the Liberal Democrats gain four seats: two from Conservatives, and one each from the Labour and UKIP. The Conservatives however gained the sole UKIP seat, retaining their majority on the council. The other half of seats were elected in [[2021 Gosport Borough Council election|2021]], when the Conservatives gained one seat from Labour. This left the council with a Conservative majority of four.
