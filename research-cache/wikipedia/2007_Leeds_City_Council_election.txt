The '''2007 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England.

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's 99 seats were contested. Those seats up for election were those of the second-placed candidate elected for every ward at the [[2004 Leeds City Council election|2004 all-out election]], who had been granted a three-year term to expire in 2007.

Despite [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] gaining three council seats, the council remained in [[no overall control]] as no [[Politics of the United Kingdom#Political parties|political party]] had an [[overall majority]] of councillors. In [[coalition government|coalition]] since [[2004 Leeds City Council election|2004]], the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] and [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] council [[Administration (government)#Europe|administration]] continued.<ref name="archive">{{cite web| url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/files/Internet2007/2010/11/lcc%203rd%20may%202007.pdf | title = Leeds City Council Election results - 3rd May 2007 |publisher= leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 2013-01-17 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20110104223402/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/files/Internet2007/2010/11/lcc%203rd%20may%202007.pdf | archive-date = 2011-01-04}}</ref><ref name="results">{{Cite web| url = http://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/news/latest-news/top-stories/results-round-up-1-2096578 | title = Results round up  | access-date = 2013-01-15 |work= [[Yorkshire Evening Post]]}}</ref><ref name="archive2">{{cite web| url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/Council_and_democracy/Councillors_democracy_and_elections/Elections__publishing_results/page.aspx?pageID=baa06c5e-e54d-413c-8fb4-b6cfceb33ef9 | title = Leeds City Council Election results - 3rd May 2007 |publisher= leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 2013-01-13 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070520205747/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/Council_and_democracy/Councillors_democracy_and_elections/Elections__publishing_results/page.aspx?pageID=baa06c5e-e54d-413c-8fb4-b6cfceb33ef9 | archive-date = 2007-05-20}}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{Cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/vote2007/councils/html/da.stm | title = English local elections 2007: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-15 |publisher= BBC News}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{Cite web| url = http://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2007/67/ | title = Local Elections 2007: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-15 |publisher= andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref>
