The '''2000 Windsor and Maidenhead Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2000 to elect members of [[Royal Borough of Windsor and Maidenhead|Windsor and Maidenhead]] [[Unitary authority|Unitary]] Council in [[Berkshire]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] lost overall control of the council to [[no overall control]].<ref name=lose>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/uk_politics/vote2000/locals/145.stm | title = Windsor & Maidenhead Royal | access-date = 2010-11-26 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

==Election result==
The election saw a trial of mobile [[polling station]]s in an attempt to increase [[Voter turnout|turnout]].<ref name=hours>{{cite news | title = Open all hours | newspaper = [[The Times]] | page = 23 | date = 2000-04-27 }}</ref>
