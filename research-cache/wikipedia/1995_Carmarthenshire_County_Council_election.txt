{{Use dmy dates|date=April 2022}}
The '''first elections to the [[Carmarthenshire County Council]]''' were held on 4 May 1995. It was followed by the [[1999 Carmarthenshire County Council election|1999 election]]. Results are drawn from the national<ref>Western Mail, 6 May 1995</ref> and local<ref>{{cite news|title=Election Special|newspaper=South Wales Evening Post|date=5 May 1995}}</ref> press.
