The '''2008 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 1 May 2008 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England.

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's 99 seats were contested, plus an additional vacancy in [[Calverley and Farsley (ward)|Calverley and Farsley]] ward as Amanda Carter stood down. Those seats up for election were those of the first-placed candidate elected for every ward at the [[2004 Leeds City Council election|2004 all-out election]], who had been granted a four-year term to expire in 2008.

It saw the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] and [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] [[coalition government|coalition administration]] continue their control of the council. Despite both [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] and the Liberal Democrats winning more seats, the Conservatives won the majority of votes cast for the first time since [[1992 Leeds City Council election|1992]].

The Liberal Democrats regained a total of 24 seats on the council by defeating former Liberal Democrat-turn-independent and then Conservative councillor for [[Weetwood (ward)|Weetwood]] [[electoral ward|ward]], Brian Jennings. This had followed [[Hyde Park, Leeds|Hyde Park]] and [[Woodhouse, Leeds|Woodhouse]] councillor, Kabeer Hussain, defecting from the Liberal Democrats to Labour in October 2007, who he then left less than six months later to sit as an [[independent (politician)|Independent]] before the 2008 election.<ref name="archive">{{cite web| url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/files/Internet2007/2010/11/lcc%201st%20may%202008.pdf | title = Leeds City Council Election results - 1st May 2008 | publisher = leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 2013-01-18 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20110104223245/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/files/Internet2007/2010/11/lcc%201st%20may%202008.pdf | archive-date = 2011-01-04}}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/elections/local_council/08/html/da.stm | title = Elections 2008: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-18 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref><ref name="YEP">{{cite news| url = http://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/news/latest-news/top-stories/local-elections-leeds-results-1-2179981 | title = Local elections: Leeds results  | access-date = 2013-01-18 | newspaper = [[Yorkshire Evening Post]]}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{Cite web |url = http://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2008/67/ | title = Local Elections 2008: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-18 | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref>
