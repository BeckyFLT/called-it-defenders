The '''2012 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 3 May 2012 to elect members to [[Leeds City Council]] in England. It was held on the same day as other [[2012 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across the UK]].

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's seats were up for election. The councillors subsequently elected replaced those elected when their individual seats were previously contested in [[2008 Leeds City Council election|2008]].

Since taking majority control of the council in [[2011 Leeds City Council election|2011]], the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] council administration increased their total number of councillors from 55 to 63. They won eight more seats at the election, including six from the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]].<ref>[https://theleedscitizen.files.wordpress.com/2012/04/statement-of-persons-nominated-3-may-2012.pdf Statement of Persons Nominated] Leeds City Council</ref><ref name="results">{{Cite web | url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/docs/LCC%20Election%20Results%202012.pdf | title = Leeds City Council Election Results 3 May 2012 | access-date = 2013-01-22 | publisher = leeds.gov.uk | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20130320060255/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/docs/LCC%20Election%20Results%202012.pdf | archive-date = 20 March 2013 | url-status = dead }}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{cite news| url = https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/vote2012/council/E08000035.stm | title = Vote 2012 Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-22 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref><ref name="YEP">{{cite news| url = http://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/news/latest-news/top-stories/leeds-full-results-leeds-city-council-local-election-results-2012-1-4510562 | title = Leeds: Full results: Leeds City Council local election results 2012  | access-date = 2013-01-22 | newspaper = [[Yorkshire Evening Post]]}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{Cite web| url = https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/pdf/2012/leeds12.pdf | title = Local Elections 2012: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-22 | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref>
