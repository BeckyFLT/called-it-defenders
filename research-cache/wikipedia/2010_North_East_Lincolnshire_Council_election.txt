{{Use dmy dates|date=April 2026}}The '''2008 North East Lincolnshire Council election''' took place on 6 May 2010 to elect a third of the members of [[North East Lincolnshire Council]], the council of [[North East Lincolnshire]] in [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=BBC NEWS {{!}} Election 2010 {{!}} North East Lincolnshire council |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/election2010/council/html/3935.stm |access-date=2026-04-08 |website=[[BBC News]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as the other [[2010 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] as well as the [[2010 United Kingdom general election]]. The previous council election took place in [[2008 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2008]] and the following election was held in [[2011 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2011]]. In the election, the council stayed under [[no overall control]].<ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=May 2008 |title=Local Elections 2008 |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/humber/content/articles/2008/04/02/local_elections08_feature.shtml |access-date=2026-04-08 |website=[[BBC News]] |language=en-gb}}</ref>
