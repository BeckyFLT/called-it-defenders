'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Rotherham|Rotherham]] Metropolitan Borough Council''' were held on 3 May 2007. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party kept overall control of the council. One third of the council was up for election and no boundary changes were made.<ref name=results>{{cite news|work=BBC News |title=Election 2007 {{!}} Local Council Elections {{!}} Rotherham council|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/vote2007/councils/html/cf.stm|access-date=19 October 2012|date= 4 May 2007}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 54
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 7
*Others 2
