[[File:Leeds1983.png|right|thumb|500px|Map of the results for the 1983 Leeds council election.]]
Elections to [[Leeds City Council]] were held on Thursday, 5 May 1983, with one third of the council to be elected.<ref name="results">{{cite news |title=District results: Leeds |work=[[The Yorkshire Post]] |date=6 May 1983}}</ref>

The unwinding of much of [[SDP-Liberal Alliance|Alliance]]'s momentum helped [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] defend most of their [[1982 Leeds City Council election|1982 losses]], with the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] and Alliance gains restricted to a seat apiece in [[Morley, West Yorkshire|Morley]] North and [[Burmantofts]] respectively. The Conservative ousted by the Alliance gain in ultra-marginal [[Horsforth (ward)|Horsforth]] the year before won his way back onto the council with the only other gain of the night.<ref name="results" />
