The '''2007 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="election2007">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/vote2007/councils/html/12ue.stm|title=Huntingdonshire|date=4 May 2007|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=5 July 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 39
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 11
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 2<ref name="election">{{cite news|title=Results - Election 2007|date=5 May 2007|work=[[The Times]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]|page=83}}</ref>

==Background==
19 seats on the council were contested at the election, with the Conservatives controlling the council before the election with 40 of the 52 seats.<ref name="gear">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/hunts-life/candidates_gear_up_for_elections_1_408269|title=Candidates gear up for elections|date=11 April 2007|work=Hunts Post|access-date=6 July 2014|archive-date=3 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303234308/http://www.huntspost.co.uk/hunts-life/candidates_gear_up_for_elections_1_408269|url-status=dead}}</ref> A number of sitting [[councillor]]s stood down at the election including Conservatives Jean Chandler ([[Fenstanton]] [[Wards and electoral divisions of the United Kingdom|ward]]) and Nick Guyatt, and Liberal Democrat Shirley Menczer ([[Brampton, Cambridgeshire|Brampton]] ward).<ref name=gear/>

As well as candidates from the Conservative, Liberal Democrat and [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] parties, there were also [[Green Party of England and Wales|Green Party]] candidates in the 3 Huntingdon wards, a [[British National Party]] candidate in [[St Neots]] [[Eynesbury, Cambridgeshire|Eynesbury]] and a [[UK Independence Party]] candidate in [[Upwood]] and [[The Raveleys]].<ref name=gear/> Meanwhile, the only councillor to have served since the council was founded in 1974, Conservative Mike Newman, stood again in [[Somersham]] ward.<ref name=gear/>

==Election result==
The Conservatives suffered a net loss of 1 seat, after losing 2 seats to the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]], but also gaining 1 seat.<ref name="history">{{cite news|title=Election history is made|date=5 May 2007|work=[[Peterborough Evening Telegraph]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref> This meant the Conservatives stayed in control of the council with 39 councillors, compared to 11 for the Liberal Democrats and 2 [[Independent (politician)|independents]].<ref name=history/> One of the 2 Liberal Democrat gains came in Huntingdon North, where 19-year-old Sam Kemp took the seat by 19 votes from Conservative Maggie Wheeler.<ref name=history/>
