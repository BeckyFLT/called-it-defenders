The '''1998 Leeds City Council election''' took place on 7 May 1998 to elect members of [[City of Leeds]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[West Yorkshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council, alongside a vacancy in [[Wortley, Leeds|Wortley]] (following [[Fabian Hamilton]]'s election as [[Leeds North East (UK Parliament constituency)|Leeds North East]] [[Member of Parliament|MP]]) were up for election.

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=local>{{Cite news| title = Local Elections results |work= [[The Times]] | page = 46 | date = 1998-05-09 |issn=0140-0460}}</ref> Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] in the election was 24.66%.<ref name="results">{{cite web| url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/lcc/cncllrs/elect_98/elecmain.html | title = Election of Councillors : May 1998 |publisher= leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 2012-12-20 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/19991023093156/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/lcc/cncllrs/elect_98/elecmain.html | archive-date = 1999-10-23}}</ref>
