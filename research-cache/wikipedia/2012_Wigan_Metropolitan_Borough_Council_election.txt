The '''2012 Wigan Council elections''' to the Thirtieth Wigan Council were held on 3 May 2012, the same day as other [[2012 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].<ref>[http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/28751897-48FE-4D62-AB18-2F4894F75302/0/StatementofPersonsNomSt17C.pdf Candidates for 2012] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120417075303/http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/28751897-48FE-4D62-AB18-2F4894F75302/0/StatementofPersonsNomSt17C.pdf |date=17 April 2012 }} Wigan Council</ref>  One-third of the council was up for election.

As [[Wigan Metropolitan Borough Council]] is elected in thirds, change in vote share is compared with the corresponding 2008 elections and calculated on that basis.
