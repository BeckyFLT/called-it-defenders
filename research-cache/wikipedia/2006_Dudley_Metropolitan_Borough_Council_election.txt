'''Elections to Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council''' were held on 4 May 2006.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] retained control of the council, while [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] gained one seat and the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] lost one seat.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2006/59/|title=Local Election Results 2006 – Dudley|publisher=Local Elections Archive Project|access-date=19 July 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Dudley-1973-2012.pdf|title=Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council Election Results 1973-2012|publisher=The Elections Centre|access-date=19 July 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/RP06-26/RP06-26.pdf|title=Local elections 2006|publisher=House of Commons Library|access-date=19 July 2022}}</ref>
