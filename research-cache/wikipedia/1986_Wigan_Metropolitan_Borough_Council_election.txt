[[File:Wigan84.png|thumb|500px|right|Map of the results of the 1986 Wigan council election]]
Elections to the [[Metropolitan Borough of Wigan|Wigan]] council were held on Thursday, 8 May 1986, with one-third of the seats scheduled for re-election. Since the [[1984 Wigan Council election|previous election]] three by-elections had taken place, with [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] gaining the seat being fought in Orrell from the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] as well as holding their seats in Bedford-Astley and Beech Hill. The election seen fewer contestants than any previous election since the council's creation in 1973, with a record of four seats (Abram, [[Hindley, Greater Manchester|Hindley]], Hindley Green and Leigh Central) unchallenged. Even amongst those contested there were no minor parties represented, the fewest [[SDP-Liberal Alliance|Alliance]] candidates since the [[1980 Wigan Council election|1980 election]] and a record low of Conservative contenders at sixteen. The results were similarly record-breaking as Labour won twenty-two seats, with the Conservative and Alliance majorities in their respective heartland wards—and only holds—of Swinley and Langtree reduced to slender majorities. Labour won a high of 65% share of the vote, conversely the Conservatives share fell to a low of 13.1%, with their actual vote dropping into four figures for the first time. Overall turnout rose from the preceding year's underwhelming figure of 29.2%, to a more commonplace 35.8%.<ref name="results">{{cite web | url = http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | title = Wigan Council results from 1973 to 2008 | publisher = wigan.gov.uk | access-date = 2012-01-30 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20111120022015/http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | archive-date = 2011-11-20 | url-status = dead }}</ref>
