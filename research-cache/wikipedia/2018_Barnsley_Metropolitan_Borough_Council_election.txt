The '''2018 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2018 to elect members of [[Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref>{{cite web |title = Local government and combined authority mayoral elections - Thursday 3 May 2018 |url = https://www.barnsley.gov.uk/services/voting-and-elections/local-government-and-combined-authority-mayoral-elections-thursday-3-may-2018/ | website = barnsley.gov.uk |publisher = [[Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council]] |access-date = 24 April 2018 |df = dmy-all}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2018 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
