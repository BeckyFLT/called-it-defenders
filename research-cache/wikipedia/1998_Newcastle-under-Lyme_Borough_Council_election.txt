'''Elections to [[Newcastle-under-Lyme (borough)|Newcastle-under-Lyme]] Borough Council''' were held on 7 May 1998.  One third of the council stood for election, and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party kept control of the council.  After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 42
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 9
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 5
