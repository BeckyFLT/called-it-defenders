'''Elections to [[Newcastle-under-Lyme (borough)|Newcastle-under-Lyme]] Borough Council''' were held on 4 May 2000.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party kept overall control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 30
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 16
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 9
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1
