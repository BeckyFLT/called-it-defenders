The '''1982 Wandsworth Council election''' took place on 6 May 1982 to elect members of [[Wandsworth London Borough Council]] in [[London]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="London 1982 election">{{cite web|title=London Borough Council Elections 6 May 1982|url=https://londondatastore-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/docs/LBCE_1982-5-6.pdf|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402114315/https://londondatastore-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/docs/LBCE_1982-5-6.pdf|url-status=dead|archive-date=2 April 2015|website=London Datastore|publisher=Greater London Council|access-date=29 March 2015}}</ref>

==Background==
Wandsworth had been under Conservative majority control since the borough's 1978 elections.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2022-05-06 |title=Wandsworth: Labour ends 44 years of Tory rule in shock local election defeat {{!}} ITV News |url=https://www.itv.com/news/london/2022-05-06/labour-ends-44-years-of-tory-rule-in-wandsworth-in-shock-local-election-defeat |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240525232819/https://www.itv.com/news/london/2022-05-06/labour-ends-44-years-of-tory-rule-in-wandsworth-in-shock-local-election-defeat |archive-date=2024-05-25 |access-date=2026-06-21 |work=ITV News |language=en-GB}}</ref> According to the Greater London Council, ward boundaries changed in only one London borough between the 1978 and 1982 elections (Enfield), meaning the 1982 contest in Wandsworth was fought on the same ward boundaries as in 1978.<ref name=":0" />

==Election result==
Wandsworth's electorate at the election was 196,713, of whom 48.7% turned out to vote.<ref name=":0">{{cite report |url=https://data.london.gov.uk/download/24r9z/575f3aa9-8b89-4096-a7bb-ffc893bcfff9/LBCE_1982-5-6.pdf |title=London Borough Council Elections: 6 May 1982 |date=1982 |publisher=Greater London Council |access-date=2026-06-21 |via=London Datastore |format=PDF}}</ref> The Conservative Party won 33 of the council's 61 seats, retaining its majority, while Labour won 25 seats.<ref name=":0" /> Across the borough, the Conservatives took 42.7% of the vote, Labour took 38.1%, and the SDP–Liberal Alliance took 18.8% .<ref name=":0" />
