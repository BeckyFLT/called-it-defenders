The '''2023 Amber Valley Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023<ref>{{Cite web |last=Council |first=Amber Valley Borough |date=2023-02-16 |title=Elections 4 May 2023 |url=https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/councillors-and-elections/elections/elections-4-may-2023/ |access-date=2023-04-05 |website=www.ambervalley.gov.uk |language=en-GB}}</ref> to elect all 42 councillors for [[Amber Valley|Amber Valley Borough Council]]. This was on the same day as other [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|local elections in England]].

Following the boundary review by the [[Local Government Boundary Commission for England|Local Government Boundary Commission]], this was the first election under the new boundaries. In addition to the new ward boundaries the council was no longer elected by thirds, with the whole council elected once every 4 years.<ref>{{Cite web |title=UK Government Web Archive |url=https://webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ukgwa/20220923031005/https://www.lgbce.org.uk/all-reviews/east-midlands/derbyshire/amber-valley |access-date=2023-03-23 |website=webarchive.nationalarchives.gov.uk}}</ref> Along with [[Derby City Council]], who also changed to whole council elections in 2023, Amber Valley was the last authority in Derbyshire to switch to whole council elections. The last election where all members were elected was at the [[2000 Amber Valley Borough Council election|2000 election]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Amber Valley Borough Council Election Results 1973-2012 |url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Amber-Valley-1973-2012.pdf |access-date=4 Oct 2025}}</ref>

Under the boundary review, the number of councillors elected was reduced from 45 to 42. Prior to the election the council was led by Conservative councillor Kevin Buttery, who had been leader since May 2021. He chose not to stand for re-election.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Bisknell |first1=Eddie |title=All you need to know ahead of 2023 Amber Valley elections |url=https://www.derbytelegraph.co.uk/news/local-news/you-need-know-ahead-2023-8341517 |access-date=29 April 2023 |work=Derbyshire Live |date=12 April 2023}}</ref>

Labour won a majority at the election, winning several seats from the Conservatives. The Labour leader, Chris Emmas-Williams, was appointed leader of the council at the subsequent annual council meeting on 24 May 2023.<ref>{{cite web |title=Council meeting, 24 May 2023 |url=https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/council/committees-and-meetings/committee-documents?CommitteeRef=CNL&MeetingRef=2270 |website=Amber Valley Borough Council |access-date=31 May 2023}}</ref>
