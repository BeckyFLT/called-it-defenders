The '''2008 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 1 May 2008 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/elections/local_council/08/html/29uq.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 44
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 4<ref>{{cite news | title = Local elections: Results: Voters in the cities and the shires have their say in the ballot box | work = The Guardian | page = 7 | date = 2008-05-02 }}</ref>

==Election result==
The results saw the Conservatives retain control after gaining 3 seats from the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] to have 44 seats compared to 4 for the Liberal Democrats.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/kent/7378982.stm | title = Tories take control in Maidstone | access-date = 2010-11-23 | date = 2008-05-02 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>
