}}The '''2021 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2021 to elect members of [[Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England. It took place on the same day as other [[2020 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Local government elections |url=https://www.local.gov.uk/about/our-meetings-and-leadership/political-composition/local-government-elections |publisher=[[Local Government Association]] |access-date=8 August 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210510000000/https://www.local.gov.uk/about/our-meetings-and-leadership/political-composition/local-government-elections |archive-date=10 May 2021}}</ref> The election was originally due to take place in May 2020, but was postponed due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Local elections postponed for a year over coronavirus |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-51876269 |work=BBC News |date=13 March 2020 |access-date=8 August 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200313171804/https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-51876269 |archive-date=13 March 2020}}</ref>
