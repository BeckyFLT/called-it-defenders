The '''2012 Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' was held on 3 May 2012 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Dudley|Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in the [[West Midlands (county)|West Midlands]], England, as part of the [[2012 United Kingdom local elections]]. 26 seats were up for election, and the results saw the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] losing to a majority of 11 seats to [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]].<ref name=E&S>{{cite news|title=Surprise results see big swing to Labour|newspaper=Express & Star|date=4 May 2012}}</ref>
