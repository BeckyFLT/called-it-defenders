The '''2019 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="20190502_Results">{{cite web |title=Local election results 2 May 2019 |url=https://www.oldham.gov.uk/homepage/987/local_election_results_2_may_2019 |website=Oldham Borough Council |publisher=Oldham Borough Council |access-date=30 June 2019 }}{{Dead link|date=January 2022 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The election took place on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] in England.
