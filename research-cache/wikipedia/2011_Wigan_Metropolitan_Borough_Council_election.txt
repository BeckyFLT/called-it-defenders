'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Wigan|Wigan]] Council''' were held in May 2011. One-third of the council was up for election. These elections were held as part of the [[2011 United Kingdom local elections]].<ref>{{cite news| url = https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/election2011/council/html/bw.stm | title = Election 2011: Wigan council | access-date = 2012-07-06 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref><ref name="wiganmbc">{{cite web| url = http://kinnear.wiganmbc.gov.uk/electionresults/index.asp?ElectionDate=05+May+2011 | title = Local Election Results 05 May 2011 | publisher = wiganmbc.gov.uk |access-date = 2012-07-06}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{cite web| url = https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2011/41/ | title = Local Elections Archive Project: 2011 – Wigan | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk |access-date = 2012-07-06}}</ref>
