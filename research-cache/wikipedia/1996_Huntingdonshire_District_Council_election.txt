The '''1996 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 2 May 1996<ref>{{cite web|title=1996 Election Results Summary|url=https://www.https/|website=huntingdonshire.gov.uk|access-date=24 June 2025|archive-date=19 August 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130819141428/http://https/|url-status=dead}}</ref> to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire District Council]] in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="election96">{{cite news|title=Complete list of results - Elections|date=4 May 1996|work=[[The Times]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]|page=8<!--|access-date=29 June 2014-->}}</ref> These seats were last contested in the [[1992 United Kingdom local elections]].<ref>{{cite web |title=The Huntingdonshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2017, Statutory Instrument 2017 No. 480 |url=https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/480/article/1/made |website=legislation.gov.uk |access-date=31 March 2022}}</ref>
