The '''2023 Uttlesford District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect members of [[Uttlesford District Council]] in [[Essex]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.uttlesford.gov.uk/local-elections-2023 |title=Local elections 2023 |access-date=6 January 2023 |publisher=[[Uttlesford District Council]]}}</ref><ref name="UDC March 2023">Uttlesford District Council - Notice of Election - District elections 4 May 2023 https://www.uttlesford.gov.uk/media/11910/Notice-of-election-election-of-district-councillors-on-Thursday-4-May-2023/pdf/District_NoE-_FINAL1.pdf?m=638158606616200000</ref><ref name="UDC April 2023">Uttlesford District Council - Statement of Persons Nominated - District elections 4 May 2023 https://www.uttlesford.gov.uk/media/11916/Statement-of-persons-nominated-Election-of-district-councillors/pdf/SoPN_District.pdf?m=638162886375670000</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] across England. 

Prior to the election the residents' association '[[Residents for Uttlesford]]' held a majority.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2019-05-03 |title=Just four Tories left in Uttlesford after district council rout by opposition |url=https://www.bishopsstortfordindependent.co.uk/news/just-four-tories-left-in-uttlesford-after-district-council-rout-by-opposition-9069250/ |access-date=2023-04-05 |website=Bishop's Stortford Independent |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |date=2019-05-03 |title=Conservatives lose council to local residents' association |language=en-GB |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-essex-48155121 |access-date=2023-04-05}}</ref>

The election saw Residents for Uttlesford retain their majority. The Conservative and Liberal Democrat group leaders both lost their seats. The Greens lost all their seats on the council (the three seats it held prior to the election having all been councillors elected for Residents for Uttlesford but who later defected).<ref>{{cite news |last1=Ridler |first1=Charlie |title=Uttlesford District Council election results in full |url=https://www.saffronwaldenreporter.co.uk/news/23507474.uttlesford-district-council-election-results-full/ |access-date=11 June 2023 |work=Saffron Walden Reporter |date=8 May 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Local elections 2023: Conservatives lose control of Brentwood Council |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-essex-65474909 |access-date=12 June 2023 |work=BBC News |date=5 May 2023}}</ref>
