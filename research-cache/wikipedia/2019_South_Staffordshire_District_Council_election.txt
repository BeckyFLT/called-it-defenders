The '''2019 [[South Staffordshire]] [[District council (England)|District Council]] [[election]]''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of the [[South Staffordshire District Council]] in [[Elections in England|England]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Election Results South Staffordshire Council |url=https://www.sstaffs.gov.uk/elections/election-results.cfm?election=25 |website=www.sstaffs.gov.uk |access-date=4 August 2021 |language=en}}</ref> It was held on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
