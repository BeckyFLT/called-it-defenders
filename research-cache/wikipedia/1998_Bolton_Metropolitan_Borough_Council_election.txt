The '''1998 Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 7 May 1998 to elect members of [[Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council]] in [[Greater Manchester]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party kept overall control of the council.<ref name=results>{{cite news| title = Local Elections results | newspaper = [[The Times]] | page = 46 | date = 9 May 1998 }}</ref>

20 seats were contested in the election, with 15 being held by Labour, 3 by the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] and 2 by the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]].<ref name=verdict>{{cite news |title=Voters' chance to give verdict on Labour's first 12 months |url=https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/archive/1998/05/06/Lancashire+Archive/6154298.Voters__chance_to_give_verdict_on_Labour_s_first_12_months/ |access-date=8 September 2019 |work=[[The Bolton News]] |date=6 May 1998 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120426172925/https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/archive/1998/05/06/Lancashire+Archive/6154298.Voters__chance_to_give_verdict_on_Labour_s_first_12_months/ |archive-date=26 April 2012}}</ref> The three main parties contested all of the 20 seats, with three [[Socialist Labour Party (UK)|Socialist Labour Party]] and four other candidates standing.<ref name=verdict/>

The election saw the lowest [[Voter turnout|turnout]] in at least 30 years at 23.5%, a significant drop from the 36.8% turnout in the [[1996 Bolton Council election|1996 election]].<ref name=disgrace>{{cite news |title=Low poll 'a disgrace' |url=https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/archive/1998/05/08/Lancashire+Archive/6154003.Low_poll__a_disgrace_/ |access-date=8 September 2019 |work=[[The Bolton News]] |date=8 May 1998 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120426172949/https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/archive/1998/05/08/Lancashire+Archive/6154003.Low_poll__a_disgrace_/ |archive-date=26 April 2012}}</ref> [[Farnworth]] [[Wards of the United Kingdom|ward]] saw the lowest turnout at 15%, while the highest was 32.6% in Astley Bridge.<ref name=disgrace/> The Conservatives gained 2 seats in the election to become the main opposition party on the council again.<ref name=disgrace/> They gained Astley Bridge and [[Bradshaw, Greater Manchester|Bradshaw]] wards from Labour, having defeated the Labour candidate in Bradshaw by 2 votes after 5 recounts.<ref name=disgrace/> However Labour held on in [[Kearsley]] by 8 votes and won [[Westhoughton]] from the Liberal Democrats to remain firmly in control of the council.<ref name=disgrace/>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 47
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 8
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 5<ref name=results/>
