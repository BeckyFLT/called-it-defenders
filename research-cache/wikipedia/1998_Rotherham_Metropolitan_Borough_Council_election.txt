Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Rotherham|Rotherham]] Metropolitan Borough Council were held on 7 May 1998.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party kept overall control of the council.<ref name=results>{{Cite news | title = Local Elections results | work = [[The Times]] | page = 46 | date = 9 May 1998 |issn=0140-0460}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 65
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 1
