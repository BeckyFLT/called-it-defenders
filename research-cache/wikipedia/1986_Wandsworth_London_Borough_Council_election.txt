The '''1986 Wandsworth Council election''' took place on 8 May 1986 to elect members of [[Wandsworth London Borough Council]] in [[London]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="London 1986 election">{{cite web|title=London Borough Council Elections 8 May 1986|url=https://londondatastore-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/docs/LBCE_1986-5-8.pdf|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141217153923/https://londondatastore-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/docs/LBCE_1986-5-8.pdf|url-status=dead|archive-date=17 December 2014|website=London Datastore|publisher=London Residuary Body|access-date=29 March 2015}}</ref>
