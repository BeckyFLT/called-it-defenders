{{main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}

'''Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council''' were held in May 1982.<ref name="Hyndburn Council election records">{{cite journal|title=Hyndburn Council election records|journal=Hyndburn Council election records|date=May 1982|pages=1}}</ref> One third of the council was up for election. Labour were defending 8 seats, The Conservatives 7. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] held 7 seats with the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] holding their 7 and the Liberal/SDP Alliance fielding a full slate of candidates taking a seat off Labour (Baxenden).

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] (20)
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] (27-1=26)
*[[Social Democratic Party (UK)|SDP]] (27-1=26)
