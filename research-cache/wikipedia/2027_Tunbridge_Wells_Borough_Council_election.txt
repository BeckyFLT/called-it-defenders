The '''2027 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' will be held on 6 May 2027 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]] in [[Kent]], [[England]]. This will be on the same day as other [[2027 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] held across the United Kingdom.

==Background==

===Local government reorganisation===

Due to proposed [[upcoming structural changes to local government in England|structural changes to local government in England]], the [[2026 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election|2026 election]] was intended to be the final election to the council before it was abolished and replaced by West Kent Council, a new [[unitary authority]].<ref>{{Cite web|first=Hsin-Yi |last=Lo |author-link= |first2= |last2= |author2-link= |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cdx762yve7do |website=bbc.com |title=Four new councils confirmed for Kent and Medway |pages= |date=16 July 2026 |access-date= |work= |publisher=[[BBC News]] |place= |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260818203742/https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cdx762yve7do |archive-date=18 August 2026 }}</ref> However, on 7 September 2026, the government announced it was withdrawing plans for the planned restructuring pending review. Following this announcement, local elections due to be held in 2027 under the existing local electoral calendar would proceed.<ref>{{Cite web|first=Becky |last=Morton |author-link= |first2= |last2= |author2-link= |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2zkj09dy4o |website=bbc.com |title=Plans for major overhaul of councils halted |pages= |date=8 September 2026 |access-date=15 September 2026 |work= |publisher=[[BBC News]] |place= |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260908123457/https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2zkj09dy4o |archive-date=8 September 2026 }}</ref>
