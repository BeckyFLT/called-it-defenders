The '''2023 West Suffolk District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect members of [[West Suffolk District Council]] in [[Suffolk]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.westsuffolk.gov.uk/council/elections/vacancies-elections-results/elections/ |title=Current vacancies and elections |access-date=5 April 2023 |publisher=[[West Suffolk District Council]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] in England.

==Summary==
The council had been under Conservative majority control prior to the election. After the election the Conservatives remained the largest group, but lost their majority. A coalition of Labour, the West Suffolk Independents, Liberal Democrats, Greens and independent councillors formed after the election, with Labour councillor Cliff Waterman being appointed the new leader of the council at the subsequent annual council meeting on 23 May 2023.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Green |first1=Tamika |title=Bury St Edmunds councillor Cliff Waterman on 'exciting' new era for West Suffolk Council after being elected coalition leader |url=https://www.suffolknews.co.uk/bury-st-edmunds/news/west-suffolk-is-in-very-safe-hands-coalition-leader-on-ne-9314341/ |access-date=4 July 2023 |work=Suffolk News |date=25 May 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Sinclair |first1=Andrew |title=West Suffolk: Alliance formed to stop Tories running council |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-suffolk-65693992 |access-date=7 January 2024 |work=BBC News |date=24 May 2023}}</ref>
