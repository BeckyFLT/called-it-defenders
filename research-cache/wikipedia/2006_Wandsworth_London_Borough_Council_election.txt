The '''2006 Wandsworth Council election''' took place on 4 May 2006 to elect members of [[London Borough of Wandsworth|Wandsworth London Borough Council]] in [[London]], [[England]]. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] Party stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=elections>{{cite news | title = Local elections | newspaper = [[The Times]] | page = 9 | date = 2006-05-05 }}</ref>

The Conservatives were always expected to hold control<ref name=actor>{{cite news | title = Tories bask in biggest wins since Falklands factor boost; London analysis | newspaper = [[Evening Standard]] | page = 10 | date = 2006-05-05 }}</ref> and the low rates of [[council tax]] in Wandsworth were used as an example by the Conservatives in neighbouring councils such as [[London Borough of Hammersmith and Fulham|Hammersmith and Fulham]].<ref name=yuppies>{{cite news | title = Inner-city yuppies give Labour good reason to fear the future | newspaper = [[The Times]] | page = 8 | date = 2006-05-06 }}</ref> During the campaign one [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] candidate, Nick Bowes, had said that the party was being well beaten and described the campaign as being "miserable, exhausting and lonely".<ref name=sweat>{{cite news | first = Joe | last = Murphy | title = Labour candidate tells of 'cold sweat' | newspaper = [[Evening Standard]] | page = 19 | date = 2006-04-21 }}</ref>
