'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Wigan|Wigan]] Council''' were held on 4 May 1978, with one third of the council up for election as well as vacancies in Ward 2 and 17. Previous to the election, a [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] councillor in Ward 9 defected to become an [[Independent Labour]], reducing Labour's majority to 38. This seat was up for vote and became the only Labour gain of the night, as they suffered five losses to the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]], and their majority reduced to 30 - half of what it was in [[1973 Wigan Council election|1973]]. The election seen a first of all wards being contested, with turnout rising marginally to 34.7%.<ref name="results">{{cite web | url = http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | title = Wigan Council results from 1973 to 2008 | publisher = wigan.gov.uk | access-date = 2012-01-29 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20111120022015/http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | archive-date = 2011-11-20 | url-status=dead }}</ref>
