The '''2007 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/vote2007/councils/html/29uq.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 41
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 7<ref name=hold/>

==Election result==
The results saw the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] lose its last [[councillor]], Ronnie Ooi, on the council.<ref name=toehold>{{cite news | first = Will | last = Woodward | title = Elections 2007: Labour: Left struggles to maintain toehold on rural and suburban councils | newspaper = The Guardian | page = 6 | date = 2007-05-05 }}</ref> Ooi lost his seat in [[Southborough, Kent|Southborough]] and [[High Brooms]] [[Wards of the United Kingdom|ward]] after campaigning by distancing himself from the national Labour government and calling on voters to preserve an opposition to the Conservatives on the council.<ref name=disgusted>{{cite news | first = Greg | last = Hurst | title = Disgusted of Tunbridge Wells closes door on Labour's last manstanding | newspaper = The Times|location=London | page = 29 | date = 2007-04-25 }}</ref>
