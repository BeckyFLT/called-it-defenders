[[File:South Staffordshire UK ward map 2007.svg|thumb|Results of the 2007 South Staffordshire District Council election]]
The '''2007 South Staffordshire District Council election''' to the [[South Staffordshire District Council]] took place on 3 May 2007, with the [[Featherstone, Staffordshire|Featherstone]] and [[Shareshill]] Ward results delayed following the death of a candidate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2007/327/|title=Local election results 2007|date=n.d.|publisher=Local Elections Archive Project|access-date=26 September 2021}}</ref> All 49 seats were up for election, 41 of which went to [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] candidates.<ref name=data>{{cite web|url=http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/South-Staffordshire-1973-2011.pdf|title=South Staffordshire Council Election Results 1973-2011|date=2011|last1=Rallings|first1=Colin|last2=Thrasher|first2=Michael|publisher=The Elections Centre, Plymouth University|access-date=26 September 2021}}</ref> 13 of the 14 seats won unopposed went to Conservatives.<ref name=data/>
