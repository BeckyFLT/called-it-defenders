The '''1996 [[Leeds City Council]] elections''' were held on Thursday, 2 May 1996, with a third of the council up for election.

The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] suffered another routing, although with a less impressive [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] vote and a slight recovery in their own vote, were able to hold on to three seats. Labour furthered their stranglehold on the council with an additional seven gains, with all but [[Burmantofts]] ([[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]]) and [[Pudsey|Pudsey South]] ([[Independent (politician)|Independent]]) from the Tories. As a result of the gains, those two wards - along with the Tory losses of [[Aireborough]], [[Temple Newsam (ward)#Councillors since 1973|Halton]], [[Morley, West Yorkshire|Morley North]] and [[Weetwood]] wards - were now represented entirely by Labour (a first for most). The other Labour gain was snatching the remaining Tory seat in the former Tory-Liberal battleground of  [[Otley]] & [[Wharfedale]].

The Lib Dems offset their loss to Labour in Burmantofts with gains from the Tories in [[Horsforth (ward)|Horsforth]] and the newly three-way marginal [[Alwoodley (ward)#Councillors since 1980|North]] ward. The results, however, were a disappointment to the Lib Dems - evidenced by their poor performance in Otley & Wharfedale, and a vote sunk to their [[1992 Leeds City Council election|1992 level]]. Another parallel to the 1992 election was the slump in overall turnout to a record low.

Peter Kersting's decision to forgo defending his seat for Pudsey South seen the absence of Independents from the council for the first time since [[1983 Leeds City Council election|1983]]. The [[Green Party of England and Wales|Greens]] advanced further into a respectable second place in [[Wortley, Leeds|Wortley]], in contrast to the stagnant fortunes of the [[Liberal Party (UK, 1989)|Liberals]] and the weakened support for the sole [[Socialist Party (England and Wales)|Militant Labour]] candidate.<ref name="results">{{cite news |title=District results: Leeds |work=[[The Yorkshire Post]] |date=3 May 1996}}</ref>
