The '''2024 North East Lincolnshire Council election''' was held on Thursday 2 May 2024, alongside the [[2024 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections in the United Kingdom]] on the same day. 12 of the 42 members of [[North East Lincolnshire Council]] in [[Lincolnshire]] were elected.

The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] lost control of the council to [[no overall control]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=North East Lincolnshire election result |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/election/2024/england/councils/E06000012}}</ref> They remained the largest party, and continued to run the council as a minority administration.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Madden |first1=Richard |last2=Morris Poxton |first2=Ivan |title=Conservatives to continue running minority council |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv22pqlz5lgo |access-date=14 July 2024 |work=BBC News |date=24 May 2024}}</ref>

==Background==
North East Lincolnshire was created as a [[unitary authorities of England|unitary authority]] in 1996. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] held a majority on the council until [[2003 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2003]], when the council fell to [[no overall control]].<ref>{{cite web |title=North East Lincolnshire Council Election Results 1995-2012 |url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/North-East-Lincolnshire-1995-2012.pdf |website=Elections Centre |access-date=2 April 2024}}</ref> Labour retook the council in [[2012 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2012]],<ref>{{cite web |title=BBC News - Vote 2012 - North East Lincolnshire |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/vote2012/council/E06000012.stm |website=BBC News |access-date=5 April 2024}}</ref> but lost the council in [[2016 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2016]].<ref>{{cite news |title=No Overall Control North East Lincolnshire |url=https://www.itv.com/news/calendar/update/2016-05-06/no-overall-control-north-east-lincolnshire |access-date=5 April 2024 |work=ITV News |date=6 May 2016}}</ref> The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] won their first majority on the council in [[2019 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2019]], and have controlled the council since then. In the [[2023 North East Lincolnshire Council election|previous election]], the Conservatives won 7 seats (down 3) with 41.5% of the vote, Labour won 5 (up 1) with 38.1%, [[independent politician|independents]] won 2 (up 2) with 9.6%, and the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] defended their seat up for election with 7.0%.

The seats up for election in 2024 were last contested in [[2021 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2021]]; because of the delay of all local elections due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom|COVID-19 pandemic]], the seats are up for election after 3 years rather than the usual 4. In that election, the Conservatives gained 8 seats with 60.3% of the vote, Labour lost 7 with 25.6%, and the Liberal Democrats lost 1 with 5.5%.
