{{Short description|Local election in Devon, UK}}
[[File:East Devon UK ward map 2011.svg|thumb|Results of the 2011 East Devon District Council election]]
'''Elections to [[East Devon District Council]]''' were held on 5 May 2011, alongside other [[2011 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across the United Kingdom]]. All 59 seats on the council were up for election. Following the election the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] remained in [[no overall control|overall control]].
