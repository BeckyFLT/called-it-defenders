The '''1990 Wandsworth Council election''' took place on 3 May 1990 to elect members of [[Wandsworth London Borough Council]] in [[London]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="London 1990 election">{{cite web|title=London Borough Council Elections 3rd May 1990|url=https://londondatastore-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/docs/LBCE_1990-5-3.pdf|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160113101252/https://londondatastore-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/docs/LBCE_1990-5-3.pdf|url-status=dead|archive-date=13 January 2016|website=London Datastore|publisher=London Research Centre|access-date=29 March 2015}}</ref>
