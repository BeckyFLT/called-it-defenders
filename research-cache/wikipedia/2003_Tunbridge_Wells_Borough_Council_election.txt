The '''2003 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 1 May 2003 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite web| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2003/locals/html/299.stm | title = Local elections | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 33
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 12
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 3<ref name=britain>{{cite news | title = How Britain voted: English and Scottish councils | work = The Independent | pages = 18–19 | date = 2003-05-03 }}</ref>
