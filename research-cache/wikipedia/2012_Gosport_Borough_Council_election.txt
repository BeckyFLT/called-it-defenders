The '''2012 Gosport Council election''' was held on 3 May 2012 to elect members of the [[Gosport|Gosport Council]] in England.<ref name="BBC">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-17299200 |title=Local elections 2012 |access-date=2012-04-30 |publisher=BBC News}}</ref> This was the same day as other [[2012 United Kingdom local elections]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Local Elections Archive Project – 2012 – Gosport|url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2012/219/|access-date=2020-09-10|website=andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref> The Conservatives gained three seats from the Liberal Democrats, whilst Labour gained one seat each from the Conservatives and Liberal Democrats. Both Labour and the Conservatives saw an increased vote share, whilst the Liberal Democrats' vote share fell.

After the election the composition of the council was:

* Conservative: 24
* Labour: 5
* Liberal Democrats: 5
