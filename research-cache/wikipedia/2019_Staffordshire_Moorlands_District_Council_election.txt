The '''2019 Staffordshire Moorlands District Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[Staffordshire Moorlands District Council]] in England.<ref name="staffsmoorlands">{{cite web|url=https://www.staffsmoorlands.gov.uk/article/3559/District-Town-and-Parish-Elections-2nd-May-2019|title=District, Town and Parish Elections 2nd May 2019 - Staffordshire Moorlands District Council|website=staffsmoorlands.gov.uk|access-date=2020-05-22 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416045116/https://www.staffsmoorlands.gov.uk/article/3559/District-Town-and-Parish-Elections-2nd-May-2019 |archive-date=16 April 2026}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
