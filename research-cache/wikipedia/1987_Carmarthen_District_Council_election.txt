'''An election to [[District of Carmarthen|Carmarthen District Council]]''' was held on 7 May 1987.<ref name="CJ 15-5-87">{{cite news|title=Electors bury the bunker issue|newspaper=Carmarthen Journal|date=15 May 1987}}</ref>  It was preceded by the [[1983 Carmarthen District Council election|1983]] election and followed by the [[1991 Carmarthen District Council election|1991]] election. On the same day there were elections to the other district local authorities and community councils in [[Wales]].<ref>{{cite news|title= How Wales Went to the Polls|newspaper=Western Mail|date=8 May 1987}}</ref>
{{TOC right}}

==Boundary changes==
A limited number of boundary changes had taken place since the previous election. Some wards were also renamed.
