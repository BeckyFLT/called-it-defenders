'''Elections to [[Amber Valley Borough Council]]''' in [[Derbyshire]], [[England]] took place on Thursday 2 May 2019. One third of the council seats were up for election.<ref name="Statement of persons nominated">{{Cite web|url=https://info.ambervalley.gov.uk/docarc/docviewer.aspx?docguid=928c67225a3742adb0b6a66c190aa6d6 |title=Statement of persons nominated |access-date=8 May 2019 }}</ref> The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] gained overall control of the council, taking a total of five seats from the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]]s.<ref name="Amber Valley Election Results 2019">{{Cite web|url=https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/councillors-and-elections/elections/local-elections/borough-election-results/2-may-2019 |title=Amber Valley Election Results 2019|access-date=8 May 2019 }}</ref> In addition, the [[Green Party of England and Wales|Green Party]] gained representation for the first time. After the election, the composition of the council was:-

* [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 25
* [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 19
* [[Green Party of England and Wales|Green]] 1

===Belper Central===
Candidate Neil Ploughman was suspended by the Labour Party when alleged antisemitic Facebook posts were discovered; however, he remained on the ballot. After being elected, he initially sat as an independent on the authority. He was subsequently reinstated by the party with no charge to answer.<ref name="Candidate suspended over 'anti-Semitic' posts wins election">{{Cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-derbyshire-48147733 |title=Candidate suspended over 'anti-Semitic' posts wins election |access-date=8 May 2019}}</ref> He later left the party in 2021 sitting as a 'socialist'.
