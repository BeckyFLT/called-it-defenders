The '''1999 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/271.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-21 | work = [[BBC News Online]]}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 28
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 12
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 7
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1<ref name=britain>{{cite news | title = Britain votes: Local Council Election Results | newspaper = [[The Independent]] | page = 12 | date = 1999-05-07}}</ref>
