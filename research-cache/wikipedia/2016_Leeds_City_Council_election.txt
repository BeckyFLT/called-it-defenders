The '''2016 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 5 May 2016 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England.<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.leeds.gov.uk/council/Pages/Leeds-City-Council-election.aspx |title= Leeds City Council Election |work= Leeds City Council |access-date=8 April 2016}}</ref> It was on the same day as other [[2016 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's seats were up for election. The councillors subsequently elected replaced those elected when their individual seats were previously contested in [[2012 Leeds City Council election|2012]].

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] retained all 21 of their contested council seats, maintaining their majority control of the council with a total of 63 of the 99 elected councillors. It was [[2015 Leeds City Council election|the second election in a row]] in which no party gained or lost any seats.

Incumbent [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] councillors, Debra Coupar ([[Cross Gates and Whinmoor (ward)|Cross Gates & Whinmoor]]), Roger Harington ([[Gipton and Harehills (ward)|Gipton & Harehills]]) and Janette Walker ([[Headingley]]), all stood for new wards. Coupar was elected to represent [[Temple Newsam (ward)|Temple Newsam]] and Walker replaced Coupar as a councillor for [[Cross Gates and Whinmoor (ward)|Cross Gates & Whinmoor]]. Harington lost to the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] in [[Weetwood (ward)|Weetwood]].<ref>{{cite web | title = Roger Harington is Labour’s candidate for Weetwood ward in May 2016. | url = https://leedsnwclp.wordpress.com/2016/02/09/roger-harington-is-labours-candidate-for-weetwood-ward-in-may-2016 | website = leedsnwclp.wordpress.com | publisher = [[Leeds North West (UK Parliament constituency)|Leeds North West]] [[Constituency Labour Party]] | date = 9 February 2016}}</ref>
