{{main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}

'''Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council''' were held in May 1980.<ref name="Hyndburn Council election records"/> 1980 was the second year of local elections on the new boundaries.<ref name="Hyndburn Council election records">{{cite journal|title=Hyndburn Council election records|journal=Hyndburn Council election records|date=May 1980}}</ref> One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] party lost overall control of the council. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] taking 8 seats from the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]].

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] (28−8=20)
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] (19+8=27)
