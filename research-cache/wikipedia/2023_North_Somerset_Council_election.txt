The '''2023 North Somerset Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect all 50 councillors to the [[North Somerset Council]] in England. It was conducted on the same day as the [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections across England]].

Prior to the election the council was under [[no overall control]], being run by a 'rainbow coalition' of Liberal Democrats, Labour, Greens and independent councillors, led by independent councillor Steve Bridger. Following the election the council remained under no overall control and a similar coalition emerged, but led by Liberal Democrat councillor Mike Bell.<ref>{{cite web |title=No party has won a majority in North Somerset |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-somerset-65496994 |website=BBC News |publisher=BBC |date=5 May 2023|access-date=7 May 2023}}</ref><ref name=BBC>{{cite news |title=North Somerset Council announces leader of partner administration |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-somerset-65588990 |access-date=23 May 2023 |work=BBC News |date=14 May 2023}}</ref>

==Background==
North Somerset Council is elected every 4 years, with all council seats being up for election. Following the [[2019 North Somerset Council election|2019 election]] a rainbow coalition consisting of the North Somerset Independent Group, the Liberal Democrats, the Labour Party, the Portishead Independents and the Green Party had formed, initially led by Independent Group leader, Don Davies.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Postans |first1=Adam |title=Rainbow coalition to control North Somerset Council |url=https://www.bristolpost.co.uk/news/local-news/rainbow-coalition-control-north-somerset-2856458 |access-date=23 May 2023 |work=Bristol Live |date=10 May 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Council make-up and group leaders |url=https://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/council-democracy/councillors-committees/council-make-group-leaders |website=North Somerset Council |access-date=27 April 2023}}</ref> Davies resigned as leader in May 2022 and was succeeded by his independent colleague Steve Bridger.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Williams |first1=Charlie |title=Council elects Steve Bridger as new leader |url=https://www.thewestonmercury.co.uk/news/local-council/20484211.council-elects-steve-bridger-new-leader/ |access-date=23 May 2023 |work=Weston Mercury |date=11 May 2022}}</ref> Liberal Democrat councillor Mike Bell served as deputy leader in the administration prior to 2023.<ref>https://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/council-democracy/councillors-committees/councillors/executive-members</ref>

Throughout the previous council's term from 2019 to 2023, bus and transport services became a considerable issue in the local area. With services such as the X5 Weston-Super-Mare to Cribbs Causeway being cut or reduced and other services such as the X2 Yatton to Bristol and 126 Weston to Wells being axed entirely.<ref>https://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/news/council-working-save-bus-routes-following-more-cuts</ref> In the later part of the council's term a new 'demand bus service' was being created and the council successfully saved some services from being entirely removed.<ref>https://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/news/council-working-save-bus-routes-following-more-cuts</ref><ref>https://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/news/first-look-new-demand-buses-their-way-north-somersets-communities</ref>

Following the 2023 election the council remained under no overall control, with the Conservatives the largest group.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Wimperis |first1=John |title=North Somerset Council local elections live: Reaction as Conservatives largest party amid Labour and Green gains |url=https://www.somersetlive.co.uk/news/somerset-news/north-somerset-local-elections-2023-8409197 |access-date=23 May 2023 |work=Somerset Live |date=5 May 2023}}</ref> On 12 May 2023, it was announced there would be a partnership between the Liberal Democrats (sitting as a group with three independents), Labour, the Green Party, the Portishead Independents and two other independents (sitting as their own group).<ref>{{cite web |title=Partnership administration announced |url=https://n-somerset.gov.uk/news/partnership-administration-announced-north-somerset |website=North Somerset Council |access-date=14 May 2023}}</ref> Mike Bell, leader of the Liberal Democrat group was announced as the new leader of the council, with Labour councillor Catherine Gibbons being deputy leader, with these positions to be confirmed at the annual council meeting on 23 May 2023.<ref name=BBC/>
