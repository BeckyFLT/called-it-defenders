The '''2010 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 6 May 2010 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England. It was held on the same day as the [[2010 UK General Election|2010 general election]] and other [[2010 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across the UK]].

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's seats were up for election. The subsequently elected councillors replaced those elected when their individual seats were previously contested in [[2006 Leeds City Council election|2006]].

The result of the election saw the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] gain five council seats and take [[minority government|minority control]] of the council. They held 48 of the 99 total seats and negotiated a [[confidence and supply|confidence-and-supply agreement]] with the two [[Green Party of England and Wales|Green Party]] councillors to achieve a [[majority]]. It replaced a six-year [[coalition government|coalition]] between the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]], which had also been supported by the three Green councillors before the election.<ref name="BBC">{{cite news| url = https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/10177984 | title = Leeds City Council under minority Labour leadership | publisher = BBC News | date=27 May 2010}}</ref><ref name="YEP">{{cite news| url = http://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/news/latest-news/top-stories/leeds-city-council-local-election-results-in-full-1-2244826 | title = Leeds City Council local election: Results in full  | access-date = 2013-01-20 | newspaper = [[Yorkshire Evening Post]]}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{Cite web| url = https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2010/67/ | title = Local Elections 2010: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-20 | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref>
