'''Elections to Wigan Metropolitan Borough Council''' were held on 4 May 2006. One-third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party kept overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite news| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2006/locals/html/bw.stm | title = Local elections: Wigan | access-date = 2009-12-17 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref> Overall turnout was 29.2%.<ref name="results">{{cite web | url = http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | title = Wigan Council results from 1973 to 2008 | publisher = wigan.gov.uk | access-date = 2012-01-30 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20111120022015/http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | archive-date = 20 November 2011 | url-status = dead }}</ref><ref name="wiganmbc">{{cite web | url = http://kinnear.wiganmbc.gov.uk/electionresults/index.asp?ElectionDate=04+May+2006 | title = Local Election Results 04 May 2006 | publisher = wiganmbc.gov.uk | access-date = 2012-06-28 | archive-date = 4 March 2016 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20160304134311/http://kinnear.wiganmbc.gov.uk/electionresults/index.asp?ElectionDate=04+May+2006 | url-status = dead }}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{cite web| url = https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2006/41/ | title = Local Elections Archive Project: 2006 – Wigan | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk |access-date = 2012-06-28}}</ref>
