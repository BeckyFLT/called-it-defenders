The '''2022 North East Lincolnshire Council election''' took place on 5 May 2022 to elect members of [[North East Lincolnshire Council]] in England.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nelincs.gov.uk/your-council/elections-and-voting/election-dates-and-results/north-east-lincolnshire-council-ward-elections-5-may-2022/ |title=North East Lincolnshire Council Ward Elections - 5 May 2022 |access-date=26 May 2022 |work=North East Lincolnshire Council}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2022 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
