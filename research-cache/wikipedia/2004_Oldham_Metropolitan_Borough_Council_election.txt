[[File:Oldham UK local election 2004.svg|thumb|Results of the 2004 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
Elections to [[Oldham Council|Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council]] were held on 10 June 2004.  All seats on the council were contested with boundary changes since the last election in [[2003 Oldham Council election|2003]].<ref name=hold>{{cite web| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2004/locals/html/3660.stm | title = Oldham council | access-date = 2009-12-16 | publisher = [[BBC News Online]]}}</ref> The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] retained overall control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] – 32
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] – 25
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] – 2
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] – 1
