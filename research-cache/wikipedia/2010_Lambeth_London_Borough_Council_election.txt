'''Elections to [[Lambeth London Borough Council]]''' were held on 6 May 2010.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2010/26/|title=Local Elections Archive Project - 2010 - Lambeth|website=www.andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref> All 63 seats were up for election. Turnout was 58%.<ref>[http://moderngov.lambeth.gov.uk/mgElectionResults.aspx?ID=7&RPID=24063206 "Local Elections - Thursday 6 May 2010"]. Lambeth Council. Retrieved 31 March 2014.
</ref> The Labour Party retained control of the council, increasing its majority. The elections took place on the same day as [[2010 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections]] and the [[2010 United Kingdom general election|United Kingdom general election]].
