The '''2019 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England.<ref name="Results_030519">{{cite web | title = Leeds City Council election results 2019 | url = https://www.leeds.gov.uk/your-council/elections/leeds-city-council-election-results  | website = leeds.gov.uk |publisher = [[Leeds City Council]] | date = 3 May 2019}}</ref> It was held on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|UK local elections across England and Northern Ireland]].

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's 99 seats were up for election. This followed the re-election of all of the 99 council seats in the [[2018 Leeds City Council election|2018 all-out council election]]. It had resulted from an [[electoral ward]] [[boundary review]] and saw the election of three councillors for each of the 33 [[electoral wards]] and their new ward boundaries. The third-placed candidate elected in every ward was granted a single year term and their seats are up for election this year.

Elected councillors were granted the usual four-year term until 2023 in order to return to the typical election cycle of elections in three of every four years.<ref>{{cite web |title=LGBCE {{!}} Leeds {{!}} LGBCE Site |url=http://www.lgbce.org.uk/all-reviews/yorkshire-and-the-humber/west-yorkshire/leeds |website=www.lgbce.org.uk |access-date=1 June 2021 |language=en |archive-date=25 May 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180525232556/http://www.lgbce.org.uk/all-reviews/yorkshire-and-the-humber/west-yorkshire/leeds |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url= https://www.leeds-live.co.uk/news/leeds-news/leeds-city-council-election-2019-16100606 |title= Leeds City Council election 2019: Everything you need to know |work= [[Trinity Mirror|LeedsLive]] |date= 9 April 2019}}</ref>

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] maintained their majority control of the council despite losing four seats, also retaining their one [[Calverley and Farsley (ward)|Calverley and Farsley]] councillor by 27 votes.
