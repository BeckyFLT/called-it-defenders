The '''2018 Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2018 to elect members of [[Bolton Council]] in [[Greater Manchester]], England.<ref>{{cite web |title = Notices and results: local election May 2018 |url = http://www.bolton.gov.uk/website/pages/Noticesandresults.aspx | website = bolton.gov.uk |publisher = [[Bolton Council]] |access-date = 22 April 2018 |df = dmy-all}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2018 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]]. The Labour Party retained overall control of the Council with a majority of 1.

21 seats were contested and the Conservative Party won 9 seats, the Labour Party won 8 seats, the Liberal Democrats won 2 seats, and Farnworth and Kearsley First won 2 seats.<ref>{{cite news |title=Four seats keep Labour in control at town hall |work=[[The Bolton News]] |pages=4–5 |date=5 May 2018}}</ref>

After the election, the total composition of the council was as follows:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 31
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 19
*[[UK Independence Party]] 3
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 4
*[[Farnworth and Kearsley First]] 3
