The '''2006 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2006 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One-third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2006/locals/html/29uq.stm | title = Local elections: Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 38
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 9
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 1<ref>{{cite news | first = Graeme | last = Wilson | title = Conservatives Cameron's crusade puts Tories back on victory trail | newspaper = The Daily Telegraph | page = 4 | date = 2006-05-05 }}</ref>
