The '''2003 North Somerset Council election''' took place on 1 May 2003 to elect members of [[North Somerset]] [[Unitary authority|Unitary]] Council in [[Somerset]], [[England]]. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] lost overall control of the council to [[no overall control]].<ref name="lose">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2003/locals/html/189.stm|title=Local elections|publisher=[[BBC News]]|access-date=15 August 2011}}</ref>
