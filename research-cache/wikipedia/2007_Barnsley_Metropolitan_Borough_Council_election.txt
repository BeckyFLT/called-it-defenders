The '''2007 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Barnsley|Barnsley]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[South Yorkshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/vote2007/councils/html/cc.stm|title=Barnsley|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=18 June 2011}}</ref><ref name="results2">{{cite web| url = http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Barnsley-1973-2012.pdf | title = Elections Centre: Barnsley 1973-2012 | publisher = electionscentre.co.uk/plymouth.ac.uk |access-date = 2017-07-25 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20170725171829/http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Barnsley-1973-2012.pdf | archive-date = 2017-07-25}}</ref>

==Background==

Prior to the election, a number of Barnsley Independent Group councillors had removed themselves from the grouping and sat as [[Independent (politician)|Independent]]. Those Independents had earlier lost a seat in a [[2006 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election#By-elections between 2006 and 2007|Penistone by-election]] to the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]], and suffered a defection to Labour in St. Helens. The Conservatives had also suffered a defection to the BIG in Penistone West.<ref name="comp">{{cite web | url = http://www.barnsley.gov.uk/bguk/Council_Democracy/Your_Councillors | title = Your Councillors | publisher = barnsley.gov.uk | access-date = 2017-08-12 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070807150420/http://www.barnsley.gov.uk/bguk/Council_Democracy/Your_Councillors | archive-date = 7 August 2007 | url-status = dead }}</ref>

==Election results==
The results saw Labour retain a majority of 3 with 33 [[councillor]]s, after losing 2 seats to the Barnsley Independent Group.<ref name="slip">{{cite news|url=http://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/around-yorkshire/local-stories/labour_slip_in_barnsley_1_2454542|title=Labour slip in Barnsley|date=4 May 2007|work=[[The Yorkshire Post]]|access-date=20 June 2011}}</ref> The Barnsley Independent Group moved to 20 seats after ousting 2 long time Labour councillors Alan Schofield and Alex Vodden.<ref name=slip/> The Conservatives remained on 5 seats, the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 2 and 3 councillors were [[Independent (politician)|non-aligned]].<ref name=slip/> The [[British National Party]] failed to win any seats, but did come second in the [[Wards of the United Kingdom|wards]] of Central, Dearne North and [[Darton]] West.<ref name=slip/> Overall turnout increased slightly to 33%.<ref name="comp" />
