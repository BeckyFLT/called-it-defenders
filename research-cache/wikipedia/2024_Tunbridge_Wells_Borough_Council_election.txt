The '''2024 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election'''  took place on 2 May 2024 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]] in [[Kent]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://tunbridgewells.gov.uk/council/voting-and-elections/elections/may-2024/notices-of-election/borough |title=Notice of election |date=11 March 2024 |access-date=10 April 2024 |publisher=[[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2024 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].

The council reported borough-wide turnout of 37% and published detailed results for each ward.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Local election results|url=https://tunbridgewells.gov.uk/news/2024/may/local-election-results|website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council|date=2024-05-07|access-date=2 August 2026|language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Election results by party, 2 May 2024|url=https://democracy.tunbridgewells.gov.uk/mgElectionResults.aspx?ID=25&RPID=0|website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council|access-date=2 August 2026|language=en-GB}}</ref>

Following a review of ward boundaries, the election was contested with new electoral divisions and with a reduced number of seats, 39 down from 48.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-kent-68277045|title=Local elections 2024: Maidstone and Tunbridge Wells residents prepare to vote|date=April 9, 2024|website=BBC News}}</ref>

==Summary==
The council was under [[no overall control]] prior to the election, being run by a coalition of the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]], local party the [[Tunbridge Wells Alliance]], and [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]], led by Liberal Democrat councillor Ben Chapelard. The election saw the Liberal Democrats take majority control of the council.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Esson |first1=Daniel |title=Liberal Democrats take control of Tunbridge Wells Borough Council at 2024 local election |url=https://www.kentonline.co.uk/tunbridge-wells/news/liberal-democrats-take-control-of-borough-council-306208/ |access-date=7 July 2024 |work=Kent Online |date=3 May 2024}}</ref>
