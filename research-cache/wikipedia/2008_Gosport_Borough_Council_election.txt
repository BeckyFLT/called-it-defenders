Elections to [[Gosport]] Borough Council in [[Hampshire]], England were held on 1 May 2008.  Half of the council was up for election and the council stayed under [[no overall control]].

Before the election the Labour and Liberal Democrat parties had held power on the council with the [[casting vote]] of the mayor.<ref name=coalition>{{cite web| url = http://www.portsmouth.co.uk/local-elections/Election-2008-Coalition-on-cards.4044440.jp | title = Election 2008: Coalition on cards again as Gosport Lib Dems close gap | access-date = 2008-05-10 | date = 2008-05-02| publisher = [[The News (Portsmouth)|The News]]}}</ref> The election saw the Liberal Democrats close the gap with the Conservative Party after making 5 gains, while the Labour Party lost four of the five seats they had held. However following the election the Conservative Party said that they would take charge of the council as they remained the largest party.<ref name=power>{{cite web| url = http://www.portsmouth.co.uk/local-elections/Gosport-Tories-take-power.4046071.jp | title = Gosport Tories take power | access-date = 2008-05-10 | date = 2008-05-02| publisher = [[The News (Portsmouth)|The News]]}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 16
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 14
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 4
