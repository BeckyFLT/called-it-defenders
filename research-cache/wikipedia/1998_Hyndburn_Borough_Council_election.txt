{{main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}

'''Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council''' were held on 7 May 1998.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party stayed in overall control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 35
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 12
