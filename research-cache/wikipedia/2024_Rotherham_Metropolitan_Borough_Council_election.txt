The '''2024 Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' was held on Thursday 2 May 2024,<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Elections in Rotherham |url=https://www.rotherham.gov.uk/elections-voting/upcoming-elections |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231230141930/https://www.rotherham.gov.uk/elections-voting/upcoming-elections |archive-date=30 Dec 2023 |access-date=30 Dec 2023 |website=Rotherham Council}}</ref> alongside the [[2024 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections in the United Kingdom]], which were held on the same day. It elected all 59 councillors to the [[Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council]] for a four-year term, ending in 2028.<ref name=":0" /> Labour retained their majority on the council.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Rotherham election result |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/election/2024/england/councils/E08000018}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Andrews |first1=Danielle |title=Local elections 2024: What do results mean for Rotherham? |url=https://www.thestar.co.uk/news/politics/local-elections-2024-what-do-results-mean-for-rotherham-4618876 |access-date=9 June 2024 |work=The Star |date=7 May 2024}}</ref>

== Electoral process ==
The council elects its councillors every four years.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.gov.uk/guidance/local-government-structure-and-elections#electoral-cycles|title=Local government structure and elections|work=GOV.UK|access-date=2018-04-27|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/694598/Election_Timetable_in_England.pdf|title=Election Timetable in England}}</ref> The election took place by [[first-past-the-post voting]], with wards being represented by two or three councillors.

All [[Elections in the United Kingdom#Registration procedure|registered electors]] ([[British citizen|British]], [[Irish citizen|Irish]], [[Commonwealth citizen|Commonwealth]] and [[European Union]] citizens) living in Rotherham Borough aged 18 or over were entitled to vote in the election. People who live at two addresses in different councils, such as university students with different term-time and holiday addresses, are entitled to be registered for and vote in elections in both local authorities. Voting in-person at polling stations took place from 07:00 to 22:00 on election day, and voters were able to apply for postal votes or proxy votes in advance of the election.
