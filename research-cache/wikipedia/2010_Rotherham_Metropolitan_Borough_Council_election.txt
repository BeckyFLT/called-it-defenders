The '''2010 Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2010 to elect members of [[Rotherham]] Council in [[South Yorkshire]], England as part of the [[2010 United Kingdom local elections]]. One third of the council was up for election.

After the election, the composition of the council was:<ref name="BBC Election 2010 Results">[https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/election2010/council/html/3744.stm BBC Election 2010 Results],</ref>
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 50
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 10
*[[British National Party|BNP]] 1
*Others 2
