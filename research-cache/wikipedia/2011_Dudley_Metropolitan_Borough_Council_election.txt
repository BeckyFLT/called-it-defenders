The '''2011 Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council''' election was held on 5 May 2011 to elect members of [[Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in the [[West Midlands (county)|West Midlands]], [[England]], as part of the [[2011 United Kingdom local elections]]. 24 seats were up for election, and the results saw the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] hold on to a slightly reduced majority of 15.
