[[File:Newcastle-under-Lyme UK local election 2007.svg|thumb|Results of the 2007 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election]]
'''Elections to Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council''' were held on 3 May 2007.  One third of the council was up for election and the council stayed under [[no overall control]].

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 20
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 20
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 18
*[[UK Independence Party]] 2
