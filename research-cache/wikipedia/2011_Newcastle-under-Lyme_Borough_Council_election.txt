The '''2011 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election''' was held on Thursday 5 May 2011 to elect 21 members to [[Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council]], the same day as other [[2011 United Kingdom local elections|local elections in the United Kingdom]]. It elected one-third of the council's 60 members to a four-year term and one additional member in Kidsgrove Ward to a one-year term following the resignation of Tina Morrey.<ref name=LEAP1>{{citeweb|title=Kidsgrove Ward - Newcastle-under-Lyme|url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/ward/5125/|publisher=Local Elections Archive Project|last=Teale|first=Andrew|accessdate=4 July 2025}}</ref> The election for Seabridge Ward was postponed until 23 June 2011 following the death of a candidate.<ref name=LEAP3>{{citeweb|title=Seabridge Ward - Newcastle-under-Lyme|url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/ward/5133/|publisher=Local Elections Archive Project|last=Teale|first=Andrew|accessdate=4 July 2025}}</ref> It was preceded by the [[2010 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election|2010 election]] and followed by the [[2012 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election|2012 election]]. The council remained under [[no overall control]].<ref name=HoC>{{citeweb|title=Local elections 2011|url=https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/RP11-43/RP11-43.pdf|date=24 May 2011|last=Tetteh|first=Edmund|publisher=The House of Commons Library|accessdate = 4 July 2025}}</ref> Turnout across the council was 36.3%.<ref name=TEC1>{{citeweb|title=Local Elections Handbook 2011|url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Local-Elections-Handbook-2011-complete.pdf|last1=Rallings|first1=Colin|last2=Thrasher|first2=Michael|publisher=The Elections Centre|accessdate = 4 July 2025}}</ref>
