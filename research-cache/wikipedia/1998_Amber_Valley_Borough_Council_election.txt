The '''1998 Amber Valley Borough Council election''' took place on 7 May 1998 to elect members of [[Amber Valley]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Derbyshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party kept overall control of the council.<ref name=policy>{{cite news | title = Policy and politics: Local Elections: Analysis: Council poll results | publisher = [[The Guardian]] | page = 16 | date = 1998-05-09 }}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 37
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 6<ref name=policy/>
