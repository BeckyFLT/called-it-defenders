'''Elections to Wigan Council''' were held on 6 May 2010. One-third of the council was up for election.<ref>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/election2010/council/html/3791.stm | title = Election 2010: Wigan council | access-date = 2012-07-04 | publisher = BBC News | date=19 April 2009}}</ref><ref name="wiganmbc">{{cite web | url = http://kinnear.wiganmbc.gov.uk/electionresults/ShowWard.asp?ElectionDate=06+May+2010 | archive-url = https://archive.today/20121223221027/http://kinnear.wiganmbc.gov.uk/electionresults/ShowWard.asp?ElectionDate=06+May+2010 | url-status = dead | archive-date = 23 December 2012 | title = Local Election Results 06 May 2010 | publisher = wiganmbc.gov.uk | access-date = 2012-06-28 }}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{cite web| url = http://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2010/41/ | title = Local Elections Archive Project: 2010 – Wigan | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk |access-date = 2012-06-28}}</ref>
