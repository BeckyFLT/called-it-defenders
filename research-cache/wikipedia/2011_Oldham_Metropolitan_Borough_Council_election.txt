[[File:Oldham UK local election 2011.svg|thumb|Results of the 2011 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] Council''' were held on 5 May 2011. One third of the council was up for election.

[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] gained overall control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was,<ref>{{Cite web |title=BBC News - Election 2011 - England council elections - Oldham |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/election2011/council/html/bp.stm |access-date=2026-01-19 |website=BBC News |language=en-GB}}</ref>

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 34
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 21
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 4
*[[Independent politician|Independent]] 1
