The '''2019 North Somerset Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[North Somerset Council]] in England.

North Somerset unitary council is elected every four years, with fifty [[councillor]]s to be elected. Since the first election to the unitary authority in 1995, the council has either been under [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] control, or no party has held a majority. The Conservatives gained a majority at the [[2007 North Somerset Council election|2007 election]] and retained control for 12 years. Following these elections, a "rainbow coalition" of Independents, Liberal Democrats, Labour and Green councillors replaced the previous Conservative administration.

== Background ==
North Somerset Council held local elections on 2 May 2019 along with councils across England as part of the [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|2019 local elections]]. The council elects its members in all-out elections, with all fifty of its councillors up for election every four years. Councillors defending their seats in this election were previously elected in [[2015 North Somerset Council election|2015]]. In that election, thirty-six Conservative councillors, six independent councillors, four Liberal Democrat councillors, three Labour councillors and one Green councillor were elected. In subsequent by-elections, Labour gained one seat from the Conservatives.<ref>Brain, Nicholas (1 September 2017). "[http://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/0917-election-of-a-distritc-councillor-for-Weston-super-Mare-North-Worle.pdf North Worle by-election] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170902002751/http://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/0917-election-of-a-distritc-councillor-for-Weston-super-Mare-North-Worle.pdf |date=2 September 2017 }}" (PDF). North Somerset Council. Retrieved 1 September 2017.</ref>

In the build up to the 2019 election, ten incumbent councillors made up of eight Conservative councillors, one Liberal Democrat councillor and the sole Green councillor announced they would not be seeking re-election.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://www.thewestonmercury.co.uk/north-somerset-council-local-election-2019-candidates-1-5978668|title=ELECTIONS 2019: All the candidates you can vote for tomorrow|last=Young|first=Eleanor|website=Weston Mercury|date=May 2019 |language=en|access-date=2019-05-10}}</ref> Additionally, a group of independent councillors representing the town of [[Portishead, Somerset|Portishead]] received approval from the [[Electoral Commission (United Kingdom)|Electoral Commission]] to be listed as "Portishead Independents" on ballots,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.northsomersettimes.co.uk/news/portishead-independents-to-stand-in-council-elections-in-2019-1-5514108|title=New political group to stand in Portishead elections|last=Wright|first=Tom|website=North Somerset Times|date=12 May 2018 |language=en|access-date=2019-05-10}}</ref> and the group listed candidates in three of the four wards in their town.<ref name=":0" />
