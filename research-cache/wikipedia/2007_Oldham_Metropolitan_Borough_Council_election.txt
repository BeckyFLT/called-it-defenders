'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] Metropolitan Borough Council''' were held on 3 May 2007.  One third of the Council was up for election.

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] lost overall control of the Council.

After the election, the composition of the council was

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 30
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 26
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 3
*[[Independent (politician)]] 1
