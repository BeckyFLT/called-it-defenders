The '''1999 Oldham Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of  [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[Greater Manchester]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite news| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/178.stm | title = Oldham | access-date = 2010-04-09 | work = [[BBC News Online]]}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 33
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 26
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 1<ref name=britain>{{cite news | title = How Britain voted: Council Election Results | newspaper = [[The Independent]] | page = 12 | date = 1999-05-08 }}</ref>

==Election result==
Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] in the election was 32.7%.<ref name=results>{{cite web | url = http://www.oldham.gov.uk/local_govt/councillors/results.htm | title = Oldham MBC Local Election Results 6th May 1999 | access-date = 2010-04-10 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20000615034125/http://www.oldham.gov.uk/local_govt/councillors/results.htm | archive-date = 15 June 2000 | publisher = Oldham Metropolitan Borough | url-status = dead | df = dmy-all }}</ref>
