The '''2018 Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2018 to elect members of [[Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref>{{cite web |title = Elections 2018 |url = http://www.dudley.gov.uk/community/elections/democracy-elections/elections-2018/ | website = dudley.gov.uk |publisher = [[Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council]] |access-date = 24 April 2018 |df = dmy-all}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2018 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
