The '''2006 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2006 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="election2006">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2006/locals/html/12ue.stm|title=Local elections: Huntingdonshire|date=4 May 2006|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=5 July 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 40
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 10
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 2<ref name="councillors">{{cite web|url=http://www.huntsdc.gov.uk/Councils+and+Democracy/Council/Councillors |title=Councillors |work=Huntingdonshire District Council |url-status=dead |access-date=1 October 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040827123501/http://www.huntsdc.gov.uk/Councils%2Band%2BDemocracy/Council/Councillors |archive-date=27 August 2004 |df=dmy-all }}</ref>

==Election result==
The [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] gain in [[Huntingdon]] East by David Priestman was the first time the party had won a seat in the town of Huntingdon on the council.<ref name="history">{{cite news|title=Election history is made|date=5 May 2007|work=[[Peterborough Evening Telegraph]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]<!--|access-date=6 July 2014-->}}</ref>
