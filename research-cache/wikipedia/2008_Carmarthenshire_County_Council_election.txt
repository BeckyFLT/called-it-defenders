[[File:Carmarthenshire UK ward map 2008.svg|thumb|Results of the 2008 Carmarthenshire County Council election]]
The '''fourth election to [[Carmarthenshire County Council]]''' was held on 1 May 2008.<ref name="2008 ccc results">{{cite web|title=Election Archive |url=http://www.carmarthenshire.gov.uk/English/council/electionsandvoting/Documents/County%20Council%20Election%20Results%201st%20May%202008.pdf |publisher=Carmarthenshire County Council |access-date=11 March 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140311181821/http://www.carmarthenshire.gov.uk/English/council/electionsandvoting/Documents/County%20Council%20Election%20Results%201st%20May%202008.pdf |archive-date=11 March 2014 }} These results were removed from the council website in 2015.</ref> It was preceded by the [[2004 Carmarthenshire County Council election|2004 election]] and followed by the [[2012 Carmarthenshire County Council election|2012 election]]. While the Independent councillors again had the largest number of seats, Plaid Cymru gained considerable ground, notably in the Llanelli and Ammanford areas. The Independents formed a coalition with Labour.

Full results of the election were published in the local press.<ref>{{cite news|title=Results|newspaper=South Wales Evening Post|date=2 May 2008}}</ref>
