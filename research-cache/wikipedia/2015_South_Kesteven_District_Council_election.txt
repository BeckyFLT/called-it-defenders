The '''2015 South Kesteven District Council election''' took place on 7 May 2015 to elect members of [[South Kesteven]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Lincolnshire]], [[England]]. The whole council was up for election after boundary changes reduced the number of seats by two.<ref name="fire">{{cite news|url=http://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/local/new-south-kesteven-district-council-boundaries-come-under-fire-1-6683339|title=New South Kesteven District Council boundaries come under fire|last=Hawkins|first=Judith|date=11 April 2015|work=Grantham Journal|access-date=11 May 2015}}</ref> The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="boston">{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-lincolnshire-32612952|title=Election 2015: UKIP tie with Tories in Boston local elections|date=8 May 2015|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=12 May 2015}}</ref>

==Background==
After the [[2011 South Kesteven District Council election|previous election in 2011]] the Conservatives remained in control of the council with 38 seats, compared to 12 [[Independent (politician)|independents]], 6 for [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] and 1 each for the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] and [[Lincolnshire Independents]].<ref name="authority">{{cite news|title=Local authority elections: England|date=7 May 2011|work=[[The Guardian]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref><ref name="results2011">{{cite web|url=http://www.southkesteven.gov.uk/CHttpHandler.ashx?id=4830&p=0|title=Declaration of result of poll|publisher=South Kesteven District Council|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref><ref name="biting">{{cite news|title=Tories lose by-elections in nail-biting night|date=24 June 2011|work=[[The Independent]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref> By the time of the 2015 election there were two [[UK Independence Party]] [[councillor]]s, after Mike and Jean Taylor [[Defection|defected]] from the Conservatives in January 2015, following their failure to be re-selected as Conservative candidates for the council election.<ref name="selected">{{cite news|url=http://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/local/husband-and-wife-councillors-not-selected-by-conservatives-for-next-election-in-grantham-1-6310883|title=Husband and wife councillors not selected by Conservatives for next election in Grantham|last=Newton|first=Graham|date=19 September 2014|work=Grantham Journal|access-date=13 May 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924023603/http://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/local/husband-and-wife-councillors-not-selected-by-conservatives-for-next-election-in-grantham-1-6310883|archive-date=24 September 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="defect">{{cite news|url=http://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/politics/politics-news/breaking-news-grantham-councillors-defect-to-ukip-from-the-conservative-party-1-6545842|title=Breaking news: Grantham councillors defect to UKIP from the Conservative Party|date=27 January 2015|work=Grantham Journal|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref> Meanwhile, in [[Harrowby, Lincolnshire|Harrowby]] [[Wards and electoral divisions of the United Kingdom|ward]] the Labour councillors Ian Selby and Bruce Wells stood as independents after they were [[Preselection|deselected]] by Labour.<ref name="selby">{{cite news|url=http://www.lincolnshireecho.co.uk/Grantham-s-Mayor-Ian-Selby-announces-general/story-26302466-detail/story.html|title=Grantham's Mayor Ian Selby announces general election challenge as an independent candidate|date=8 April 2015|work=[[Lincolnshire Echo]]|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref>

The Conservative leader of the council Linda Neal stood down at the 2015 election, with Bob Adams being chosen to succeed her as Conservative group leader.<ref name="adams">{{cite news|url=http://www.lincolnshireecho.co.uk/Bob-Adams-named-South-Kesteven-Tories-new-group/story-26331713-detail/story.html|title=Bob Adams named by South Kesteven council Conservatives as new group leader|date=14 April 2015|work=[[Lincolnshire Echo]]|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref> A seat in [[Bourne, Lincolnshire|Bourne]] West was vacant by the election after the death of Conservative councillor John Smith,<ref name="smith">{{cite news|url=http://www.lincolnshireecho.co.uk/Lincolnshire-councillor-John-Smith-dies/story-26302849-detail/story.html|title=Lincolnshire councillor for SKDC, John Smith, dies|date=8 April 2015|work=[[Lincolnshire Echo]]|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref> while in [[Market Deeping|Market]] and [[West Deeping]] ward the election was postponed until 25 June after the death of independent councillor Reg Howard.<ref name="tributes">{{cite news|url=http://www.stamfordmercury.co.uk/news/local/tributes-paid-to-long-serving-market-deeping-councillor-reg-howard-1-6690808|title=Tributes paid to long-serving Market Deeping councillor Reg Howard|last=Moore|first=Alex|date=15 April 2015|work=[[Stamford Mercury]]|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref><ref name="enough">{{cite news|url=http://www.bournelocal.co.uk/news/local/call-to-leave-enough-time-to-vote-in-may-7-elections-1-6715289|title=Call to leave enough time to vote in May 7 elections|last=Moore|first=Alex|date=29 April 2015|work=The Local|access-date=13 May 2015}}</ref>

Boundary changes since the 2011 election reduced the number of councillors from 58 to 56,<ref name=fire/> while the number of wards declined from 34 to 30.<ref name="review">{{cite news|url=http://www.southkesteven.gov.uk/index.aspx?articleid=7298|title=Review of Polling Districts and Polling Places|date=22 August 2014|publisher=South Kesteven District Council|access-date=13 May 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150701024505/http://www.southkesteven.gov.uk/index.aspx?articleid=7298|archive-date=1 July 2015|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref>

==Election result==
The Conservatives kept a large majority on the council after winning 44 of the 53 seats contested on 7 May.<ref name="dominate">{{cite news|url=http://www.lincolnshireecho.co.uk/Conservatives-dominate-South-Kesteven-District/story-26466647-detail/story.html#comments|title=Conservatives dominate South Kesteven District Council elections|date=8 May 2015|work=[[Lincolnshire Echo]]|access-date=12 May 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150512190426/http://www.lincolnshireecho.co.uk/Conservatives-dominate-South-Kesteven-District/story-26466647-detail/story.html#comments|archive-date=12 May 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> The three seats in Market and West Deeping elected on 25 June had two independents and one Conservative councillors elected.<ref name="deeping">{{cite web|url=http://www.southkesteven.gov.uk/CHttpHandler.ashx?id=14790&p=0|title=Declaration of result of poll|publisher=South Kesteven District Council|access-date=28 June 2015}}</ref>
