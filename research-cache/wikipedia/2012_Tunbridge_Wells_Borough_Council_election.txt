The '''2012 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on Thursday 3 May 2012 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election.
