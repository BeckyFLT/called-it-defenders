[[File:Rotherham UK ward map 2012.svg|thumb|Results of the 2012 Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
The '''2012 Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2012 to elect members of [[Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council]] in [[South Yorkshire]], England.<ref name="BBC">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-17299200 |title=Local elections 2012 |access-date=2012-04-30 |publisher=BBC News}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2012 United Kingdom local elections]]. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] gained five seats, and one was gained by an [[Independent (politician)|Independent]]. The [[British National Party]] lost both of the seats it was defending, and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] only held one of its five seats, in Sitwell ward.
