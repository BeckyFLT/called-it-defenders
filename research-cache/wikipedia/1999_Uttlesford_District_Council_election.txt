The '''1999 Uttlesford District Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[Uttlesford District Council]] in England.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Uttlesford-1973-2011.pdf |title=Results 1973 to 2011|work=Colin Rallings and Michael Thrasher |publisher=The Elections Centre, [[Plymouth University]] |access-date=14 January 2023}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[1999 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
