The '''2026 Wandsworth London Borough Council election''' took place on 7 May 2026.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-01-01 |title=Local elections in London will test support for Reform UK and Greens |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c99k0mzj7x8o |access-date=2026-01-19 |website=[[BBC News]] |language=en-GB}}</ref> 58 members of [[Wandsworth London Borough Council]] were elected. The elections took place alongside [[2026 London local elections|local elections in the other London boroughs]] and [[2026 United Kingdom local elections|elections to local authorities across the United Kingdom]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Number of councils that have requested delay to local elections revealed - is yours one of them? |url=https://news.sky.com/story/local-elections-2026-over-a-third-of-councils-offered-a-delay-have-requested-one-is-yours-on-the-list-13494762 |access-date=2026-01-19 |website=Sky News |language=en}}</ref>

== Background ==
{{See also|Wandsworth London Borough Council elections}}
