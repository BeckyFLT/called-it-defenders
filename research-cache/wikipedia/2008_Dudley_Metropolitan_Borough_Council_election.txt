'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Dudley|Dudley]] Metropolitan Borough Council''' were held on 1 May 2008. One third of the council was up for election, with an additional seat in Sedgley ward following the resignation of a councillor.

Following the election the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] held 43 seats, the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] held 26 seats, the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] held 2 seats, and the [[United Kingdom Independence Party|UK Independence Party]] held one seat.
