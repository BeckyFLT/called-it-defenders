'''Elections to [[Gosport Borough Council]]''' took place on 6 May 2021 as part of the [[2021 United Kingdom local elections]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=|title=Election Timetable in England|url=https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/920186/Election_Timetable_in_England_2019.pdf|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200924235544/https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/920186/Election_Timetable_in_England_2019.pdf |archive-date=24 September 2020 |access-date=|website=}}</ref> These took place at the same time as the [[2021 Hampshire County Council election|elections]] for [[Hampshire County Council]] and the [[Hampshire Police and Crime Commissioner]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Elections and voting|url=https://www.hants.gov.uk/en/aboutthecouncil/electionsandvoting|access-date=2021-01-04|website=www.hants.gov.uk|language=en-GB}}</ref>

== Background ==
The previous election, held in [[2018 Gosport Borough Council election|2018]], saw the Liberal Democrats gain four seats: two from Conservatives, and one each from the Labour and UKIP. This reduced the Conservative Majority to one, remaining in control of the council, whilst UKIP lost their sole seat, in the Rowner and Holbrook ward.

The Statement of Persons Nominated was published on 9 April 2021.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Statement of Persons Nominated|url=https://www.gosport.gov.uk/media/3029/2021-GBC-Statement-of-Persons-Nominated-for-Gosport-Wards-v2-08-04-2021-/pdf/2021_GBC_-_Statement_of_Persons_Nominated_for_Gosport_Wards_v2_(08_04_2021).pdf?m=637535086420500000|website=[[Gosport Borough Council]]}}</ref>
