The '''2011 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' was held on Thursday 5 May 2011 to elect 17 members to [[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]], the same day as other [[2011 United Kingdom local elections|local elections in the United Kingdom]]. It elected approximately one-third of the council to a four-year term, including 16 regularly scheduled elections and a by-election in Brenchley and Horsmonden. It was preceded by the [[2010 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election|2010 election]] and followed by the [[2012 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election|2012 election]]. The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] held control of the council.<ref name=HoC>{{citeweb|title=Local elections 2011|url=https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/RP11-43/RP11-43.pdf|date=24 May 2011|last=Tetteh|first=Edmund|publisher=The House of Commons Library|accessdate =15 July 2025}}</ref> Turnout across the wards up for election was 46.2%.<ref name=TEC1>{{citeweb|title=Local Elections Handbook 2011|url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Local-Elections-Handbook-2011-complete.pdf|last1=Rallings|first1=Colin|last2=Thrasher|first2=Michael|publisher=The Elections Centre|accessdate = 15 July 2025}}</ref>
