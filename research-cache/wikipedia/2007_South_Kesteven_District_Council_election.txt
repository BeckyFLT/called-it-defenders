The '''2007 South Kesteven District Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[South Kesteven]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Lincolnshire]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="vote2007">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/vote2007/councils/html/32ug.stm|title=South Kesteven|date=4 May 2007|publisher=BBC News|access-date=9 May 2015}}</ref>

==Election result==
The Conservatives kept a majority on the council making a net gain of one seat to have 35 [[councillor]]s.<ref name="retain">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/england/lincolnshire/6603745.stm|title=Tories retain South Kesteven vote|date=4 May 2007|publisher=BBC News|access-date=10 May 2015}}</ref> The [[Independent (politician)|independents]] and [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] also made gains at the expense of [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] who lost five of the seven seats they were defending.<ref name=retain/> Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] at the election was 35%,<ref name="collapses">{{cite news|title=Labour loses its grip on power as support across region collapses|date=5 May 2007|work=[[Peterborough Evening Telegraph]]|via=NewsBank}}</ref> up from 29.65% at the [[2003 South Kesteven District Council election|last election in 2003]].<ref name="vote2003">{{cite web|url=http://www.politicsresources.net/area/uk/loc03/pages/SouthKesteven/electionresults.htm|title=May 1st 2003 – Ward and Parish Election Results|publisher=South Kesteven District Council|via=Political Science Resources|access-date=10 May 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304112142/http://www.politicsresources.net/area/uk/loc03/pages/SouthKesteven/electionresults.htm|archive-date=4 March 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The defeated Labour candidates included the group leader John Hurst, who lost his seat in [[Grantham]] St Johns to the Conservatives, and the party's deputy leader Rob Shorrock in Earlesfield, where independent candidates took two of the three seats for the [[Wards and electoral divisions of the United Kingdom|ward]].<ref name="deputy">{{cite news|url=http://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/features/news-features/labour-group-leader-and-deputy-lose-their-seats-1-6984|title=Labour Group leader and deputy lose their seats|date=4 May 2007|work=Grantham Journal|access-date=10 May 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150510140627/http://www.granthamjournal.co.uk/news/features/news-features/labour-group-leader-and-deputy-lose-their-seats-1-6984|archive-date=10 May 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Labour also lost seats in St Anne's ward to an independent and a Conservative, as well as a seat in Isaac Newton ward to the Conservatives, however Labour did gain a seat in [[Harrowby, Lincolnshire|Harrowby]].<ref name=deputy/> Other changes had Conservative council [[Cabinet (government)|cabinet]] member Terl Bryant lose in All Saints ward, but a Conservative candidate defeated independent councillor Angeline Percival in Glen Eden.<ref name=deputy/>
