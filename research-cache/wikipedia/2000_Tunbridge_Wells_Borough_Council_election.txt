The '''2000 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2000 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite web| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/uk_politics/vote2000/locals/131.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 31
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 11
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 5
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1<ref name=council>{{cite news | title = Local council results | work = Financial Times | page = 6 | date = 2000-05-06 }}</ref>
