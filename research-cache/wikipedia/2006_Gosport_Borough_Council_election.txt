}}Elections to [[Gosport]] Borough Council were held on 4 May 2006.  Half of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] Party lost overall control of the council to [[no overall control]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2006-05-04 |title=Local elections: Gosport |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2006/locals/html/24uf.stm |access-date=2022-07-07 |publisher=BBC News}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2006-05-05 |title=Borough Council Election – 4th May 2006 – Results |url=http://www.gosport.gov.uk/sections/your-council/council-services/electoral-registration/election---4th-may/results |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930041446/http://www.gosport.gov.uk/sections/your-council/council-services/electoral-registration/election---4th-may/results |archive-date=2007-09-30 |access-date=2022-07-07 |publisher=Gosport Borough Council}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book |last=Rallings |first=Colin |url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Gosport-1973-2012.pdf |title=Gosport Borough Council Election Results 1973-2012 |last2=Thrasher |first2=Michael |publisher=The Elections Centre |year=2012 |location=Plymouth |pages=8–9 |access-date=2022-07-07}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 17
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 9
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 8
