'''Elections to [[Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council]]''' were held on 4 May 2006.  One third of the council was up for election and the council stayed under no overall control, with the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] overtaking the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] as the largest party and resuming control of the council after a two-year break.

20 seats were contested, with the Labour Party winning 10 seats, the Conservatives 7 and the Liberal Democrats 3
Overall turnout was 34.3%.

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 22
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 21
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 17
