[[File:Wigan UK local election 1979 map.png|right|thumb|500px|Map of the results for the 1979 Wigan council election. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] in red, [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] in blue and the [[Liberal Party (UK)|Liberals]] in yellow.]]
Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Wigan|Wigan]] Council were held on 3 May 1979, with one third of the council up for vote as well as an extra vacancy in Ward 22. The election seen [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] strengthening their grip, with six gains - mostly in the wards they lost seats in at the [[1975 Wigan Council election|1975 election]] - and one loss. All but one of the gains were from the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]], with the other being from the [[Liberal Party (UK)|Liberal's]] sole seat in Ward 14. A Liberal gain in Ward 15 from Labour kept them represented on the council. Owing to [[1979 United Kingdom general election|general election]] on the same day, turnout was significantly up, to 75.7% and all wards were contested - a continuation of the feat started last election.<ref name="results">{{cite web | url = http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | title = Wigan Council results from 1973 to 2008 | publisher = wigan.gov.uk | access-date = 2012-01-30 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20111120022015/http://www.wigan.gov.uk/NR/rdonlyres/3692F771-E969-41EE-B068-1F9F20DFE0B7/0/WiganResults1973to2007.pdf | archive-date = 2011-11-20 | url-status=dead }}</ref>
