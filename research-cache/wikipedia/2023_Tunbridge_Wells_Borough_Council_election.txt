The '''2023 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]] in [[Kent]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://tunbridgewells.gov.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/442458/Notice-of-Election-Borough.pdf |title=Notice of election |date=20 March 2023 |access-date=13 April 2023 |publisher=[[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] across England.

The council's detailed declarations recorded 26,575 ballot papers issued from an electorate of 71,657, an overall turnout of 37.1%.<ref>{{cite web |title=Results |url=https://tunbridgewells.gov.uk/council/voting-and-elections/elections/may-2023/results |website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council |access-date=2 August 2026}}</ref>

The council remained under [[no overall control]], being led by a coalition of the Liberal Democrats, local party the Tunbridge Wells Alliance, Labour and an independent councillor, led by Ben Chapelard of the Liberal Democrats.<ref>{{cite web |title=Your Councillors by Party |url=https://democracy.tunbridgewells.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=PARTY&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council |access-date=1 October 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230324052743/https://democracy.tunbridgewells.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=PARTY&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |archive-date=24 March 2023}}</ref> The council remained under no overall control after the election and continued to be run by the same coalition.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Thorne |first1=Lucy |title=Tunbridge Wells local election results 2023 |url=https://www.kentlive.news/news/kent-news/tunbridge-wells-local-election-results-8413931 |access-date=1 October 2023 |work=Kent Live |date=5 May 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Borough Partnership continues following local elections |url=https://tunbridgewells.gov.uk/news/2023/may/borough-partnership-continues-following-local-elections |website=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council |access-date=1 October 2023 |date=May 2023}}</ref>
