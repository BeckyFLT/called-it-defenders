'''Elections to [[Lambeth London Borough Council]]''' were held on 7 May 1998. The whole council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] took overall control of the council.<ref name=results>{{cite news | title = Local Elections results | work = [[The Times]] | page = 46 | date = 9 May 1998 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/34120/response/88477/attach/3/Election%20of%20Councillors%207th%20May%201998.pdf|title = Councillors – a Freedom of Information request to Lambeth Borough Council|date = 27 April 2010}}</ref>
