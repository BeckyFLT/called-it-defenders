Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Dudley|Dudley]] Metropolitan Borough Council were held on 7 May 1998.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party kept overall control of the council.<ref name=results>{{Cite news | title = Local Elections results | work = [[The Times]] | page = 46 | date = 1998-05-09 }}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 58
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 7
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 7
