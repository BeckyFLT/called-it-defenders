The '''2019 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect one third of [[Tunbridge Wells Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="Tunbridge Wells Borough Council">{{Cite web|url=https://democracy.tunbridgewells.gov.uk/meetings/mgElectionResults.aspx?ID=13&V=1&RPID=9783495 |title=Borough Election, May 2019 - Thursday, 2nd May, 2019 |access-date=3 May 2019 |work=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council}}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{Cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/topics/c7ry2pm3072t |title=Tunbridge Wells Borough Council |access-date=3 May 2019 |work=BBC}}</ref> The Conservatives retained control of the council, but with a reduced majority.
