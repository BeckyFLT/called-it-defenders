The '''2006 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday, May 4, 2006 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in England.

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's 99 seats were contested, plus an additional vacancy in [[Killingbeck and Seacroft (ward)|Killingbeck & Seacroft]] [[electoral ward|ward]]. Those seats up for election were those of the third-placed candidate elected for every ward at the [[2004 Leeds City Council election|2004 all-out election]], who had been granted a two-year term to expire in 2006.

The only party to gain a seat was the [[British National Party]], defeating the [[Morley Borough Independents]] in [[Morley South (ward)|Morley South]] to elect their first and only councillor to date.

With the council remaining in [[no overall control]], the election result saw the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] and [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] [[coalition government|coalition administration]] continue their control of the council.<ref name="archive">{{cite web| url = http://leeds.gov.uk/files/Internet2007/2010/11/lcc%204th%20may%202006.pdf | title = Leeds City Council Elections - 4th May 2006 - Results | publisher = leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 2013-01-13 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20120621232314/http://leeds.gov.uk/files/Internet2007/2010/11/lcc%204th%20may%202006.pdf | archive-date = 2012-06-21}}</ref><ref name="results">{{cite news| url = http://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/news/latest-news/top-stories/local-election-results-2006-1-2156212 | title = Local Election Results 2006 | access-date = 2013-01-11 | newspaper = [[Yorkshire Evening Post]]}}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{cite news| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2006/locals/html/da.stm | title = Local elections: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-13 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref><ref name="LEAP">{{Cite web| url = http://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2006/67/ | title = Local Elections 2006: Leeds | access-date = 2013-01-13 | publisher = andrewteale.me.uk}}</ref>
