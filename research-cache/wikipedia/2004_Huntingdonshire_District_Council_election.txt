[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] in blue, [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] in yellow and [[Independent (politician)|independents]] in grey.]]
The '''2004 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 10 June 2004 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. The whole council was up for election after boundary changes reduced the number of seats by 1.<ref name="vote2004">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2004/locals/html/3706.stm|title=Huntingdonshire council|date=12 June 2004|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=3 July 2014}}</ref> The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="ballot">{{cite news|title=Ballot box - Elections|date=12 June 2004|work=[[The Times]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]|page=26}}</ref>
