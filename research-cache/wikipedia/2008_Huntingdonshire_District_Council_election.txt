The '''2008 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 1 May 2008 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="elections2008">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/elections/local_council/08/html/12ue.stm|title=Huntingdonshire|date=2 May 2008|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=6 July 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 38
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 12
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 2<ref name="full">{{cite news|title=Full election results|date=3 May 2008|work=[[The Guardian]]|publisher=[[The Guardian]]}}</ref>

==Background==
Before the election the Conservatives controlled the council with 39 seats, compared to 11 [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] and 2 [[Independent (politician)|independents]].<ref name="seeking">{{cite news|title=Two leaders are seeking re-election|date=10 April 2008|work=[[Peterborough Evening Telegraph]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref> 18 seats were contested at the election by a total of 69 candidates, with the Conservatives defending 14 of the seats.<ref name=seeking/><ref name="hdc">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/hunts-life/sixty_nine_candidates_to_contest_hdc_seats_in_may_1_414388|title=Sixty-nine candidates to contest HDC seats in May|date=4 April 2008|work=Hunts Post|access-date=8 July 2014}}</ref>

Among the [[councillor]]s who were defending seats were the Conservative leader of the council, Ian Bates, in The Hemingfords [[Wards and electoral divisions of the United Kingdom|ward]], and the leader of the Liberal Democrats on the council, Peter Downes in [[Brampton, Cambridgeshire|Brampton]].<ref name=seeking/> Ian Bates was challenged by an independent candidate, Maxine Hay, who had become a road safety campaigner after her son was killed after being hit by a car, with the Liberal Democrat candidate for the seat withdrawing in her favour.<ref name="tactical">{{cite news|title=Lib Dems' tactical withdrawal clears way for bid to oust HDC leader|date=9 April 2008|work=Hunts Post}}</ref>

==Election result==
The Liberal Democrats made a net gain of 1 seat to move to 12 councillors at the expense of the Conservatives who remained in control of the council with 38 seats.<ref name="libdems">{{cite news|title=LibDems make a gain in Huntingdonshire|date=2 May 2008|work=[[Peterborough Evening Telegraph]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref> The Liberal Democrats gained seats in [[Buckden, Cambridgeshire|Buckden]] and [[Huntingdon]] East from the Conservatives, but lost a seat back to them in [[St Neots]] [[Eaton Socon]].<ref name=libdems/>
