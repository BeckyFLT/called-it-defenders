[[Wards and electoral divisions of the United Kingdom|Wards]] in grey were not contested in 2002.]]
The '''2002 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2002 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="elections2002">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote2002/local_elections/73.stm|title=Huntingdonshire|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=2 July 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 37
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 13
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 3<ref name="local">{{cite news|title=Local Elections: Results : Huntingdonshire|date=3 May 2002|work=[[The Guardian]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref>
