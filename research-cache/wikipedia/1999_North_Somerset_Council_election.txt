[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] in blue, [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] in yellow, [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] in red, [[Independent (politician)|independent]] in grey, [[Green Party of England and Wales|Green Party]] in green and [[Liberal Party (UK, 1989)|Liberal Paty]] in orange.]]
The '''1999 North Somerset Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[North Somerset]] [[Unitary authority|Unitary]] Council in [[Somerset]], [[England]]. The whole council was up for election with boundary changes since the [[1995 North Somerset Council election|last election in 1995]] increasing the number of seats by 2.<ref name="gain">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/169.stm|title=North Somerset|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=14 August 2011}}</ref> The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] gained overall control of the council from [[no overall control]].<ref name="nations">{{cite news|title=How the nations voted|date=8 May 1999|work=[[The Times]]|page=48}}</ref>
