[[File:Oldham UK local election 2010.svg|thumb|Results of the 2010 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
'''Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Oldham|Oldham]] Council''' were held on 6 May 2010, on the same day as the UK General Election. One third of the council was up for election. The council remained in no overall control..

After the election, the composition of the council was

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 27
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 27
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 6
