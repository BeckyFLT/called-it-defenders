The '''2021 Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2021 to elect members of [[Oldham Metropolitan Borough Council]] in [[England]]. This was on the same day as other local elections.<ref>{{cite web |title=Notice of Election |url=https://www.oldham.gov.uk/info/200732/election_notices/2691/notice_of_election |website=Oldham.gov.uk |access-date=2 May 2021 |archive-date=2 May 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210502074417/https://www.oldham.gov.uk/info/200732/election_notices/2691/notice_of_election |url-status=dead }}</ref> One-third of the seats were up for election.
