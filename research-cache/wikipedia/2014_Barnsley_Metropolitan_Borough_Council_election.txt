The '''2014 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 22 May 2014 to elect members of [[Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="BBC">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/events/vote2014/england-council-election-results |title=England council results |access-date=23 May 2014 |publisher=BBC News}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2014 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
