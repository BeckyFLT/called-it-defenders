The '''2027 Amber Valley Borough Council election''' will be held on 6 May 2027<ref>{{Cite web |last=Amber Valley Borough Council |date=2025-09-05 |title=Elections |url=https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/councillors-and-elections/elections/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260922155739/https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/councillors-and-elections/elections/ |archive-date=2026-09-22 |access-date=2026-09-22 |website=www.ambervalley.gov.uk |language=en-GB}}</ref>, alongside [[2027 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections in the United Kingdom]], to elect all 42 councillors for [[Amber Valley Borough Council]].

== Background ==
The election was not scheduled to be held as Amber Valley, along with many other councils in England, were to be re-organised under the government's [[Local Government Reorganisation|Local Government Re-Organisation]] scheme.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Local government reorganisation: programme review and decisions withdrawal letter to council leaders |url=https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-government-reorganisation-programme-review-and-decisions-withdrawal-letter-to-council-leaders/local-government-reorganisation-programme-review-and-decisions-withdrawal-letter-to-council-leaders |access-date=2026-09-22 |website=GOV.UK |language=en}}</ref> Due to the pausing of the re-organisation on 7 September, the existing council boundaries will be used.

[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] currently controls the council through a minority administration, lead by Chris Emmas-Williams. Labour won majority control in the 2023 elections<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Council |first=Amber Valley Borough |date=2025-05-13 |title=4 May 2023 borough results |url=https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/councillors-and-elections/elections/local-elections/borough-election-results/4-may-2023/ |access-date=2026-09-22 |website=www.ambervalley.gov.uk |publisher=Amber Valley Borough Council |language=en-GB}}</ref>, however defections have resulted in minority rule.

Each of the 18 wards of the council elects either two or three councillors, depending on their relative populations.
