The '''2019 Windsor and Maidenhead Borough Council election''' took place on Thursday 2 May 2019. That was the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|United Kingdom local elections]] in order to elect members of [[Windsor and Maidenhead Borough Council|Windsor and Maidenhead Council]] in [[Berkshire]], England. The whole council was up for election.

Before the election, the composition of the council was:

* [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 54
* [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 1
* [[Labour Party(UK)|Labour]] 0
* [[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 2

== Background ==
The Independent Local Government Boundary Commission for England carried out an electoral review of the Royal Borough with the view to change electoral arrangements from May 2019. The Commission announced the number of councillors was to reduce and also looked into the number of wards, their names and boundaries.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www3.rbwm.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/3678/electoral_review_questions_and_answers.pdf|title=RBWM|website=RBWM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20191028153518/https://www3.rbwm.gov.uk/download/downloads/id/3678/electoral_review_questions_and_answers.pdf|archivedate=28 October 2019}}</ref> This reduced the number of councillors from 57 to 41.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.lgbce.org.uk/media/local-electoral-arrangements-finalised-for-windsor-and-maidenhead|title=LGBCE {{!}} Local electoral arrangements finalised for Windsor and Maidenhead {{!}} LGBCE Site|website=www.lgbce.org.uk|language=en|access-date=2019-10-28}}</ref>
