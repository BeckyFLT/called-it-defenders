The '''2016 Wigan Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 5 May 2016 to elect members of [[Wigan Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="BBC">{{Cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-politics-35813119 |title=Guide to May 2016 elections in Scotland, Wales, England and Northern Ireland |access-date=21 April 2016 |work=BBC News}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2016 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].

==Overview==
Prior to the election, the composition of the council was:<ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/greater-manchester-news/local-elections-2016-results-wigan-11291918 | title=Wigan local election results 2016 - who won in Wigan? | date=6 May 2016 | first=Charlotte | last=Cox | newspaper=Manchester Evening News | access-date=22 October 2018 }}</ref>

*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]]: 64
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]]: 3
* Wigan Independents: 3
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]]: 5

After the election, the composition of the council was:<ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/greater-manchester-news/local-elections-2016-results-wigan-11291918 | title=Wigan local election results 2016 - who won in Wigan? | date=6 May 2016 | first=Charlotte | last=Cox | newspaper=Manchester Evening News | access-date=22 October 2018 }}</ref>
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]]: 65
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]]: 5
* Wigan Independents: 2
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]]: 3
