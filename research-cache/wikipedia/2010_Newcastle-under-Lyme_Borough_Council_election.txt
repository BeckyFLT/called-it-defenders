{{Use dmy dates|date=April 2026}}The '''2010 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 2010 to elect a third of the members of [[Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council]], the council of [[Newcastle-under-Lyme]] in [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=BBC NEWS {{!}} Election 2010 {{!}} Newcastle-under-Lyme council |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/election2010/council/html/3722.stm |access-date=2026-04-08 |website=[[BBC News]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as the other [[2010 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] as well as the [[2010 United Kingdom general election]]. The previous council election took place in [[2008 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election|2008]] and the following election was held in [[2011 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election|2011]]. In the election, the Liberal Democrats lost two wards to Labour but gained one from the Conservatives, the council stayed under [[no overall control]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Mozafari |first=Laurence |date=2010-04-22 |title=Local council elections for 2010 |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/local/stoke/hi/people_and_places/newsid_8638000/8638294.stm |access-date=2026-04-09 |work=[[BBC News]] |language=en-GB}}</ref>
