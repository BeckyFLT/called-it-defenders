The '''2019 East Staffordshire Borough Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of the [[East Staffordshire Borough Council]] in [[Elections in England|England]].<ref name="eaststaffsbc">{{cite web|url=http://eaststaffsbc.gov.uk/elections/2019-borough-council-results|title=2019 Borough and Parish Election Results &#124; ESBC|website=eaststaffsbc.gov.uk|access-date=2020-06-12}}</ref> It was held on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
