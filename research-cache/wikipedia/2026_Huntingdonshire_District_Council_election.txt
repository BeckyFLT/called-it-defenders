The '''2026 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' was held on 7 May 2026, alongside the [[2026 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections across the United Kingdom]] being held on the same day, to elect all 52 members of [[Huntingdonshire District Council]] in [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Huntingdonshire District Council want to go ahead with local elections|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgnp1e8g54o|website=[[BBC News]]|date=2026-01-15|access-date=2026-01-30|language=en-GB}}</ref>

Prior to the election, the council was under [[no overall control]], being run by a coalition of the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]], [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]], [[Green Party of England and Wales|Greens]] and some of the independent councillors, led by Liberal Democrat councillor Sarah Conboy. After the election, the council remained under no overall control, and the Liberal Democrats overtook the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] to become the largest party on the council. Another coalition was formed after the election, again led by Sarah Conboy, this time comprising the Liberal Democrats, Greens and independent councillors.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Howgego |first1=Emma |title=Coalition to run Huntingdonshire District Council |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpwp07kz2xvo |access-date=16 July 2026 |work=BBC News |date=22 May 2026}}</ref>

Due to ongoing [[Upcoming structural changes to local government in England|local government reorganisation]], this will be the final election to Huntingdonshire District Council before it is abolished and replaced by a successor [[unitary authority]]. Elections to the successor authority are due to take place in 2027.

== Background ==
In [[2022 Huntingdonshire District Council election|2022]], the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] lost the council to [[no overall control]].<ref>{{Cite news|title=Huntingdonshire result - Local Elections 2022|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/election/2022/england/councils/E07000011|work=BBC News|access-date=2026-01-30|language=en-GB}}</ref> The Conservatives remained the largest party after the 2022 election, but the council was run by a coalition of the Liberal Democrats, Labour, Greens and independent councillors.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Davies |first1=Debbie |title=White roses and political history in Huntingdonshire |url=https://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/local-council/white-roses-for-first-hdc-meeting-8979374 |access-date=20 May 2022 |work=Hunts Post |date=18 May 2022}}</ref>
