The '''2014 Leeds City Council election''' took place on Thursday 22 May 2014 to elect members of [[Leeds City Council]] in [[England]]. It was held on the same day as the [[2014 European Parliament election in the United Kingdom|2014 European Parliament election]] and other [[2014 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across the UK]].

As per the election cycle, one third of the council's seats were up for election. The councillors subsequently elected replaced those elected when their individual seats were previously contested in [[2010 Leeds City Council election|2010]].

The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] had been in overall control of the council since [[2011 Leeds City Council election|2011]] and their majority was unchanged following this election after winning 20 of the 33 seats contested.<ref>[http://www.leeds.gov.uk/docs/Statement%20of%20Persons%20Nominated.pdf Statement of Persons Nominated] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140502013632/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/docs/Statement%20of%20Persons%20Nominated.pdf |date=2014-05-02 }} Leeds City Council</ref><ref name="BBC">{{cite news| url = https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/topics/cpr6g37145et | title = Vote 2014 Leeds | access-date = 2014-05-29 | work = [[BBC News Online]]}}</ref><ref name="results">{{Cite web | url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/council/pages/localelectionresults.aspx | title = Leeds City Council Election Results 3 May 2012 | access-date = 2014-05-29 | publisher = leeds.gov.uk | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20140529185943/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/council/pages/localelectionresults.aspx | archive-date = 29 May 2014 | url-status = dead | df = dmy-all }}</ref>
