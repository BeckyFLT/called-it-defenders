{{Main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}
Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council were held on 1 May 2003. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] party gained overall control of the council from the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party. Overall turnout was 51.5%.

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 18
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 17
