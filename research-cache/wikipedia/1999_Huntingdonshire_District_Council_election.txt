The '''1999 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="vote99">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/123.stm|title=Huntingdonshire|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=30 June 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 36
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 14
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 2
*Vacant 1<ref name="council">{{cite news|title=Local Council Election Results|date=7 May 1999|work=[[The Independent]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref>
