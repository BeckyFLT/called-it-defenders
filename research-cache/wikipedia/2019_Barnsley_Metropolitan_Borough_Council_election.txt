The '''2019 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="results"/> This was on the same day as other [[United Kingdom local elections, 2019|local elections]].
