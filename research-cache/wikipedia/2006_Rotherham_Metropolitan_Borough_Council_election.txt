Elections to [[Metropolitan Borough of Rotherham|Rotherham]] Metropolitan Borough Council were held on 4 May 2006. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party kept overall control of the council. One third of the council was up for election and no boundary changes were made.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.andrewteale.me.uk/leap/results/2006/50/|title=Local Election Results 2006 – Rotherham|publisher=Local Elections Archive Project|access-date=24 July 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Rotherham-1973-2012.pdf|title=Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council Election Results 1973-2012|publisher=The Elections Centre|access-date=24 July 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/RP06-26/RP06-26.pdf|title=Local elections 2006|publisher=House of Commons Library|access-date=24 July 2022}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 55
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 6
*Others 2
