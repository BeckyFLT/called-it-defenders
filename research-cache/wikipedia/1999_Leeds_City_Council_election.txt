The '''1999 Leeds City Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[City of Leeds]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[West Yorkshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election, as well as a vacancy each in [[Horsforth (ward)|Horsforth]], [[Moortown (ward)|Moortown]] and [[Wetherby]]. Prior to the election, the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] had gained a seat in [[Bramley, Leeds|Bramley]] from [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]], and [[Hunslet]] councillor, Mark Davies, had defected from Labour to [[Independent (politician)|Independent]] [[Socialist]].

Labour stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/134.stm | title = Leeds | access-date = 6 August 2018| work = [[BBC News Online]]}}</ref> Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] in the election was 27.3%.<ref name="results">{{cite web| url = http://www.leeds.gov.uk/lcc/cncllrs/elect_99/elecmain.html | title =  Election of Councillors : 6th May 1999 | publisher = leeds.gov.uk |access-date = 6 August 2018| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/19991002182534/http://www.leeds.gov.uk/lcc/cncllrs/elect_99/elecmain.html | archive-date = 2 October 1999}}</ref>
