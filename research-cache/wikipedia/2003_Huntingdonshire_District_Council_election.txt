The '''2003 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 1 May 2003 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="vote2003">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2003/locals/html/136.stm|title=Local elections|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=2 July 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
* [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 36
* [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 14
* [[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 3<ref name="huntingdonshire">{{cite news|title=Huntingdonshire|date=2 May 2003|work=[[The Guardian]]}}</ref>
