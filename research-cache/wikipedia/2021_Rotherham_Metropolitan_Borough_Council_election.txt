'''Elections to [[Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council]]''' were held on 6 May 2021.

Although [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] retained control of the council, the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] went from zero to 20 seats at this election.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rotherham: Large Tory gains in council all-out election |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-south-yorkshire-57038429 |website=BBC News |access-date=1 July 2021 |date=8 May 2021}}</ref>

===Changes of allegiance===
In May 2023, Councillors Emily Barley and Jill Thompson resigned from the Conservative Group and formed the 'Independent Conservative' Group. Councillor Charlie Wooding was suspended from the Conservative Group whilst an investigation took place regarding his attendance, and he subsequently resigned triggering a by-election.<ref>{{cite web|title=BBC News|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-south-yorkshire-65699921|access-date=24 May 2023|website=BBC.com/news}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Sebestyan |first=Roland |date=5 June 2023 |title=Suspended Tory councillor steps downs and triggers by-election in Dinnington |url=https://www.thestar.co.uk/news/politics/suspended-tory-councillor-steps-downs-and-triggers-by-election-in-dinnington-4169273 }}</ref>

The Rotherham Democratic Party disbanded in 2022, with its councillors thereafter sitting as independents.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rotherham Democratic Party |url=https://search.electoralcommission.org.uk/English/Registrations/PP11473 |website=The Electoral Commission |access-date=11 March 2024}}</ref>
