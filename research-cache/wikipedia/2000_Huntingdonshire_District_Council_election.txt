The '''2000 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2000 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="vote2000">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/uk_politics/vote2000/locals/58.stm|title=Huntingdonshire|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=30 June 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 37
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 13
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 3<ref name="councils">{{cite news|title=Election results: local councils - Elections|date=5 May 2000|work=[[The Times]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]|page=4}}</ref>
