[[File:Bolton UK local election 2004.svg|thumb|Results of the 2004 Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council election]]
'''Elections to [[Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council]]''' were held on 10 June 2004.  The whole council was up for election with boundary changes since the last election in [[2003 Bolton Council election|2003]].<ref name=hold>{{cite web| url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2004/locals/html/3660.stm | title = Bolton council | access-date = 2009-12-16 | publisher = [[BBC News]]}}</ref> The council stayed under no overall control, with the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] overtaking the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]], by 1 seat, as the largest party and assuming control of the council from Labour.  Overall turnout was 43.5%.
