The '''1995 Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 1995. The whole council was up for election as it was the first election since the formation of the Unitary Authority after replacing [[Langbaurgh (district)|Langbaurgh]] Borough Council. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] won the most seats and took overall control of the council.<ref>[http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/Redcar-Cleveland-1995-2012.pdf Redcar & Cleveland Council Election Results 1995-2012] Elections Centre</ref>
