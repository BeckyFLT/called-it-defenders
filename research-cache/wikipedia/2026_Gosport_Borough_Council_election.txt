{{Main|Gosport Borough Council elections}}

The '''2026 Gosport Borough Council election''' was held on 7 May 2026, alongside the [[2026 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections across the United Kingdom]] being held on the same day, to elect 14 of 28 members of [[Gosport Borough Council]] in [[Hampshire]], [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Elections and voting|url=https://www.gosport.gov.uk/elections|website=Gosport Borough Council|access-date=2026-01-30|language=en}}</ref>

Prior to the election, the council was under [[no overall control]], being run by a [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] minority administration. The Liberal Democrats lost every seat they were defending at this election. They were overtaken by both the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] and [[Reform UK]].<ref name=DavidGeorge/> After the election, the Conservatives chose Zoe Huggins to be their new group leader. She was appointed as the new leader of the council at the subsequent annual council meeting on 20 May 2026, leading a Conservative minority administration.<ref name=MayaGeorge/><ref>{{cite web |title=Gosport |url=https://www.localcouncils.co.uk/councils/?council=gosport |website=Local Councils |publisher=Thorncliffe |access-date=11 August 2026}}</ref>
