[[File:South Staffordshire UK ward map 2011.svg|thumb|Results of the 2011 South Staffordshire District Council election]]
The '''2011 South Staffordshire District Council election''' to the [[South Staffordshire District Council]] took place on Thursday 5 May 2011.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sstaffs.gov.uk/elections/local-elections--5th-may-2011.cfm|title=Local Elections - 5th May 2011|date=n.d.|publisher=South Staffordshire Council|access-date=2021-09-26}}</ref> A total of 49 seats were up for election, 42 of which went to the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]], mirroring the [[2007 South Staffordshire District Council election|2007 elections]].<ref name=data>{{cite web|url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/South-Staffordshire-1973-2011.pdf|title=South Staffordshire Council Election Results 1973-2011|date=2011|last1=Rallings|first1=Colin|last2=Thrasher|first2=Michael|publisher=The Elections Centre, Plymouth University|access-date=2021-09-26}}</ref>
