[[File:Newcastle-under-Lyme UK local election 2004.svg|thumb|Results of the 2004 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election]]
Elections to [[Newcastle-under-Lyme (borough)|Newcastle-under-Lyme]] Borough Council were held on 10 June 2004.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party gained overall control of the council from [[no overall control]].

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 31
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 14
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 14
*Caring Party 1
