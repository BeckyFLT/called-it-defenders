Elections to [[Newcastle-under-Lyme (borough)|Newcastle-under-Lyme]] Borough Council were held on 6 May 1999.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party kept overall control of the council.

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 36
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 12
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 7
*Others 1
