The '''1999 Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[Redcar and Cleveland]] [[Unitary authorities of England|Unitary]] Council in [[England]]. The whole council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/189.stm|title=Redcar & Cleveland|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=28 October 2011}}</ref>

==Election result==
Overall [[Voter turnout|turnout]] at the election was 37%.<ref name="recount">{{cite web|url=http://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/PressRel.nsf/Published/1E409DB57ADB388380256D1A0029F8F1?OpenDocument|title=Brotton ward election recount today|date=2 May 2003|publisher=Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council|access-date=29 October 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171206201530/http://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/PressRel.nsf/Published/1E409DB57ADB388380256D1A0029F8F1?OpenDocument|archive-date=6 December 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref>
