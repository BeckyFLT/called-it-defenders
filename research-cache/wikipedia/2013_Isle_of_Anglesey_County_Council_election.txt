'''An election to the [[Isle of Anglesey County Council]]'''<ref>[http://www.anglesey.gov.uk/council-and-democracy/councillors-democracy-and-elections/elections-and-voting/register-to-vote/registering-to-vote-in-2013/114526.article Registering to vote in 2013?] Isle of Anglesey</ref> was held as part of the [[2013 United Kingdom local elections]] and took place on 2 May 2013. [[Anglesey]] was the only Welsh authority voting in 2013, the election having been postponed from 2012 by the [[Welsh Government]], in order to allow an electoral review to take place.<ref>[https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-wales-north-west-wales-16590896 BBC News] Anglesey council election postponed for one year to 2013 (17 January 2012)</ref> The next full council election took place [[2017 Isle of Anglesey County Council election|on 4 May 2017]].

==Boundary review==

{{main|Isle of Anglesey electoral boundary changes 2012}}
Under The Isle of Anglesey (Electoral Arrangements) Order 2012, thirty seats (a reduction from the previous 40) were created, from eleven (previously 40) electoral divisions, by the [[Local Government Boundary Commission for Wales]].<ref>[http://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2012/2676/pdfs/wsi_20122676_mi.pdf The Isle of Anglesey (Electoral Arrangements) Order 2012] Legislation.gov.uk</ref>

==Overall results==
Turnout for the election was 50.5% of eligible voters.<ref name=plaidlabourdeal>{{cite news|title=Anglesey election: Plaid to seek Labour council deal|url= https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-wales-north-west-wales-22388459|work=BBC News |date=3 May 2013|accessdate=2017-12-02}}</ref> Independent councillors won more seats than any other group, but Plaid Cymru made strong gains, saying they would find it difficult to work with the independent group and hoped to form an alliance with Labour.<ref name=plaidlabourdeal /> The island's [[Returning officer|Returning Officer]] described democracy on the island as being "invigorated".<ref name=plaidlabourdeal />
