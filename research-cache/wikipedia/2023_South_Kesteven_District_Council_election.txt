The '''2023 South Kesteven District Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect all 56 members of [[South Kesteven District Council]] in [[Lincolnshire]], England. This was on the same day as [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections across England]].

The council was under Conservative majority control prior to the election. The Conservatives remained the largest party after the election but lost their majority. Both the Conservative leader, Kelham Cooke, and deputy leader, Adam Stokes, lost their seats.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-05-05 |title=South Kesteven eyes regime change as council leader and deputy out |url=https://thelincolnite.co.uk/2023/05/south-kesteven-eyes-regime-change-as-council-leader-and-deputy-out/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230505074142/https://thelincolnite.co.uk/2023/05/south-kesteven-eyes-regime-change-as-council-leader-and-deputy-out/ |url-status=usurped |archive-date=May 5, 2023 |access-date=2023-05-07 |website=The Lincolnite |language=en}}</ref>

A coalition of some of the independent councillors and the Greens, Liberal Democrats and Labour formed to take control of the council, narrowly beating a rival coalition of the Conservatives and some of the other independents to form an administration by one vote at the council's subsequent annual meeting on 18 May 2023. Independent councillor Richard Cleaver therefore became leader of the council, defeating the new Conservative leader, Graham Jeal.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Jaines |first1=Daniel |last2=Baynes |first2=Chris |title=South Kesteven: Independent-led coalition takes control of council |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-lincolnshire-65641866 |access-date=29 November 2023 |work=BBC News |date=18 May 2023}}</ref>
