The '''1999 Amber Valley Borough Council election''' took place on 6 May 1999 to elect members of [[Amber Valley]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Derbyshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/4.stm|title=Amber Valley|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=15 May 2011}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 32
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 11<ref name="britain">{{cite news|title=How Britain voted: Council Election Results|date=8 May 1999|work=[[The Independent]]|page=12}}</ref>
