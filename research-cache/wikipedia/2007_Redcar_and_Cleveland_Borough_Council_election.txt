The '''2007 Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[Redcar and Cleveland]] [[Unitary authorities of England|Unitary]] Council in England. The whole council was up for election and the council stayed under [[no overall control]].<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/vote2007/councils/html/ee.stm|title=Redcar & Cleveland|date=4 May 2007|publisher=BBC News|access-date=30 October 2011}}</ref>

==Background==
At the [[2003 Redcar and Cleveland Council election|last election in 2003]] [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] lost their majority on the council, after winning 23 seats, compared to 15 for the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]], 13 [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] and 8 [[Independent (politician)|independents]].<ref name="lose">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2003/locals/html/210.stm|title=Local elections|publisher=BBC News|access-date=24 November 2011}}</ref> A coalition between the Liberal Democrats, Conservatives and independents then took control of the council.<ref name="grip">{{cite news|url=http://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/archive/2003/05/12/The+North+East+Archive/7031407.Labour_loses_its_grip_on_council/|title=Labour loses its grip on council|date=12 May 2003|work=[[The Northern Echo]]|access-date=24 November 2011}}</ref>

In 2004 a Labour [[councillor]] for [[Guisborough]], and former deputy leader of the party, Keith Pudney, became an independent,<ref name="may">{{cite web|url=http://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/main.nsf/Web+Full+List/AEC7B94A18B29F8280256D1A003AC1FE?OpenDocument|title=May 2003 Election Results|publisher=Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council|access-date=26 November 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111022081328/https://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/main.nsf/Web+Full+List/AEC7B94A18B29F8280256D1A003AC1FE?OpenDocument|archive-date=22 October 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref> before becoming a Liberal Democrat in 2005.<ref name="duo">{{cite news|url=http://www.thefreelibrary.com/Duo+quit+Labour+for+the+LibDems.-a0131324649|title=Duo quit Labour for the LibDems.|date=11 April 2005|work=[[Evening Gazette (Teesside)|Evening Gazette]]|publisher=The Free Library|access-date=26 November 2011}}</ref> However, also in 2005, Labour gained a seat in Westworth from an independent at a by-election.<ref name="westworth">{{cite web|url=http://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/main.nsf/Web+Full+List/95A0976E6F84DFDC80256FF900307009?OpenDocument|title=May 2005 Election Results|publisher=Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council|access-date=26 November 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111109043056/http://redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/main.nsf/Web+Full+List/95A0976E6F84DFDC80256FF900307009?OpenDocument|archive-date=9 November 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref> A final change came in February 2007 when David Tomlin resigned from the Labour party after being convicted for falsely claiming [[Welfare spending|benefits]].<ref name="shamed">{{cite news|url=http://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/tm_headline=shamed&method=full&objectid=18625813&siteid=109975-name_page.html|title=Shamed|last=Haworth|first=Simon|date=15 February 2007|work=[[Evening Gazette (Teesside)|Evening Gazette]]|access-date=26 November 2011}}</ref> This meant that before the 2007 election Labour had 22 seats, the Liberal Democrats 16, Conservatives 13, East Cleveland Independents 2, the Independent Group 5 and 1 independent,<ref name="candidates">{{cite web|url=http://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/PressRel.nsf/Published/D55ACFCA97F8C41D802572B4002CD8B6?OpenDocument|title=160 candidates for Borough Council election|date=3 May 2007|publisher=Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council|access-date=26 November 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131005014621/http://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/PressRel.nsf/Published/D55ACFCA97F8C41D802572B4002CD8B6?OpenDocument|archive-date=5 October 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> with the Liberal Democrats, Conservatives and the 2 East Cleveland Independents forming the administration, while Labour and the other independents were in opposition.<ref name="state">{{cite news|url=http://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/tm_method=full&objectid=18961886&siteid=109975-name_page.html|title=The state of independents|last=Robson|first=Dave|date=25 April 2007|work=[[Evening Gazette (Teesside)|Evening Gazette]]|access-date=26 November 2011}}</ref>

In total 160 candidates stood for the 59 seats that were being contested at the election.<ref name=candidates/> The council already had the best female representation of any council in [[North East England]] with 30 women councillors and 75 of the candidates were female.<ref name=state/> The candidates comprised 50 from Labour, 42 Liberal Democrats, 37 Conservatives, 4 [[British National Party]] and 27 various independents.<ref name=state/> Meanwhile, 7 sitting councillors stood down at the election, Christopher Beadle, Keith Blott, Bill Goodwill, Barbara Harpham, Keith Pudney, Alma Thrower and David Tomlin.<ref name=candidates/>

==Election result==
Labour made a net gain of 6 seats, to go to 28 seats on the council, 2 short of a majority.<ref name="negotiation">{{cite news|url=http://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/archive/2007/05/05/Tees+Valley+%28northernecho_teesvalley_news%29/1379487.Negotiation_will_start_to_work_out_coalition/|title=Negotiation will start to work out coalition|date=5 May 2007|work=[[The Northern Echo]]|access-date=26 November 2011}}</ref> The gains came at the expense of the Liberal Democrats who dropped 3 to 13 seats and the Conservatives who were down 2 to 11 seats.<ref name=negotiation/> 7 independents were also elected, 4 in the Independent Group, 1 Loftus Ward Independent, 1 East Cleveland and Guisborough Independent and 1 East Cleveland Independent.<ref name=negotiation/>

Following the election the Labour group leader, George Dunning, became the new leader of the council, after Labour got the support of 2 independents, Mike Findley and Mary Lanigan.<ref name="services">{{cite news|url=http://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/archive/2007/05/18/Tees+Valley+%28northernecho_teesvalley_news%29/1408486.Leader_promises_council_services_will_be_improved/|title=Leader promises council services will be improved|date=18 May 2007|work=[[The Northern Echo]]|access-date=26 November 2011}}</ref>
