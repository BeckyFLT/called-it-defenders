The '''2027 West Suffolk District Council election''' will be held on 6 May 2027 to elect members of [[West Suffolk District Council]] in [[Suffolk]], [[England]]. This will be on the same day as other [[2027 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]] held across the United Kingdom.

==Background==

===Local government reorganisation===

Due to proposed [[upcoming structural changes to local government in England|structural changes to local government in England]], the [[2023 West Suffolk District Council election|2023 election]] would have been the final election to the council before it was abolished and replaced by an expanded West Suffolk Council, a [[unitary authority]].<ref>{{Cite web|first= |last= |author-link= |first2= |last2= |author2-link= |url=https://www.suffolklgrhub.gov.uk/lgr-explained/lgr-in-suffolk |website=suffolklgrhub.gov.uk |title=LGR in Suffolk |pages= |date= |access-date=17 September 2026 |work= |publisher=Suffolk LGR Hub |place= |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260515135037/https://www.suffolklgrhub.gov.uk/lgr-explained/lgr-in-suffolk |archive-date=15 May 2026 }}</ref> However, on 7 September 2026, the government announced it was withdrawing plans for the planned restructuring pending review. Following this announcement, local elections due to be held in 2027 under the existing local electoral calendar would proceed.<ref>{{Cite web|first=Becky |last=Morton |author-link= |first2= |last2= |author2-link= |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2zkj09dy4o |website=bbc.com |title=Plans for major overhaul of councils halted |pages= |date=8 September 2026 |access-date=15 September 2026 |work= |publisher=[[BBC News]] |place= |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260908123457/https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2zkj09dy4o |archive-date=8 September 2026 }}</ref>
