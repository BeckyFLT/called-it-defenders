The '''2000 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2000 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Barnsley|Barnsley]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[South Yorkshire]], England. Prior to the election, [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] had suffered a [[1999 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election#By-elections between 1999 and 2000|by-election loss]] in South West and two defections to [[Independent (politician)|Independent]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/uk_politics/vote2000/locals/3.stm|title=Barnsley|publisher=BBC News|access-date=13 June 2011}}</ref><ref name="results">{{cite web| url = http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Barnsley-1973-2012.pdf | title = Elections Centre: Barnsley 1973-2012 | publisher = electionscentre.co.uk|access-date = 2017-07-25 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20170725171829/http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Barnsley-1973-2012.pdf | archive-date = 2017-07-25}}</ref><ref name="results2">{{cite web| url = https://www.barnsley.gov.uk/media/4121/electionresults1979to2003.pdf | title = Election results 1979 to 2003 | publisher = Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council |access-date = 2017-07-28 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20170728185506/https://www.barnsley.gov.uk/media/4121/electionresults1979to2003.pdf | archive-date = 2017-07-28}}</ref>
