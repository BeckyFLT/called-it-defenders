The '''2024 Gosport Borough Council election''' was held on Thursday 2 May 2024, alongside the [[2024 United Kingdom local elections|other local elections in the United Kingdom]] on the same day. There were 15 seats up for election, being the usual half of the 28 members of [[Gosport Borough Council]] in [[Hampshire]], plus a vacancy in Lee West ward.<ref name="lee">{{cite web |last1=Boothroyd |first1=David |title=In like Flintshire |url=https://www.localcouncils.co.uk/2024/03/in-like-flintshire/ |website=LocalCouncils.co.uk |access-date=4 April 2024}}</ref>

The council remained under [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] majority control.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Brown |first1=Kelly |title=Gosport Borough Council: Full election results for 2024 as the Conservatives buck a national trend |url=https://www.portsmouth.co.uk/news/politics/gosport-borough-council-election-results-2024-4615027 |access-date=5 July 2024 |work=The News |date=3 May 2024}}</ref>

==Background==
Gosport has historically been controlled by all 3 major parties. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] controlled the council from its creation to [[1976 Gosport Borough Council election|1976]], when the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] won a majority.<ref name="ec">{{cite web |title=Gosport Borough Council Election Results 1973-2012 |url=https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Gosport-1973-2012.pdf |website=Elections Centre |access-date=4 April 2024}}</ref> This lasted until 1990 when the council fell into [[no overall control]], before being gained by the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] in [[1991 Gosport Borough Council election|1991]].<ref name="ec" /> At the [[1998 Gosport Borough Council election|1998 election]], the council again fell into no overall control, and was gained by the Conservatives in [[2010 Gosport Borough Council election|2010]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Gosport - Vote 2010 |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/election2010/council/html/3695.stm |website=BBC News |access-date=4 April 2024}}</ref>

The [[2022 Gosport Borough Council election|2022 election]] was to elect all seats following boundary changes. In that election, the Liberal Democrats won a majority, with 16 seats and a 40.9% vote share. The Conservatives won 10 with 42.8%, and Labour won 2 with 11.7%. The 2024 election is for seats held by the candidate who were returned in 2022 with the second-highest number of votes.<ref>{{Cite legislation UK |type=si |year=2022 |number=129 |si=The Gosport (Electoral Changes) Order 2022 |accessdate=4 April 2024}}</ref>
