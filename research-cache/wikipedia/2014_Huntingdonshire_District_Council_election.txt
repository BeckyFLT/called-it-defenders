The '''2014 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 22 May 2014 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="vote2014">{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/topics/c51gr1rxyr8t|title=Huntingdonshire|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 34
*[[UK Independence Party]] 7
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 5
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 4
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 1
*Vacant 1<ref name="me">{{cite web|url=http://www.huntingdonshire.gov.uk/SiteCollectionDocuments/HDCCMS/Documents/Democratic%20Services%20documents/Me-a-District-Councillor.pdf |title=Me, a District Councillor? |date=4 June 2014 |work=Huntingdonshire District Council |access-date=13 July 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608105436/http://www.huntingdonshire.gov.uk/SiteCollectionDocuments/HDCCMS/Documents/Democratic%20Services%20documents/Me-a-District-Councillor.pdf |archive-date=8 June 2015 |df=dmy-all }}</ref>

==Background==
At the [[2012 Huntingdonshire District Council election|last election in 2012]] the Conservatives stayed in control of the council with 39 seats, compared to 7 [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]], 3 [[UK Independence Party]], 2 [[Independent (politician)|independents]] and 1 for [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]].<ref name="vote2012">{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/special/vote2012/council/E07000011.stm|title=Huntingdonshire|date=4 May 2012|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref> However, in early 2013 Conservative [[councillor]]s, Ken Churchill from [[Little Paxton]] [[Wards and electoral divisions of the United Kingdom|ward]] and Bob Farrer from [[St Neots]] [[Eaton Ford]], left the Conservatives to become independents and then joined the UK Independence Party in November 2013.<ref name="quit">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/latest-news/st_neots_councillors_quit_conservatives_to_stand_as_independents_1_1847541|title=St Neots councillors quit Conservatives to stand as independents|last=Papworth|first=Andrew|date=1 February 2013|work=Hunts Post|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref><ref name="boost">{{cite news|url=http://www.cambridge-news.co.uk/Huntingdon-St-Ives-St-Neots/Big-boost-for-UKIP-in-Huntingdonshire-as-former-Conservative-councillors-Bob-Farrer-and-Ken-Churchill-defect-20131105145121.htm|title=Big boost for UKIP in Huntingdonshire as former Conservative councillors Bob Farrer and Ken Churchill defect|last=Curwen|first=Edward|date=5 November 2013|work=News & Crier|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref> Subsequently in January 2014 another Conservative councillor, Colin Hyams of [[Godmanchester]] ward, [[defected]] to the Liberal Democrats.<ref name="hyams">{{cite news|url=http://www.cambridge-news.co.uk/Huntingdon-St-Ives-St-Neots/Tory-Huntingdonshire-district-councillor-Colin-Hyams-defects-to-Lib-Dems-20140130114435.htm |title=Tory Huntingdonshire district councillor Colin Hyams defects to Lib Dems |last=Makey |first=Julian |date=30 January 2014 |work=News & Crier |access-date=13 July 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140406234148/http://www.cambridge-news.co.uk/Huntingdon-St-Ives-St-Neots/Tory-Huntingdonshire-district-councillor-Colin-Hyams-defects-to-Lib-Dems-20140130114435.htm |archive-date=6 April 2014 |df=dmy-all }}</ref>

The changes meant that before the election the Conservatives had 35 councillors, Liberal Democrats 7, UK Independence Party 5, independents 3, Labour had 1 seat and 1 seat was vacant after the death of the Conservative councillor for [[Warboys]] and [[Bury, Cambridgeshire|Bury]], John Pethard.<ref name="residents">{{cite news|url=http://www.cambridge-news.co.uk/Huntingdon-St-Ives-St-Neots/Huntingdonshire-residents-to-cast-their-votes-in-local-and-Euro-elections-next-week-20140513074832.htm|title=Huntingdonshire residents to cast their votes in local and Euro elections next week|last=Makey|first=Julian|date=13 May 2014|work=News & Crier|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref> 17 of the 52 seats on the council were contested with 5 councillors, Terry Clough, Nick Guyatt, Colin Hyams, Terry Rogers and Alan Williams, standing down at the election.<ref name=residents/>

==Election result==
The Conservatives stayed in control of the council after winning 11 of the 17 seats contested,<ref name="elections">{{cite news|title=Results Elections 2014|date=24 May 2014|work=[[The Times]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]|pages=88–89}}</ref> but lost 1 seat to the UK Independence Party in [[Yaxley, Cambridgeshire|Yaxley]] and [[Farcet]].<ref name="yaxley">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/latest-news/huntingdonshire_elections_2014_ukip_beats_conservatives_in_yaxley_and_farcet_election_1_3610124|title=UKIP beats Conservatives in Yaxley and Farcet election|last=Barrett|first=Hywel|date=23 May 2014|work=Hunts Post|access-date=13 July 2014}}{{Dead link|date=December 2021 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The UK Independence Party also gained a seat from the Liberal Democrats in [[Huntingdon]] East by 34 votes to end the election with 7 councillors.<ref name="east">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/latest-news/hdc_election_results_huntingdon_east_ukip_gain_from_lib_dem_1_3613891|title=HDC election results: Huntingdon East – UKIP gain from Lib Dem|date=23 May 2014|work=Hunts Post|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref><ref name=elections/> The only other change came in [[Buckden, Cambridgeshire|Buckden]] where independent Terry Hayward won a seat, which had formerly been held by Liberal Democrat William Clough before he stood down at the election.<ref name="buckden">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/latest-news/huntingdonshire_elections_2014_independent_replaces_liberal_democrats_in_buckden_1_3610169|title=Independent replaces Liberal Democrats in Buckden|last=Barrett|first=Hywel|date=23 May 2014|work=Hunts Post|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref> These defeats reduced the Liberal Democrats to 5 seats on the council,<ref name=elections/> but Sarah Conboy did hold Godmanchester for the party after Colin Hyams, who had defected to the Liberal Democrats from the Conservatives earlier in 2014, stood down.<ref name="godmanchester">{{cite news|url=http://www.huntspost.co.uk/news/latest-news/huntingdonshire_elections_2014_liberal_democrats_win_in_godmanchester_1_3610161|title=Liberal Democrats win in Godmanchester|last=Barrett|first=Hywel|date=23 May 2014|work=Hunts Post|access-date=13 July 2014}}</ref>
