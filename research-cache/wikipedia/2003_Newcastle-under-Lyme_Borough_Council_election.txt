'''Elections to [[Newcastle-under-Lyme (borough)|Newcastle-under-Lyme]] Borough Council''' were held on 1 May 2003.  One third of the council was up for election and the council stayed under [[no overall control]].

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 29
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 18
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 12
*Caring Party 1
