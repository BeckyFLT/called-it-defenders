'''Elections to [[Bolton Metropolitan Borough Council]]''' were held on 6 May 1999. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] Party kept overall control of the council.<ref name=hold>{{cite news |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote_99/locals_99/html/constituency/23.stm |title=Bolton |access-date=16 December 2009 |publisher=[[BBC News]]}}</ref>

20 seats were contested in the election, with 11 being won by the Labour Party, 5 by the Liberal Democrats and 4 by the Conservatives.

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 41
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 10
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 9
