{{Use dmy dates|date=April 2026}}The '''2008 North East Lincolnshire Council election''' took place on 1 May 2008 to elect a third of the members of [[North East Lincolnshire Council]], the council of [[North East Lincolnshire]] in [[England]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=BBC NEWS {{!}} Election 2008 {{!}} North East Lincolnshire council |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/elections/local_council/08/html/95p.stm |access-date=2026-04-08 |website=[[BBC News]]}}</ref> This was on the same day as the other [[2008 United Kingdom local elections]]. The previous council election took place in [[2007 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2007]] and the following election was held in [[2010 North East Lincolnshire Council election|2010]]. In the election, the council stayed under [[no overall control]].<ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=May 2008 |title=Local Elections 2008 |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/humber/content/articles/2008/04/02/local_elections08_feature.shtml |access-date=2026-04-08 |website=[[BBC News]] |language=en-gb}}</ref>
