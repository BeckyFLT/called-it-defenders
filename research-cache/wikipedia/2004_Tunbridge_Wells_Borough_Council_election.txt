The '''2004 Tunbridge Wells Borough Council election''' took place on 10 June 2004 to elect members of [[Tunbridge Wells (borough)|Tunbridge Wells]] [[Borough status in the United Kingdom|Borough]] Council in [[Kent]], England. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/vote2004/locals/html/3779.stm | title = Tunbridge Wells council | access-date = 2010-11-21 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 35
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] 12
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 1<ref>{{cite news | title = Elections 2004: Results at a glance | newspaper = The Guardian | page = 10 | date = 2004-06-12 }}</ref>
