The '''2007 North Somerset Council election''' took place on 3 May 2007 to elect members of [[North Somerset]] [[Unitary authority|Unitary]] Council in [[Somerset]], England. The whole council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] gained overall control of the council from [[no overall control]].<ref name="gain">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/vote2007/councils/html/hc.stm|title=North Somerset|publisher=BBC News|access-date=16 August 2011}}</ref>

==Background==
Since the [[2003 North Somerset Council election|2003 election]] the council was controlled by a coalition between the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrat]] and [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] parties.<ref name="sweep">{{cite news|url=http://www.edp24.co.uk/news/tories_sweep_north_somerset_1_311522|title=Tories sweep North Somerset|date=4 May 2007|work=[[Eastern Daily Press]]|access-date=19 August 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120819210154/http://www.edp24.co.uk/news/tories_sweep_north_somerset_1_311522|archive-date=19 August 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref> Many of the swing [[Wards of the United Kingdom|wards]] on the council were in [[Weston-super-Mare]], with a major issue in the town being the derelict Tropicana [[Lido (swimming pool)|lido]].<ref name="beach">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/programmes/politics_show/6587853.stm|title=Ballot on the beach|last=Harvey|first=Dave|date=27 April 2007|publisher=BBC News|access-date=19 August 2011}}</ref> The high number of pensioners in the town and lack of things for young people to do in the new estates were also factors in the area.<ref name=beach/>

==Election results==
The results saw the Conservatives win a majority with 43 of the 61 seats contested.<ref name=sweep/> Both the Liberal Democrat and Labour parties suffered heavy losses, with the defeated [[councillor]]s including Liberal Democrat Isabel Cummings in [[Yatton]] after 15 years on the council and the Labour couple Derek and Muriel Kraft.<ref name=sweep/>

The election in [[Weston-super-Mare]] North [[Worle]] ward was delayed after the death of the Liberal Democrat leader of the council Alan Hockridge, meaning it was not held at the same time as the other seats.<ref name="worle">{{cite web|url=http://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/Your+Council/News/news-20070503-northworleelection.htm|title=North Worle election|publisher=North Somerset Council|access-date=19 August 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120323030648/http://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/Your+Council/News/news-20070503-northworleelection.htm|archive-date=23 March 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref> The [[North Somerset local elections#2007-2011|postponed election in North Worle]] as held on 7 June and all 3 seats were won by the Conservatives taking the party to 46 seats.<ref name="north">{{cite web|url=http://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/Your+Council/News/news-20070608-northworleelectionresult.htm|title=North Worle election result|publisher=North Somerset Council|access-date=19 August 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120323030727/http://www.n-somerset.gov.uk/Your+Council/News/news-20070608-northworleelectionresult.htm|archive-date=23 March 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref>
