The '''1986 Lambeth London Borough Council election''' took place on 8 May 1986. All 64 members of [[Lambeth London Borough Council]] were elected. The elections took place alongside  [[1986 London local elections|local elections in the other London boroughs]] and [[1986 United Kingdom local elections|elections to local authorities across the United Kingdom]].

In the 1986 elections, the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] won 40 seats (up 8 from 1982), the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] won 21 (down 6), and the [[SDP–Liberal Alliance]] won 3 (down 2). Turnout was 43.0%.   

Before the 1986 election, the district auditor had identified losses due to the late setting of council rates as part of the [[rate-capping rebellion]], and surcharged the relevant councillors (half of the total), disqualifying them from office and barring them from standing again for five years.  This reduced the Labour Group on the council from the 32 [[1982 Lambeth London Borough Council election|elected in 1982]] to 3 going into the election.
