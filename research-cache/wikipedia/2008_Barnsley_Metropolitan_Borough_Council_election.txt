The '''2008 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 1 May 2008 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Barnsley|Barnsley]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[South Yorkshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council, alongside an additional vacancy in Old Town was up for election. Prior to the election the defending councillor in Penistone West, having earlier defected from [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] to the Barnsley Independent Group, fought the election as an [[Independent (politician)|Independent]]. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="change">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/shared/bsp/hi/elections/local_council/08/html/cc.stm|title=Barnsley|work=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=20 June 2011}}</ref>

==Election result==
Before the election it had been thought that the Labour Party might lose overall control of the council but they held on by only one seat.<ref name="big">{{cite news |date=2 May 2008 |title=BIG Gain on Labour Council |url=http://www.barnsleynews.co.uk/barnsley-news/10-news/42-big-gain-on-labour-council |access-date=23 August 2008 |newspaper=Barnsley News}} {{Dead link|date=October 2010|bot=H3llBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref><ref name=maintains>{{cite news | url = http://www.thestar.co.uk/barnsley/ELECTIONS-3908-Labour-maintains-control.4045584.jp | title = ELECTIONS '08: Labour maintains control in Barnsley | access-date = 23 August 2008 | date = 2 May 2008 | newspaper = The Star }}{{Dead link|date=November 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> They lost two seats to the Barnsley Independent Group but gained one seat from the [[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] to hold on.<ref name=power>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/south_yorkshire/7378924.stm | title = Lib Dems take power in Sheffield | access-date = 23 August 2008 | date = 2 May 2008 | work = [[BBC Online]]}}</ref> The Barnsley Independent Group also lost 1 seat to the Conservatives.  Overall turnout was 34.78%.<ref name=results>{{cite web | url = http://www.barnsley.gov.uk/bguk/Council_Democracy/Press_Releases/May/Local%20election%20results%202008.html | title = Local election results 2008 | access-date = 17 August 2008 | publisher = Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20090102172643/http://www.barnsley.gov.uk/bguk/Council_Democracy/Press_Releases/May/Local%20election%20results%202008.html | archive-date = 2 January 2009 | url-status = dead }}</ref>

The losses for the Labour Party were blamed on government policies, such as on immigration and the abolition of the 10p [[income tax]] band, by the leader of the council, Steve Houghton, who was himself re-elected for [[Cudworth, South Yorkshire|Cudworth]] [[Wards of the United Kingdom|ward]].<ref name=republic>{{cite news| url = http://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/election/Shaky-39republic39-hangs-on-by.4048182.jp | title = Shaky 'republic' hangs on by one seat | access-date = 23 August 2008 | date = 3 May 2008 | newspaper = [[Yorkshire Post]]| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20080719194348/http://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/election/Shaky-39republic39-hangs-on-by.4048182.jp| archive-date= 19 July 2008 | url-status= live}}</ref>
