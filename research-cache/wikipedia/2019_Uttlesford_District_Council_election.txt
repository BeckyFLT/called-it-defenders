The '''2019 Uttlesford District Council election''' took place on 2 May 2019 to elect members of [[Uttlesford District Council]] in England.<ref name="UDC May 2019">{{cite web |title=Uttlesford District Council - Results - District elections 2 May 2019 |url=https://www.uttlesford.gov.uk/article/6234/Results-District-elections-2-May-2019 |website=www.uttlesford.gov.uk |access-date=3 May 2019}}</ref> This was on the same day as other [[2019 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].
