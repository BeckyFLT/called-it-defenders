The '''2002 Wandsworth London Borough Council election''' took place on 2 May 2002 to elect members of [[London Borough of Wandsworth|Wandsworth London Borough Council]] in London, England. The whole council was up for election with boundary changes since the [[1998 Wandsworth Council election|last election in 1998]] reducing the number of seats by 1.<ref name=hold>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/hi/english/static/vote2002/local_elections/156.stm | title = Wandsworth | access-date = 2009-09-13 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref> The [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] party stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name=wandsworth>{{cite news | title = Local Elections: Results | newspaper = The Guardian | page = 9 | date = 2002-05-03 }}</ref>

The election saw an experiment in leaving [[polling station]]s open for a longer period.<ref name=qa>{{cite news| url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/politics/1965523.stm | title = Q&A: The local elections | access-date = 2009-09-14 | date = 2002-05-03 | publisher = BBC News}}</ref> Along with neighbouring [[Westminster City Council]] polling stations were open from 7 am to 10 pm instead of the standard 8 am to 9 pm.<ref name=revives>{{cite news | first = Alexandra | last = Frean | title = Postal votes trial revives interest in local polls | newspaper = The Times| page = 4 | date = 2002-05-03 }}</ref>

== Background ==
As part of a major redistricting, Wandsworth council along with every other London Council created new districts, eliminated some, and expanded others. The results of this redistrictinng were as follows:<ref>The London Borough of Wandsworth (Electoral Arrangements) Order 1977</ref><ref>legislation.gov.uk - [http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/315/made The London Borough of Wandsworth (Electoral Changes) Order 2000]. Retrieved on 2 March 2015.</ref>

=== Wards Created ===

* [[Wandsworth Common (ward)|Wandsworth Common]] (3) - Created from parts of Earlsfield, Fairfield, Springfield and St John wards

=== Wards Eliminated ===

* [[Parkside (ward)|Parkside]] (2) - Absorbed by West Hill ward
* [[St John (ward)|St John]] (3) - Land divided among Fairfield, Latchmere and St Mary's Park wards as well as part of the new Wandsworth Common ward
* [[Springfield (Wandsworth ward)|Springfield]] (3) - Land divided between Earlsfield ward and the new Wandsworth Common ward

=== Wards Expanded ===

* [[Earlsfield (ward)|Earlsfield]] - increased from 2 to 3 seats
* [[Fairfield (Wandsworth ward)|Fairfield]] - increased from 2 seats to 3
* [[Queenstown (ward)|Queenstown]] - Increased from 2 seats to 3
* [[West Hill (ward)|West Hill]] - Increased from 2 seats to 3

=== Wards Renamed ===

* [[Southfield (ward)|Southfield]] (3) - renamed to Southfields ward
During the process of redistrictinng, the number of seats on Wandworth London Borough Council dropped from 61 to 60.


In the years between this election and the previous one there were 2 by-elections triggered by the resignation and deaths of their respective councillors, however neither of them resulted in the seats changing parties.<ref name="byelections">{{cite web |last1=Minors |first1=Michael |last2=Grenham |first2=Dennis |title=London Borough Council Elections - 2 May 2002 - By-elections |url=https://data.london.gov.uk/download/3d9f79d6-f2d8-4f42-910e-a9564382c8b8/7ab5d023-6683-44ee-8638-dd00593e2055/London-Borough-Council-Elections-2002.pdf |access-date=8 Oct 2025 |website=London Datastore |publisher=[[Greater London Authority]] |page=171}}</ref> In addition to this there was a single seat that became vacant without enough time to hold a by-election to fill it, which meant the composition of the council just before the election was as follows:<ref>{{cite web |last1=Minors |first1=Michael |last2=Grenham |first2=Dennis |title=London Borough Council Elections - 2 May 2002 - Changes 1998-02 |url=https://data.london.gov.uk/download/3d9f79d6-f2d8-4f42-910e-a9564382c8b8/7ab5d023-6683-44ee-8638-dd00593e2055/London-Borough-Council-Elections-2002.pdf |access-date=8 Oct 2025 |website=London Datastore |publisher=[[Greater London Authority]] |page=167}}</ref>
