The '''2022 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election''' took place on 5 May 2022 to elect members of [[Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council]] in England.<ref name="Newcastle-under-Lyme 1">{{Cite web|url=https://www.newcastle-staffs.gov.uk/elections-registration-1/local-government-elections-2022 |title=Local government elections 2022|access-date=4 May 2022 |work=Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council}}</ref> It was held on the same day as other [[2022 United Kingdom local elections|local elections]].

Elections in [[2018 Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council election|2018]] left the council in no overall control, but the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservatives]] were defending a majority on the council after taking control through defections in 2021.<ref name="Newcastle-under-Lyme 2">{{Cite news|url=https://www.stokesentinel.co.uk/news/stoke-on-trent-news/tories-take-overall-control-newcastle-6145008 |title=Tories take overall control of Newcastle Borough Council for first time in decades|access-date=4 May 2022 |work=Stoke Sentinel|date=2 November 2021 |last1=Corrigan |first1=Phil }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Your councillors by party |url=https://moderngov.newcastle-staffs.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=PARTY&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |website=Newcastle under Lyme Borough Council |access-date=28 December 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220330044840/https://moderngov.newcastle-staffs.gov.uk/mgMemberIndex.aspx?FN=PARTY&VW=LIST&PIC=0 |archive-date=30 March 2022}}</ref>

The election saw the Conservatives retain their majority.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Corrigan |first1=Phil |title=Newcastle Borough Council local elections live 2022: Tories win majority |url=https://www.stokesentinel.co.uk/news/stoke-on-trent-news/newcastle-borough-council-local-elections-7043261 |access-date=28 December 2023 |work=Stoke-on-Trent Live |date=6 May 2022}}</ref>

==Changes 2022–2026==

* Sue Beeston, elected as a Labour councillor, left the party in November 2023 to sit as an independent. She subsequently joined the Conservative Group.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Boothroyd |first1=David |title=Scottish Labour steels seat |url=https://www.localcouncils.co.uk/2023/11/scottish-labour-steels-seat/ |access-date=28 December 2023 |work=Local Councils |publisher=Thorncliffe |date=17 November 2023}}</ref>
* In late March 2026, Reform UK councillor Lynn Dean had the whip suspended after the anti-racism campaign group Hope Not Hate uncovered posts on an undisclosed social media account containing racist comments about Black people and Muslims; Dean said she had already decided to leave the party. She resigned as councillor for Knutton on 1 April 2026, weeks before she would have faced re-election, remaining a Staffordshire county councillor for May Bank and Wolstanton. The Knutton seat was left vacant until the 2026 borough election.<ref>{{cite news |last=Corrigan |first=Philip |title=Suspended Reform UK councillor resigns from Newcastle Borough Council |url=https://uk.news.yahoo.com/suspended-reform-uk-councillor-resigns-072456202.html |work=Yahoo News UK |date=1 April 2026 |access-date=3 September 2026}}</ref>
