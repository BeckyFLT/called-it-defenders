The '''2023 East Staffordshire Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2023 to elect all 37 members of [[East Staffordshire Borough Council]] in [[Staffordshire]], England. This was on the same day as other [[2023 United Kingdom local elections|local elections across England]]. New ward boundaries took effect at this election, reducing the number of councillors from 39 to 37.<ref>{{cite legislation UK|type=si|si=The East Staffordshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2021|year=2021|number=1220|access-date=27 December 2023}}</ref>

The council was under Conservative majority control prior to the election. Following the results, the Conservatives lost control of the council to Labour for the first time in 20 years.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2023-05-05 |title=Labour takes East Staffordshire from Tories for first time in 20 years |language=en-GB |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-stoke-staffordshire-65498246 |access-date=2023-05-07}}</ref> The Labour group leader, Mick Fitzpatrick, was appointed leader of the council at the subsequent annual council meeting on 26 May 2023.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Castle |first1=Richard |last2=Price |first2=Richard |title=The eight powerful Labour politicians who'll now be running East Staffordshire |url=https://www.staffordshire-live.co.uk/news/burton-news/eight-powerful-labour-politicians-wholl-8442245 |access-date=27 December 2023 |work=Staffordshire Live |date=16 May 2023}}</ref>
