{{Main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}
Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council were held on 2 May 2002. The whole council was up for election with boundary changes since the [[2000 Hyndburn Borough Council election|last election]] in 2000 reducing the number of seats by 12. The [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party gained overall control of the council from the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] party.

Boundary changes took place for the 2002 election. The number of councillors was reduced from 45 to 35 but the number of wards remained similar but not exactly the same.
