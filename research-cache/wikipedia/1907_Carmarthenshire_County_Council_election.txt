The seventh election to the [[Carmarthenshire County Council]] was held in March 1907.<ref name="CWR 15-3-07 CarmsRes">{{cite news|title=Carmarthenshire County Council Elections. Clerical Attack Ends in a Fiasco.|url=http://newspapers.library.wales/view/3644548/3644549/1|accessdate=20 September 2015|newspaper=Carmarthen Weekly Reporter (supplement)|date=15 March 1907|page=1}}</ref> It was preceded by the [[1904 Carmarthenshire County Council election|1904 election]] and followed by the [[1910 Carmarthenshire County Council election|1910 election]].

{{TOC right}}

==Overview of the result==

The Conservatives made a conscious attempt to contest far more seats than at recent elections in an election dominated by ongoing disputes over disestablishment. However, the Liberals retained a strong majority and many sitting members were returned unopposed.<ref name="CWR 8-3-07 CarmsNoms">{{cite news|title=County Council Nominations.|url=http://newspapers.library.wales/view/3644540/3644541/12|accessdate=19 September 2015|newspaper=Carmarthen Weekly Reporter (supplement)|date=8 March 1907|page=1}}</ref>

==Boundary changes==

There were minor boundary changes leading to the creation of two additional wards, namely Ammanford and Llandissilio. The new Ammanford ward, which comprised the [[Urban district (England and Wales)|urban district]] of [[Ammanford]] was created by the division of the Bettws Ward into two.<ref name="CWR 22-6-06 Carms wards">{{cite news|title=County Council Electoral Divisions.|url=http://newspapers.library.wales/view/3584222/3584225/21|accessdate=13 October 2015|newspaper=Carmarthen Weekly Reporter|date=22 June 1906|page=3}}</ref> Significant population growth had taken place in the eastern part of the county as a result of the growth of the [[anthracite]] coal trade but demands for further additional wards to be formed in the Llanelli district were rejected. The second new ward was at Llandissilio in the western part of the county, on the Pembrokeshire border. It was formed out of portions of the existing [[Whitland]] and [[Llanboidy]] wards.<ref name="CWR 22-6-06 Carms wards" /> There was less unanimity in favour of the new Llandissilio ward than was the case at Ammanford, and there were suggestions that an additional rural ward was being created to counterbalance the new industrial ward at Ammanford. A proposal for a third new ward, at [[Newchurch, Carmarthenshire|Newchurch]] on the outskirts of [[Carmarthen]] was rejected in due course.

As a result of these boundary changes, the Council included 53 elected members as opposed to 51 previously. The number of aldermen remained at 17.

==Candidates==

Eighteen wards were contested, compared with only six in 1904.

Four of those elected at the first election in 1889, and who had served continuously since then, sought re-election. Earl Cawdor, Sir James Drummond, C.E. Morris and D.C. Parry (Llanelli) were all returned unopposed.<ref name="CWR 8-3-07 CarmsNoms" />
  
==Outcome==

While a small number of wards were hotly contested and the Conservatives made a small number of gains, their attempt to change the political composition of the council was unsuccessful.

In Llanarthney, a determined attempt was made to unseat Henry Jones-Davies, with allegations that the Conservative David Farr Davies, a colliery manager, was supported by some labour figures.<ref name="CWR 15-3-07 CarmsRes" />

==Election of aldermen==

In addition to the 51 councillors the council consisted of 17 county aldermen. Aldermen were elected by the council, and served a six-year term. Following the elections the following eight aldermen were elected (with the number of votes in each case).
