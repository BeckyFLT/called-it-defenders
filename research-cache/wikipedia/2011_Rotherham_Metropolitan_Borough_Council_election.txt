The '''2011 Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council election''' was held on Thursday 5 May 2011 to elect 21 members to the [[Rotherham Metropolitan Borough Council]], the same day as other [[2011 United Kingdom local elections|local elections in the United Kingdom]]. It elected one-third of the council's 63 members to a four-year term. [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] held control of the council after the election.<ref name=HoC>{{citeweb|title=Local elections 2011|url=https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/RP11-43/RP11-43.pdf|date=24 May 2011|last=Tetteh|first=Edmund|publisher=The House of Commons Library|accessdate = 16 July 2024}}</ref>
