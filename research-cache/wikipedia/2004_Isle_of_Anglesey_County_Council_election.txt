The '''2004 Isle of Anglesey County Council election''' took place on Thursday 10 June 2004 to elect members of the [[Isle of Anglesey County Council]] in [[Wales]]. This was the same day as other [[2004 United Kingdom local elections|United Kingdom local elections]]. The next full council election was on [[2008 Isle of Anglesey County Council election|1 May 2008]].

==Election result==
Forty county councillors were elected from forty [[Ward (electoral subdivision)|electoral wards]]. Fourteen seats had no election because there were no opposing candidates.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-wales-north-west-wales-22388459 |title=Anglesey election: Plaid to seek Labour council deal |work=BBC News |date=3 May 2013 |accessdate=23 March 2019}}</ref>

Prior to the election the county council had been governed for 15 months by a coalition led by [[Plaid Cymru]]'s Bob Parry. After the 2004 election Plaid Cymru became the official opposition with 8 councillors. There were 28 councillors not affiliated to any political party, comprising ten declared [[Independent politician|Independent]]s and eighteen who gave no description.<ref name=nwlwhocontrols>{{cite news|url=https://www.dailypost.co.uk/news/north-wales-news/labour-hail-return-fold-2928035 |title=Labour hail a return to the fold - Who controls the councils? |date=15 June 2004 |work=North Wales Live |access-date=23 March 2019}}</ref>

==Events prior to 2008 election==
The Plaid Cymru councillor for the [[Llanfihangel Ysgeifiog]] ward, Hughie Noel Thomas, resigned from the party group in October 2006. He was jailed for nine months on 6 November 2006 (and disqualified from being a county councillor) for falsifying [[Post Office]] records. He had been sub-postmaster in the village of [[Gaerwen]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dailypost.co.uk/news/north-wales-news/postmaster-jailed-48k-false-accounting-2885860 |title=Postmaster jailed for £48k false accounting |date=7 November 2006 |work=North Wales Live |access-date=23 March 2019}}</ref> Independent councillor Eric Jones was elected at a by-election in 2007.
