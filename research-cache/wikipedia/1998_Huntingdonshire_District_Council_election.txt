The '''1998 Huntingdonshire District Council election''' took place on 7 May 1998 to elect members of [[Huntingdonshire]] [[Non-metropolitan district|District]] Council in [[Cambridgeshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election and the [[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="local">{{cite news|title=Local Elections results|date=9 May 1998|work=[[The Times]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]|page=46}}</ref>

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 34
*[[Liberal Democrats (UK)|Liberal Democrats]] 14
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 3
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 2<ref name="government">{{cite news|title=Local government election results|date=8 May 1998|work=[[The Independent]]|publisher=[[NewsBank]]}}</ref>
