The '''2006 Barnsley Metropolitan Borough Council election''' took place on 4 May 2006 to elect members of [[Metropolitan Borough of Barnsley|Barnsley]] [[Metropolitan Borough]] Council in [[South Yorkshire]], [[England]]. One third of the council was up for election, with an extra vacancy in Penistone East caused by a resignation,<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.thestar.co.uk/whats-on/out-and-about/sigh-of-relief-for-ruling-party-as-the-unthinkable-is-avoided-1-268409|title=Sigh of relief for ruling party as the 'unthinkable' is avoided|publisher=thestar.co.uk|access-date=10 August 2017}}</ref> and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour Party]] stayed in overall control of the council.<ref name="hold">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/shared/bsp/hi/vote2006/locals/html/cc.stm|title=Local elections: Barnsley|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=18 June 2011}}</ref><ref name="results2">{{cite web| url = http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Barnsley-1973-2012.pdf | title = Elections Centre: Barnsley 1973-2012 | publisher = electionscentre.co.uk/plymouth.ac.uk |access-date = 2017-07-25 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20170725171829/http://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Barnsley-1973-2012.pdf | archive-date = 2017-07-25}}</ref>

==Background==
The Barnsley Independent Group, which had been formed by [[Independent (politician)|independents]] on the council, were contesting an election for the first time.<ref name="rise">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/politics/4941644.stm|title=The rise of the Independents|last=Hakala|first=Anna|date=25 April 2006|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=18 June 2011}}</ref> They put up candidates in 17 of the 21 [[Wards of the United Kingdom|wards]] which had seats up for election and 7 of their [[councillor]]s were defending seats.<ref name=rise/>

==Election results==
The results saw Labour stay in control of the council, making a net gain of 1 to hold 34 seats.<ref name="expit">{{cite news|url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/uk_news/england/south_yorkshire/4974914.stm|title=Labour holds firm in ex-pit towns|date=5 May 2006|publisher=[[BBC News Online]]|access-date=18 June 2011}}</ref>
