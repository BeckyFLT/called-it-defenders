{{main|Hyndburn Borough Council elections}}

Elections to [[Hyndburn]] Borough Council were held on 6 May 1999.  One third of the council was up for election and the [[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] party lost overall control of the council to [[no overall control]].

After the election, the composition of the council was:
*[[Labour Party (UK)|Labour]] 23
*[[Conservative Party (UK)|Conservative]] 23
*[[Independent (politician)|Independent]] 1
